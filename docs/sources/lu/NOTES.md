# Lucerne (LU) sources: notes for natural persons, tax years 2025 and 2026

Verified 2026-10-13. Each fact is followed by its official source. The documents are the source of truth; never copy a number from this file.

## Filing channels (tax year 2025)

- eSteuern.LU is the online channel for tax year 2025: fully web based, no installation, also on tablet or smartphone. It went online on 26 January 2026 and replaces the downloadable software (https://news.lu.ch/html_mail.jsp?id=0&email=news.lu.ch&mailref=000l6dq0000ti000000000000cgcqzjb).
- Login: you create your own eSteuern account with an e-mail address and a mobile phone number, once. You then open the return with the PersID and the Zugangscode eFiling printed on the return you received (https://steuern.lu.ch/-/media/Steuern/Dokumente/Publikationen/2026/Merkblatt_2025_eSteuern.pdf). No AGOV or other state login is mentioned.
- Electronic filing: all attachments (salary certificates, 3a certificates and so on) are uploaded; no signature is needed because the access code identifies you (https://steuern.lu.ch/-/media/Steuern/Dokumente/Steuerformulare/2025/Wegleitung930109LU2025HADEFneu.pdf).
- After clicking Steuererklärung einreichen you have 72 hours to correct and resubmit; only then does the tax office see the return. You can also switch to printing and filing on paper (https://steuern.lu.ch/-/media/Steuern/Dokumente/Publikationen/2026/Merkblatt_2025_eSteuern.pdf).
- Paper: print (or fill in by hand), sign, and send with all attachments to Steuererklärungen Kanton Luzern, c/o Scan-Center, Postfach, 8010 Zürich, with the pre-printed form Steuererklärung Natürliche Personen (https://steuern.lu.ch/-/media/Steuern/Dokumente/Steuerformulare/2025/Wegleitung930109LU2025HADEFneu.pdf).
- Scanning rules for paper: A4 loose sheets, no staples or clips, readable copies, no plastic sleeves; general mail and extension requests go to the Gemeindesteueramt, not to the Scan-Center (https://steuern.lu.ch/-/media/Steuern/Dokumente/Publikationen/2026/Merkblatt_2025_A_Information_zur_Steuererklrung.pdf).
- Paper is still allowed after the switch to eSteuern.LU (https://news.lu.ch/html_mail.jsp?id=0&email=news.lu.ch&mailref=000l6dq0000ti000000000000cgcqzjb).
- Returns for 2024 can now only be filed on paper; the 2024 desktop software stays available as a backup for Windows, macOS and Linux (https://steuern.lu.ch/steuererklaerung/steuererklaerung_natp/Installation).
- For employees and non-employed persons the assessing authority is the commune (Gemeindesteueramt); self-employed persons deal with the Dienststelle Steuern (https://steuern.lu.ch/natuerlichepersonen/Wissenswertes_NP/unselbstaendig_erwerbende).
- Technical support for eSteuern.LU runs through lu-support.etax.ch and a hotline; it answers no tax questions, which go to the Gemeindesteueramt (https://steuern.lu.ch/steuererklaerung/helpdesk_hotline).
- A public training platform (schulen-lu.etax.ch) with published test PersIDs and access codes lets anyone try eSteuern.LU without transmitting data (https://steuern.lu.ch/steuererklaerung/Testsystem_esteuernlu).
- Tax mail can be received in the ePost app of Swiss Post (https://steuern.lu.ch/steuererklaerung/ePost).
- Software family: the canton calls it eSteuern.LU and a standard solution already used in many cantons, without naming a vendor (https://news.lu.ch/html_mail.jsp?id=0&email=news.lu.ch&mailref=000l6dq0000ti000000000000cgcqzjb). The official pages link etax.ch subdomains for training and support, the web app identifies itself as eTax LU, and the 2024 backup software downloads from webupdate.ringler.ch. This points to the Ringler eTax family; that is an inference from domains, not a statement by the canton (https://steuern.lu.ch/steuererklaerung/steuererklaerung_natp/Installation).

## Third-party software

- PC-made forms from private providers are accepted if they are identical to the official forms, carry the PersID, are dated and signed, and come with the data sheet defined by the Dienststelle Steuern (Barcode-Blatt). Electronic returns from private providers that support eFiling are also accepted (https://steuern.lu.ch/-/media/Steuern/Dokumente/Steuerformulare/2025/Wegleitung930109LU2025HADEFneu.pdf).
- Steuerbuch rule: providers must obtain the form templates from the Dienststelle Steuern, keep all their specifications, may not design their own forms, and must generate the barcodes defined by the Dienststelle. Returns without the barcode sheet can be rejected (https://steuerbuch.lu.ch/-/media/Steuerbuch/Dokumente/Archiv/01_01_2026/LUStB_Band2_2026_01_01.pdf, § 145 Nr. 1).
- No list of approved providers, no approval procedure and no eFiling interface specification for providers was found on steuern.lu.ch or steuerbuch.lu.ch (https://steuern.lu.ch/steuererklaerung).

## Deadlines

- The filing deadline is the one printed on the return; no fixed calendar date is published (https://steuern.lu.ch/-/media/Steuern/Dokumente/Publikationen/2026/Merkblatt_2025_A_Information_zur_Steuererklrung.pdf).
- Steuerbuch principle: a return is due within 30 days of delivery; extensions follow the rules in the same chapter (https://steuerbuch.lu.ch/-/media/Steuerbuch/Dokumente/Archiv/01_01_2026/LUStB_Band2_2026_01_01.pdf, § 145 Nr. 2).
- Extension online (e-Fristerstreckung) before the deadline. Latest dates you can set yourself for the 2025 return: 31 August 2026 for employees and non-employed persons; 30 November 2026 for self-employed persons, taxpayers with a professional representative and persons with limited tax liability (https://steuern.lu.ch/-/media/Steuern/Dokumente/Steuerformulare/2025/Wegleitung930109LU2025HADEFneu.pdf).
- Self-employed persons, professionally represented taxpayers and persons with limited tax liability have a general deadline of 31 August 2026 without any request (same guide).
- Longer extensions, or extensions after the second reminder, cannot be requested online: send a reasoned request by e-mail or letter to the Gemeindesteueramt (self-employed: frist.dst@lu.ch). They are granted only with great restraint (same guide).
- The online extension asks for the PersID and the AHV number (https://steuern.lu.ch/-/media/Steuern/Dokumente/Publikationen/2025/930498_Merkblatt_2025_Nachtrgliche_ordentliche_Veranlagung_NOV.pdf).
- No fee for an extension is stated. The first reminder is free; the second reminder carries a fee (https://steuerbuch.lu.ch/-/media/Steuerbuch/Dokumente/Archiv/01_01_2026/LUStB_Band2_2026_01_01.pdf, § 145 Nr. 3).
- For the 2026 return the same maxima move to 31 August 2027 and 30 November 2027 (https://steuern.lu.ch/-/media/Steuern/Dokumente/Steuerformulare/2026/Wegleitung930109LU2026VADEFneu.pdf).
- Payment: the provisional bill (Akontorechnung) 2026 is sent in June 2026 and is due by 31 December 2026 (https://steuern.lu.ch/-/media/Steuern/Dokumente/Publikationen/2026/Merkblatt_2025_A_Information_zur_Steuererklrung.pdf).

## Source tax (Quellensteuer, NOV: nachträgliche ordentliche Veranlagung, and rectification)

- NOV request: online form, from December of the tax year until 31 March of the following year. The commune then sends the return; from then on returns come every year until the source tax liability ends (https://steuern.lu.ch/natuerlichepersonen/Steuerarten/quellensteuer/Steuererklaerung_NOV).
- The 31 March deadline is a legal deadline and cannot be extended (https://steuern.lu.ch/natuerlichepersonen/Steuerarten/quellensteuer/Haeufig_gestellte_Fragen).
- A filed request cannot be withdrawn. If you leave Switzerland, the request period ends at deregistration. Married couples are assessed together even if only one spouse meets the conditions (https://steuern.lu.ch/-/media/Steuern/Dokumente/Publikationen/2025/930498_Merkblatt_2025_Nachtrgliche_ordentliche_Veranlagung_NOV.pdf).
- Mandatory NOV for residents above the income, other-income or wealth thresholds listed in the Merkblatt; whoever meets them must ask for the return form by 31 March of the following year (same Merkblatt).
- Non-residents can request NOV only if quasi-resident; they must ask again each year and give a Swiss representative or postal address (same Merkblatt).
- Source tax already deducted is credited without interest; only amounts actually remitted by the employer count (same Merkblatt).
- Rectification (Neuberechnung) of the deduction for a wrong gross salary, wrong rate-determining income or wrong tariff: by 31 March of the year after the payment, by e-mail to dst.qs@lu.ch or by post, with evidence (https://steuern.lu.ch/natuerlichepersonen/Steuerarten/quellensteuer/Neuberechnung).
- Tariffs: A and B, C, H with (Y) and without (N) church tax, G, pension lump sums, and text files for payroll software, for 2025 and 2026 (https://steuern.lu.ch/natuerlichepersonen/Steuerarten/quellensteuer/quellensteuer_tarife).
- The ordinary source tax tariffs were adjusted on 1 January 2026; the new France telework agreement applies from the same date (https://steuern.lu.ch/-/media/Steuern/Dokumente/Publikationen/2026/QS_Tarifschreiben_2026.pdf).

## e-tax statements (eCH-0196) and other imports

- eSteuern.LU imports the values of bank e-tax statements (eSteuerauszüge) automatically. The page does not name the eCH-0196 standard (https://steuern.lu.ch/-/media/Steuern/Dokumente/Publikationen/2026/Merkblatt_2025_eSteuern.pdf).
- Previous-year data: for 2025 you import the 2024 file (extension lunp2024); from the next period the previous-year data is taken over automatically (https://news.lu.ch/html_mail.jsp?id=0&email=news.lu.ch&mailref=000l6dq0000ti000000000000cgcqzjb).
- Master data are filled in automatically after login (same press release).
- Receipts can be photographed with the SNAP.SHARE app and added to the online return (https://steuern.lu.ch/-/media/Steuern/Dokumente/Publikationen/2026/Merkblatt_2025_eSteuern.pdf).
- Barcode: a printed PC return must include the signed barcode sheet (Barcode-Blatt) defined by the Dienststelle Steuern (https://steuern.lu.ch/-/media/Steuern/Dokumente/Steuerformulare/2025/Wegleitung930109LU2025HADEFneu.pdf).

## New for 2025 and 2026

- 2025: deductions raised and tariffs adjusted for cold progression; the StG revision 2025 raised and changed several deductions and created a new degressive social deduction; life annuities are taxed on their income share instead of a flat share (https://steuern.lu.ch/-/media/Steuern/Dokumente/Publikationen/2026/Merkblatt_2025_A_Information_zur_Steuererklrung.pdf).
- 2025: the StG revision of 18 March 2024 entered into force on 1 January 2025 (https://srl.lu.ch/api/de/change_documents/file_dictionaries/2905/pdf_file).
- 2025: the earlier home office rules still apply; withholding tax credits now appear on the 2025 final bill (https://steuern.lu.ch/-/media/Steuern/Dokumente/Publikationen/2026/Merkblatt_2025_A_Information_zur_Steuererklrung.pdf).
- 2025: eSteuern.LU replaces the downloadable software; limited taxpayers can now also file online (same Merkblatt and https://news.lu.ch/html_mail.jsp?id=0&email=news.lu.ch&mailref=000l6dq0000ti000000000000cgcqzjb).
- 2026: tariffs and deductions adjusted for cold progression by the government decision of 16 September 2025 (https://srl.lu.ch/api/de/change_documents/file_dictionaries/3040/pdf_file).
- 2026: the state tax units changed compared with 2025 (https://steuern.lu.ch/publikationen/nav_einheiten_tarife).
- 2026: Steuerbuch updates as of 1 January 2026, among them commuting, other professional costs, insurance premiums, child and childcare deductions (https://steuerbuch.lu.ch/-/media/Steuerbuch/Dokumente/Archiv/01_01_2026/LUStB_Band1_2026_01_01.pdf).
- 2026: new mileage rate for business trips in expense regulations from 1 January 2026 (https://steuern.lu.ch/natuerlichepersonen/lohnausweis).
- 2027: the StG amendment of 11 May 2026 (life annuities on income share in § 29 and § 40) enters into force on 1 January 2027 (https://srl.lu.ch/api/de/change_documents/file_dictionaries/3112/pdf_file).
- 2027: the cold progression adjustment for 2027 was announced on 17 September 2026 and the 2027 tariff tables are already online (https://steuern.lu.ch/publikationen/nav_einheiten_tarife/b_berechnungstabellen_steuertarife).
- The Dienststelle explains the coming abolition of the imputed rental value after the federal vote of 28 September 2025 in newsletter 3/2026 (https://news.lu.ch/html_mail.jsp?id=0&email=news.lu.ch&mailref=000l6600000760000000000000pnu2ee).

## Tariffs, multipliers and deductions (pointers to documents)

- Mechanics: the income tariff in § 57 StG gives the tax per unit (Steuer je Einheit). Abs. 1 is the scale for single persons, Abs. 2 a separate family scale for married couples and for unmarried persons who live with and mainly support children or dependants; there is no splitting divisor. The total cantonal, communal and church tax is the tax per unit times the sum of the state, commune and parish units (https://steuern.lu.ch/-/media/Steuern/Dokumente/Steuerformulare/2025/Wegleitung930109LU2025HADEFneu.pdf, pages 8 and 58 to 59).
- Wealth tax: a per mille rate per unit (§ 60 StG), multiplied by the same units, with a worked example (https://steuern.lu.ch/publikationen/nav_einheiten_tarife/b_berechnungstabellen_steuertarife).
- A maximum total burden for income and wealth applies (§ 62 StG) (https://srl.lu.ch/api/de/versions/4667/pdf_file).
- Tariff brackets 2025 and 2026: https://steuern.lu.ch/-/media/Steuern/Dokumente/Publikationen/2024/Tariftabellen_und_Abzge_fr_Homepage_2025.pdf and https://steuern.lu.ch/-/media/Steuern/Dokumente/Publikationen/2025/Tariftabellen_und_Abzge_2026.pdf. Full tables per 100 francs: https://steuern.lu.ch/-/media/Steuern/Dokumente/Publikationen/2024/Tabelle_Einkommenstarife_2025.pdf and https://steuern.lu.ch/-/media/Steuern/Dokumente/Publikationen/2025/Tabelle_Einkommenstarife_2026.pdf.
- Units (Steuereinheiten) for the state, every commune and the Roman Catholic, Reformed and Christ Catholic parishes, with totals: 2025 at https://steuern.lu.ch/-/media/Steuern/Dokumente/Publikationen/2025/Steuereinheiten_2025_NP.pdf and 2026 at https://steuern.lu.ch/-/media/Steuern/Dokumente/Publikationen/2026/Steuereinheiten_2026_NP.pdf. Church tax is in the same table.
- Which scale applies to which family form: Steuerbuch Band 1, § 42 Nr. 3 and § 57 Nr. 1 (https://steuerbuch.lu.ch/-/media/Steuerbuch/Dokumente/Archiv/01012025/LUStB_Band1_2025_01_01.pdf).
- Deductions at a glance by form Ziffer, canton and federal side by side: https://steuern.lu.ch/-/media/Steuern/Dokumente/Publikationen/2025/Abzge_auf_einen_Blick_2025.pdf and https://steuern.lu.ch/-/media/Steuern/Dokumente/Publikationen/2026/Abzge_auf_einen_Blick_2026.pdf.
- Detailed rules in Steuerbuch Band 1: commuting § 33 Nr. 1, meals § 33 Nr. 2, other professional costs § 33 Nr. 3, pillar 3a § 40 Nr. 5, insurance premiums § 40 Nr. 6, medical costs § 40 Nr. 8, donations § 40 Nr. 9, training § 40 Nr. 11, second earner § 40 Nr. 12, children § 42 Nr. 2, childcare § 42 Nr. 4, degressive social deduction § 42 Nr. 6 (2025: https://steuerbuch.lu.ch/-/media/Steuerbuch/Dokumente/Archiv/01012025/LUStB_Band1_2025_01_01.pdf, 2026: https://steuerbuch.lu.ch/-/media/Steuerbuch/Dokumente/Archiv/01_01_2026/LUStB_Band1_2026_01_01.pdf).
- Professional costs follow the federal professional expense ordinance by analogy (StV § 11) (https://srl.lu.ch/api/de/versions/4586/pdf_file).
- Medical cost flat rates: directive of the finance director of 7 September 2005, still linked (https://steuern.lu.ch/-/media/Steuern/Dokumente/Publikationen/krankheitskosten_finanzdirektor_2005.pdf).
- Federal tax: the Ergänzungsblatt converts the cantonal net income to the federal one (https://steuern.lu.ch/-/media/Steuern/Dokumente/Steuerformulare/2025/Ergaenzungsblatt_DB_LU_2025_HA_rechenbar.pdf).
- Rental value rates are in the section Mietwertansätze 2025 of the 2025 guide (page 56 in its table of contents) (https://steuern.lu.ch/-/media/Steuern/Dokumente/Steuerformulare/2025/Wegleitung930109LU2025HADEFneu.pdf).

- The support site returns HTTP 403 with a bot challenge to the fetcher (https://lu-support.etax.ch/hc/de-ch).
- Source tax tables C, G, H and pension lump sums for 2025 and 2026 are listed but not added one by one (https://steuern.lu.ch/natuerlichepersonen/Steuerarten/quellensteuer/quellensteuer_tarife).
- No French or English guide found (https://steuern.lu.ch/publikationen/nav_wegleitungen).
- Conflict, childcare cap: StG § 40 Abs. 1l and § 42 Abs. 1b (versions in force 2025 and 2026, from the StG revision of 18 March 2024) state a higher maximum for third-party childcare than Abzüge auf einen Blick 2025 and 2026, Tariftabellen und Abzüge 2025 and 2026, and the 2025 guide (Ziffer 360). Ask before coding (https://srl.lu.ch/api/de/versions/4405/pdf_file and https://steuern.lu.ch/-/media/Steuern/Dokumente/Publikationen/2025/Abzge_auf_einen_Blick_2025.pdf). Read with the Steuerbuch, § 42 Abs. 1b counts its maximum with the own-care amount included, which agrees with the overviews; only § 40 Abs. 1l reads otherwise: see "Open points for the rules" below.
- Conflict, life annuities: § 29 Abs. 3 StG in the versions in force until 30 September 2026 still has the old flat share, while the 2025 Merkblatt and the 2025 guide (Ziffer 134 and 135) apply the income share from 2025. The amendment of 11 May 2026 writes the income share into the StG from 1 January 2027, and the consolidated version Stand 1 October 2026 already shows the new text (https://srl.lu.ch/api/de/versions/4903/pdf_file and https://srl.lu.ch/api/de/change_documents/file_dictionaries/3112/pdf_file).
- Conflict, eFiling rules: Steuerbuch § 145 Nr. 1a (validity date 1 January 2024) still requires the canton's downloadable software for eFiling and gives a longer correction window with a limit on transmissions, while the 2025 eSteuern Merkblatt gives 72 hours and the 2025 guide accepts eFiling from private providers. The Steuerbuch chapter predates eSteuern.LU (https://steuerbuch.lu.ch/-/media/Steuerbuch/Dokumente/Archiv/01_01_2026/LUStB_Band2_2026_01_01.pdf).

## Open points for the rules (packages/rules-lu, 2026-10-14)

Found while building packages/rules-lu from the documents of this folder (ids in parentheses; "p." is the page of the PDF). The engine follows the documents as stated here and reports, in each result, what it assumed (`assumptions`) and the rules whose detail the documents leave open (`unverifiedRules`); where two documents give different amounts, it computes no tax.

- Childcare cap (the conflict above), read again: StG § 42 Abs. 1b raises the own-care amount "auf höchstens" the maximum "für die ungedeckten Kosten der Drittbetreuung" (lu-2025-law-steuergesetz p. 24, lu-2026-law-steuergesetz p. 24); the Steuerbuch's table gives that maximum as "Maximaler Fremdbetreuungsabzug (inkl. Eigenbetreuungsabzug)" (lu-2025-deductions-steuerbuch-band1 p. 190, lu-2026-deductions-steuerbuch-band1 p. 195); the guide gives the childcare part "zusätzlich zum Eigenbetreuungsabzug" (lu-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 34), and the canton's tables give that part alone (lu-2025-tariff-tariftabellen-abzuege p. 2, lu-2026-tariff-tariftabellen-abzuege p. 2). These agree: own care plus childcare at most the § 42 amount. StV § 14b gives parents taxed apart half each, own care included (lu-2025-law-steuerverordnung p. 6, lu-2026-law-steuerverordnung p. 6). Only § 40 Abs. 1l (p. 23), a general deduction of "die nachgewiesenen Kosten bis" the same amount, reads as if childcare alone could reach it; the StG's own transitional table (p. 104) still names § 40 Abs. 1l for childcare. rules-lu follows § 42 with the Steuerbuch, the guide and the tables, and says so in `unverifiedRules` whenever a child's childcare reaches the cap. A human should confirm.
- Rounding of the taxes: not stated. The guide multiplies the tax per unit by the units and rounds ties both ways: 2,201.50 x 3.75 = 8,255.625 printed 8,255.65 (lu-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 58), 55.50 x 3.75 = 208.125 printed 208.10 (p. 60), 2,198.50 x 3.65 = 8,024.525 printed 8,024.50 (lu-2026-guide-wegleitung-unterjaehrig p. 58). rules-lu computes the state, communal and church tax each to the centime. Shares of a deduction (3 %, 5 %, 14 %, 20 %, 0.3 %) are taken to the nearest franc: the guide's sample takes 0.3 % of 449,642 as 1,349 (p. 14, Wertschriftenverzeichnis Ziffer 6) and 3 % of 87,410 as 2,622 (p. 16). Both said in `unverifiedRules`.
- Misprints in the 2025 guide's tariff tables (the law and the Berechnungstabelle agree with each other on all 20,902 rows of each year, scripts/lu-tariff.mjs --check): p. 58, the single tariff's rows 10,500, 11,000, 11,500 and 12,000 print the tax of 10,200, 10,600, 11,100 and 11,600 (1.50, 3.50, 6.00, 8.50 instead of 3.00, 5.50, 8.00, 10.50); p. 59, the row 1,424,400 prints 79,766.50 where 5.6 % of the income is 79,766.40 (lu-2025-tariff-berechnungstabelle-einkommen p. 273: 79,766.40); p. 59's list of the family rates prints 1,0 / 2,0 / 3,0 / 4,0 % for the third to sixth steps where § 57 Abs. 2 has 1,50 / 2,50 / 3,00 / 3,50 % (the page's own table follows the law). rules-lu uses the law; test/tariffs.test.ts pins each row.
- At the top of the family tariff the steps of § 57 Abs. 2 give 79,760.90 per unit at 1,424,300 (2025), and "5,6 Prozent des Einkommens" above it gives 79,766.40 at 1,424,400, while 5.6 % of 1,424,300 would be 79,760.80. Not a conflict between documents (the Berechnungstabelle prints the same), recorded because it is easy to "fix" by mistake. The single tariff joins exactly (119,301.00 at 2,093,000).
- The 2025 guide's sample return still uses a few 2024 amounts: form V (p. 16) 5,100 for spouses with pension contributions (2025: 5,200, StG § 40 Abs. 1g, lu-2025-tariff-tariftabellen-abzuege p. 2, the guide's own p. 54); page 3 (p. 17) 4,900 for both spouses earning (5,000), 7,000 per child (8,000) and 1,100 own care (2,000), while its form prints the 2025 amounts next to the lines. Its tariff examples use 3.75 units "z.B. Gemeinde Beromünster, römisch katholisch" and "Rothenburg, römisch katholisch" (p. 58 to 60), while the 2025 table of units gives 3.70 and 3.45 (lu-2025-communal-steuereinheiten p. 1 and 2); the 2026 guide's 3.65 for Beromünster against 3.60 (lu-2026-communal-steuereinheiten p. 1). Illustrations only; rules-lu tests the sample's lines computed with the 2025 rules (net salaries, form B, tax-free wealth).
- The flat costs of securities (0.3 % up to CHF 3 million, 0.1 % above, at most their income): the guide excludes loans and unlisted private shares (lu-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 40), but its sample applies the flat to a total that includes a loan (DA, 50,000; p. 14). The household input does not tell loans apart: rules-lu applies the flat to every account and security given and says so in `assumptions`.
- Base of the medical costs and donations: the law takes 5 % and 20 % of the income less the deductions "nach den §§ 33–40 Absatz 1g" (StG § 40 Abs. 1h and 1i, p. 22), so before professional training (1m), while the form and the guide take them from the Nettoeinkommen, Ziffer 310, after training (lu-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 17 and 32). rules-lu computes both and no tax when they give different deductions.
- The higher insurance amount: the law gives it to taxpayers "ohne Beiträge gemäss Absatz 1d und 1e" (§ 40 Abs. 1g; 1d also names AHV/IV contributions), the canton's practice documents to those without contributions to a pension fund or pillar 3a, for spouses both (lu-2025-deductions-steuerbuch-band1 p. 166, lu-2026-deductions-steuerbuch-band1 p. 171; lu-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 54; lu-2025-tariff-tariftabellen-abzuege p. 2). rules-lu follows the practice documents.
- Life annuities (the conflict above): rules-lu does not cover Ziffer 134/135. A pension given as "other" is counted in full as übrige Renten (136/137), and the result says that a Leibrente is not covered.
- Church tax: only members of a recognised church pay it (StG § 240 Abs. 1); "Gehören die Mitglieder einer Familie verschiedenen Konfessionen an", the claim is shared in proportion of each confession's members to all members (§ 240 Abs. 2, p. 96). No document gives a worked example or says who the members are; the return asks the confession of each taxpayer and of each child (lu-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 13 and 20). rules-lu counts the taxpayers and the children counted on the return, needs each one's confession, applies each church's units to its share, and says so in `unverifiedRules` when the members differ. The Christ Catholic parish has 0.31 units in every commune (the tables' heading).
- Personalsteuer: CHF 50 from whoever is taxable at the end of the year, from the year they turn 18, one for spouses, none for people known to be in need (StG § 230 to 232, p. 94). rules-lu adds it once per return and assumes no exemption (said in `assumptions`).
- Höchstbelastung (StG § 62, p. 33): the documents don't say how the cut is shared between the state, the commune and the parish, or rounded; rules-lu shows one cut for income and one for wealth, against the taxable income and wealth in whole CHF 100 and CHF 1,000. When the church tax was not asked and the cut could depend on it, no tax.
- Flat professional costs: the base includes replacement income (daily allowances, unemployment benefits), and the deduction is at most that income (lu-2025-deductions-steuerbuch-band1 p. 102, § 33 Nr. 3 Ziff. 1); for part of a year the flat amounts are cut by whole months (same page; lu-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 28). The household input gives no months: rules-lu counts a full year and says so.
- Childcare of a married couple where one spouse has no paid work: the documents say only that the costs must be caused by "Erwerbstätigkeit oder Ausbildung der steuerpflichtigen Person" (§ 42 Abs. 1b; Steuerbuch § 42 Nr. 4 Ziff. 1.2). rules-lu counts childcare for a couple only when both earn (said in `notCounted`); costs due to an inability to work (§ 42 Abs. 1c, no limit) are not covered.
- Shared custody: without child support, half the child deduction, half the insurance amount per child and half the tax-free wealth per child (§ 42 Abs. 2), own care and childcare by StV § 14b; with child support received, the child counts in full (the result notes that a child living with each parent in turn gives each half of the care amounts). The family tariff without child support goes to the parent who cares for the children longer or, at equal time, earns more (lu-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 20): rules-lu asks it (cantonal.familyTariff) and computes no tax without the answer.
- Pillar 3a catch-up payments (from 2026 in federal law): no Lucerne document of the library mentions them; rules-lu computes no tax when one is given.
- Communes: both tables of units list 79 communes (lu-2025-communal-steuereinheiten, lu-2026-communal-steuereinheiten); Honau is in neither, while the 2025 guide's rental value groups still list it (lu-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 56). The tables give no BFS numbers; rules-lu finds communes by name.
- The return has no federal column; the authority derives the federal tax (lu-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 54; the optional Ergänzungsblatt, lu-2025-form-ergaenzungsblatt-dbst). Since the wiring, rules-lu's result carries the federal tax as the Ergänzungsblatt derives it (below, "Wiring Lucerne in the app").
- 2026: the regular guide is not published; rules-lu's 2026 amounts cite the 2026 law, the canton's 2026 tables, the Steuerbuch of 1 January 2026 and the 2026 guide for a liability ending during the year (lu-2026-guide-wegleitung-unterjaehrig, same pages as the 2025 guide, 2026 amounts).
- Not covered by rules-lu: property (Ziffer 190, 420; rental values), self-employment (110 to 119, 430 to 435), capital benefits taxed apart (§ 58), lottery winnings and their stakes (WV Ziffer 7), qualified participations (WV Ziffer 10), inheritances and simple partnerships (164, 414), life annuities (134/135) and permanent charges (256), weekly stays and shift work (form B 216, 228 to 231), the support deduction (370), AHV contributions paid directly (284 to 286), part-year liability, source tax.

## Wiring Lucerne in the app (2026-10-14)

Found while giving rules-lu its federal tax and wiring Lucerne as a preview. Each cites the document id and page; the numbers live in the documents.

- Federal tax: the return has one column for all taxes. The guide says the federal income and deductions are the cantonal ones "soweit in der nachstehenden Übersicht nichts anderes vermerkt wird", and lists the differences: travel to work, the degressive social deduction, property, the rental value, insurance premiums, qualified participations, parties, the two-earner deduction, the children, own care and childcare, support, the married couple, capital payments (lu-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 54 and 55; lu-2026-guide-wegleitung-unterjaehrig p. 54 and 55). The optional Ergänzungsblatt Direkte Bundessteuer lays them out Ziffer by Ziffer (lu-2025-form-ergaenzungsblatt-dbst p. 1): the Nettoeinkommen of Ziffer 310 plus the cantonal travel costs (208t, 209t) and insurance deduction (270), less the federal ones (Ziffer 12, Nettoeinkommen), less the medical costs above a share of Ziffer 12, the donations up to a share of Ziffer 12, the parties, the two-earner and childcare deductions (Ziffer 19, Reineinkommen), less the children and the married couple (Ziffer 23). rules-lu (src/ifd.ts) follows the sheet: every deduction the guide doesn't list as different keeps its cantonal amount inside Ziffer 310, and the federal rules of packages/rules-ch give the rest, each on its Ziffer of the sheet. Ziffer 4, 6, 11, 14 and 21 are not covered (property, qualified participations, disability costs, support), as for the cantonal tax.
- Where that gives another federal tax than Geneva's form for the same household (pinned in packages/rules-lu/test/federal.test.ts; a seeded test of 2,000 households is equal otherwise):
  - daily allowances count in the base of the 3 % flat for other professional costs (lu-2026-deductions-steuerbuch-band1 p. 106, § 33 Nr. 3 Ziff. 1), as on Geneva's form, while Zurich's, Basel-Landschaft's and Zug's federal columns take the salary alone; the federal instructions say "salaire net" (ch-2026-ifd-instructions-personnes-physiques p. 6) without naming allowances;
  - the net salary of that flat is box 11 of the certificate, after a buy-in of box 10.2 (lu-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 21), where packages/rules-ch takes the salary less boxes 9 and 10.1;
  - the flat costs of keeping securities (WV Ziffer 6) count for the federal tax too: the overviews print the same flat in their Bund column (lu-2025-deductions-abzuege-auf-einen-blick p. 1, lu-2026-deductions-abzuege-auf-einen-blick p. 1), where Geneva's form takes the costs given;
  - the medical costs and the donations are measured on Ziffer 12, before the two-earner and childcare deductions (Ziffer 17 and 18), where Geneva's subtotals 92.20 and 92.40 come after them; the overviews word the base as "Nettoeinkommen (Ziffer 310)" in both columns, the sheet as its Ziffer 12;
  - a 2025 side job: no federal document of the library gives the 2025 federal flat's rate (packages/rules-ch, ifdSideJobFlat), but the 2025 overview prints the flat in its Bund column (lu-2025-deductions-abzuege-auf-einen-blick p. 1, Ziffer 236/237), so Lucerne's 2025 federal tax with a side job is computed, where Zug's, Bern's and Basel-Landschaft's are not. A human could confirm with the Dienststelle Steuern.
- Conflicts in the canton's federal amounts (the federal circular's are used, packages/rules-ch): the 2025 Ergänzungsblatt prints the federal cap on travel costs and the married couple's insurance amount without contributions otherwise than the federal circular (ch-2026-ifd-progression-deductions p. 1 and 2, 2025 column) and the 2025 overview (lu-2025-deductions-abzuege-auf-einen-blick p. 1); the guide's page on the differences prints that insurance amount as the sheet does, in 2025 and in the 2026 edition (lu-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 54, lu-2026-guide-wegleitung-unterjaehrig p. 54), while the 2026 overview prints the circular's. The sheet's labels in packages/filing-lu leave the amounts out. A human should confirm with the Dienststelle Steuern which amounts the assessment uses (the sheet's look like earlier years').
- Pension fund membership for the 3a limit: rules-lu (existing rule) counts a buy-in taken from the salary (box 10.2) as membership, and a main job only; the federal insurance amount reads membership as the other cantons' federal columns do (the answer, else box 10.1 of any job). Through Ziffer 310 the 3a deduction is the cantonal one; the two readings differ only for a certificate with box 10.2 and no 10.1, or a fund on a side job only.
- Limits for the final check: the cantonal rules record each cap, threshold, minimum and flat-or-actual choice; on the lines the federal tax takes as they are (form B's meals, other costs and side job, training, debt interest, 3a) a limit is both taxes'; travel, insurance and the additional and social deductions have each tax's own, the federal ones on the sheet's Ziffern.
- Childcare given for the household (the invoices of a crèche): the limit is per child under 14 living with you, on top of own care (StG § 42 Abs. 1 lit. b, lu-2026-law-steuergesetz p. 24; StV § 14b, lu-2026-law-steuerverordnung p. 6, for a child living with each parent in turn). It counts where it can be told apart per child: one such child, or a total within the smallest of their limits (then any split gives the same). Otherwise the questionnaire asks the amount per child (lu.child_care, as form U/F lists each child, lu-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 34), and the tax is not computed without it. Childcare given for a child of 14 or more, or not living with you, is not counted and the result says so (before the wiring it was left out silently).
- The children: the questionnaire asks, per child, whether they live in your household (page 1 of the return asks it, lu-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 20), whether they were under 14 at the end of the year (own care and childcare, p. 34) and whether they stay away from home for their education (352, p. 34; asked from 14, or for a child living elsewhere). A child who does not live with you leaves the federal tax not computed (packages/rules-ch reads no such child yet), as in Zug.
- The church tax: each adult's confession and each child's (page 1, Ziffer 2 and 3, p. 20), for the children the household counts (not when the other parent counts them, child support paid); the church tax is computed once every adult answered (opt-in), and a child's confession not answered then stops the computation (rules-lu's NOTE.childConfession, fail closed). The shares of a family of several confessions follow rules-lu's reading of § 240 Abs. 2 (open point above).
- The family tariff in shared custody without child support: asked as rules-lu needs it (lu.family_tariff, p. 20); the federal tax reads the shared question on child support and who earns more (the parental scale, ch-na-circulaire-30-epoux-famille p. 24).
- The 3a catch-up: no Lucerne document of the library gives its cantonal rule (rules-lu fails closed), so the plans show no catch-up saving and say why.
- The 3a line is now kept when nothing is deducted (no earned income), so a payment given still needs the user's OK, as on the other cantons' forms; the actual professional costs are flagged as needing an OK or as an estimate only when they replace the flat. Both were found by the seeded comparison with Geneva's flags.
- The return for 2026: the blank 2025 forms cannot be downloaded and the 2026 ones are not out (Gaps); packages/filing-lu maps the 2025 forms from the guide's sample return (lu-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 14 to 19: form B, the securities list's summary, pages 2 to 4) and the 2025 Ergänzungsblatt for both years, 2026 flagged provisional. The back of form B (person 2) is not printed in the guide; its Ziffern are the overview's (lu-2025-deductions-abzuege-auf-einen-blick p. 1) and page 3's. Forms V, S, K and U/F are not laid out: the copy panel gives the lines they carry. The return prints the husband as Person 1 (p. 20); the copy panel says to swap the values when a couple's return has them the other way round.
- Communes: by name, the canton's 79; no BFS numbers. The plans assume the city of Lucerne when no commune is answered.
- Asking for more time to file: online with the e-Fristerstreckung at steuern.lu.ch, else in writing to the Gemeindesteueramt (lu-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 10); the final check cites that page. The official software is eSteuern.LU, in the browser, with an own account and the PersID and Zugangscode eFiling printed on the return (lu-2025-efiling-merkblatt-esteuern).
