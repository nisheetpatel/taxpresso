# St. Gallen (SG) sources: notes for natural persons, tax years 2025 and 2026

Verified 2026-10-13. Each fact is followed by its official source. The documents are the source of truth; never copy a number from this file.

## Filing channels (tax year 2025)
- E-Tax SG is the canton's online tax return, a web app started at steuern.sg.ch/etaxnp (app host etax.sg.ch). From tax period 2025 it replaces the downloadable eTaxes software; it runs on computer, tablet and phone without installation (https://www.sg.ch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/elektronische-steuererklaerung/etaxnp.html).
- No desktop version is offered for tax period 2025. The old eTaxes desktop software stays downloadable for tax years 2021 to 2024 (https://www.sg.ch/content/dam/sgch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/formulare-und-wegleitungen/einkommens-und-vermoegenssteuer/2025-hs/Wegleitung_NP_2025.pdf, page 5).
- Login is the cantonal E-Login, a service of the canton and the St.Gallen communes built on AGOV, the federal authorities login; it uses the "AGOV access" app or a security key instead of a password (https://www.sg.ch/content/dam/sgch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/formulare-und-wegleitungen/einkommens-und-vermoegenssteuer/2025-hs/Faltblatt_NP_2025.pdf).
- Fully digital filing (eFiling): upload all required receipts and submit; no signed receipt and no postal dispatch (Wegleitung 2025, page 10, URL above).
- Without uploads: only the data is sent electronically; print and sign the Freigabequittung produced by E-Tax SG and mail it with the receipts to the Gemeindesteueramt (Wegleitung 2025, page 10).
- A return prepared in E-Tax SG can only be submitted electronically; printing it and mailing it instead is not possible (Wegleitung 2025, page 10).
- Upload limits: 10 MB per file, 100 MB and 100 documents per return; password-protected or digitally signed PDFs can make the submission fail (https://www.sg.ch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/elektronische-steuererklaerung/etaxnp.html).
- Paper filing: Formular 1 (return) and Formular 2 (securities list) are mandatory for everyone; other forms only when needed. The return goes to the Gemeindesteueramt and is signed personally; spouses both sign (Wegleitung 2025, pages 6 to 8).
- Someone else can fill in a return in E-Tax SG with the taxpayer's register number and access code (printed top right of the mailed return), or through the share function, which the owner can revoke (https://www.sg.ch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/elektronische-steuererklaerung/etaxnp.html).
- Representatives need a written power of attorney; the duty to sign the return personally cannot be delegated (Wegleitung 2025, page 8).
- Partial-year 2026 returns (emigration or death in 2026) are also filed in E-Tax SG, which detects them automatically (https://www.sg.ch/content/dam/sgch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/formulare-und-wegleitungen/einkommens-und-vermoegenssteuer/2026-uj/Formular_8_NP_2026_UJ.pdf).
- Since release 1.1.9 of 2 July 2026, data for the next tax period can be pre-entered in E-Tax SG (https://www.sg.ch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/elektronische-steuererklaerung/etaxnp.html).
- A public demo environment exists at etaxdemo.sg.ch; it needs an E-Login and cannot transmit (same page).
- The eKonto/eFaktoren portal shows the tax account, adjusts the provisional bill and offers the online extension (https://www.sg.ch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/ekonto-efaktoren-privatperson---ihr-online-steuerportal.html).

## Third-party software
- The guide allows commercial software from private providers "under very specific conditions" and says these programs are not checked (homologiert) by the Kantonales Steueramt (Wegleitung 2025, page 9).
- Printouts from such software are accepted only if identical in layout and content to the official forms, enclosed per original form (A3, double-sided A4, not stapled), black on white, with name and register number on every sheet, and dated and signed (Wegleitung 2025, page 9).
- No approval procedure, list of approved software or data interface for third-party software was found on the sg.ch pages read (https://www.sg.ch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/elektronische-steuererklaerung/etaxnp.html).
- Vendor: the FAQ for the old eTaxes desktop software names Information Factory as its software manufacturer (https://www.sg.ch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/elektronische-steuererklaerung/etaxnp/download-elektronische-steuererklaerung-frueherer-jahre/faq-etaxes.html).
- sg.ch does not name the vendor of E-Tax SG. The help site that sg.ch links as "E-Tax SG Hilfe" is hosted at sg-support.etax.ch and its footer reads "Support © Ringler Informatik AG"; it is a vendor-run site, so it is not in the manifest (https://sg-support.etax.ch/hc/de).

## Deadlines
- The guide says the return and its enclosures are due by the Einreichetermin printed on page 1 of the personal return; no general date for natural persons was found on the canton pages read (Wegleitung 2025, page 8).
- Extension: ask before the deadline, online at steuern.sg.ch with the register number and date of birth, otherwise in writing to the Gemeindesteueramt (Wegleitung 2025, page 8).
- The same online request is offered on the Schalter E portal (https://www.schalter-e.sg.ch/eservices/kanton/fristverlaengerung-steuererklaerung.html) and in eKonto (https://www.sg.ch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/ekonto-efaktoren-privatperson---ihr-online-steuerportal.html).
- The online request is approved or refused immediately; an extension is only granted if the tax bills due are paid (https://www.sg.ch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/fristverlaengerung/privatpersonen.html).
- The return deadline is an authority deadline: a timely request with a sufficient reason gives a right to an extension, and it can be extended electronically without further ado; legal deadlines cannot be extended (https://www.sg.ch/content/dam/sgch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuerbuch/art-158-227-stg/161_1.pdf).
- No extension fee and no latest extension date are stated on the pages and documents read (same sources).
- The City of St.Gallen tax office says approved extension requests from fiduciaries and representatives are not confirmed (communal page, https://www.stadt.sg.ch/home/verwaltung-politik/direktionen/inneres-finanzen/steueramt.html).
- The final bill is payable within 30 days; Ausgleichszinsen apply to provisional payments (Wegleitung 2025, page 49).

## Source tax (Quellensteuer, NOV: nachträgliche ordentliche Veranlagung, and rectification)
- Residents taxed at source can request an NOV by 31 March of the following year; when leaving Switzerland, the deadline is the deregistration date (Art. 112ter Abs. 3 StG, https://www.gesetzessammlung.sg.ch/api/de/versions/3822/pdf_file).
- A request filed in due form and time cannot be withdrawn. For residents, NOV then continues every year until source taxation ends; for non-residents it covers only the year requested (https://www.sg.ch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuerarten/quellensteuer/haeufig-gestellte-fragen.html).
- Since tax year 2021 the canton no longer corrects source tax for extra deductions such as pillar 3a, second-pillar buy-ins or debt interest; NOV is the only way (same FAQ page).
- Mandatory NOV (Art. 112bis StG) applies when gross income reaches the amount set by the federal finance department, or when there are income or assets not taxed at source; people with other income or assets must request the return form by 31 March of the following year (Art. 112bis Abs. 4 StG, same law PDF).
- Non-residents can request NOV by 31 March when most of their worldwide income is taxable in Switzerland, their situation is comparable to a resident's, or a treaty deduction requires it (Art. 122a StG, same law PDF).
- Rectification: a person who disagrees with the employer's source-tax certificate, or did not get one, can ask for a ruling (Verfügung) by 31 March of the year after the payment (Art. 186 Abs. 1bis StG, same law PDF).
- Employers can correct their own source-tax returns until the end of March of the following year (FAQ page above).
- Request forms: 51.1.13a for residents and 51.1.13b for non-residents, sent to Kantonales Steueramt, Quellensteuer, Davidstrasse 41, Postfach 1245, 9001 St.Gallen (https://www.sg.ch/content/dam/sgch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/formulare-und-wegleitungen/quellensteuer/weitere-formulare/veranlagung/SG_51-1-13a_Antrag-Veranlagung_Ansaessige-Quest_def_.pdf).
- 2025 and 2026 tariffs A, B, C, G, H, capital benefits and the yearly Berechnungsgrundlagen are on the source-tax forms page; the 2026 files sit in a folder named 20251 (https://www.sg.ch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/formulare-wegleitungen/quellensteuern.html).
- German cross-border commuters returning daily get the linear tariffs L, M, N, P and Q (https://www.sg.ch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuerarten/quellensteuer.html).
- Practice notes: StB 105 Nr. 1 on foreign employees and StB 112 Nr. 1 on NOVA follow ESTV Kreisschreiben Nr. 45 (https://www.sg.ch/content/dam/sgch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuerbuch/art-105-129-stg/112_1.pdf).

## e-tax statements (eCH-0196) and other imports
- The guide refers to bank tax statements imported with E-Tax SG: when filing with the signed release receipt and receipts by post, the full statements must be enclosed; with full eFiling they are transmitted automatically (Wegleitung 2025, page 36).
- The E-Tax SG help (vendor-hosted, see above) describes adding an eSteuerauszug, which is recognised by its barcode pages and read automatically, with options to reclassify entries (https://sg-support.etax.ch/hc/de/articles/25891860972956-eSteuerauszug-hochladen).
- For the old eTaxes software, an imported eSteuerauszug replaced the upload of the bank statement as a required enclosure (https://www.sg.ch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/elektronische-steuererklaerung/etaxnp/download-elektronische-steuererklaerung-frueherer-jahre/faq-etaxes.html).
- No sg.ch page read names the eCH-0196 standard, and no barcode was seen on the blank 2025 forms (https://www.sg.ch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/formulare-wegleitungen/einkommens-vermoegenssteuer-privatpersonen.html).
- Prior-year import: E-Tax SG imports data from eTaxes 2024 files (.sgnp2024) and from E-Tax SG exports (.etax.zip) (https://www.sg.ch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/elektronische-steuererklaerung/etaxnp.html).

## Tariffs, multipliers and deductions (pointers to documents)
- The tariff gives the einfache Steuer at 100 percent; it is multiplied by the Gesamtsteuerfuss (canton plus political commune plus church), set every year (Art. 5 and 6 StG, https://www.gesetzessammlung.sg.ch/api/de/versions/3822/pdf_file; Wegleitung 2025, page 50).
- One income tariff for everyone; couples and single parents use Vollsplitting: the rate for half the income applies to the whole income (https://www.sg.ch/content/dam/sgch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/formulare-und-wegleitungen/einkommens-und-vermoegenssteuer/tarife-und-steuerfuesse/tarife-privatpersonen-gueltig-ab/Tarif%202025%20SG%20Def.pdf, page 1).
- Wealth tax is a flat einfache Steuer in per mille, then multiplied the same way (Tarif 2025, page 1).
- 2026 income tariff: https://www.sg.ch/content/dam/sgch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/formulare-und-wegleitungen/einkommens-und-vermoegenssteuer/tarife-und-steuerfuesse/tarife-privatpersonen-gueltig-ab/Tarif_2026_SG_DEF.pdf (published, created March 2026).
- The cantonal multiplier is set by the Kantonsrat with the budget (Art. 6 StG); its history including 2025 and 2026 is in StB 6 Nr. 1 (https://www.sg.ch/content/dam/sgch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuerbuch/art-1-12-stg/006_1.pdf).
- The 2025 multipliers for every commune and church community are in https://www.sg.ch/content/dam/sgch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/formulare-und-wegleitungen/einkommens-und-vermoegenssteuer/tarife-und-steuerfuesse/steuerfuesse-st-gallische-gemeinden/Steuerfuesse%202025%20finale%20Version%20fuer%20Internet.pdf.
- The 2026 multipliers are in https://www.sg.ch/content/dam/sgch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/formulare-und-wegleitungen/einkommens-und-vermoegenssteuer/tarife-und-steuerfuesse/steuerfuesse-st-gallische-gemeinden/Steuerf%C3%BCsse%202026%20f%C3%BCr%20Versand.pdf.
- Those PDFs give, per commune, the political commune's multiplier and Grundsteuer rate, the Catholic and Protestant church rates per Kirchgemeinde, and the combined totals; the Christkatholisch rate is one statewide rate; church rates include a Zentralsteuer (headers of both PDFs).
- 2025 deduction caps and flat rates are in the Kurzübersicht and in Formular 4 (https://www.sg.ch/content/dam/sgch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/formulare-und-wegleitungen/einkommens-und-vermoegenssteuer/2025-hs/Kurzuebersicht_Wegleitung_NP_2025.pdf).
- Steuerbuch practice for deductions: commuting StB 39 Nr. 3, meals StB 39 Nr. 8, pillar 3a StB 45 Nr. 9, childcare StB 45 Nr. 14, donations StB 46 Nr. 4, child deduction StB 48 Nr. 1 (index: https://www.sg.ch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuerbuch/inhaltsverzeichnis.html).
- Federal tax differences on the same return are in Merkblatt 32 (https://www.sg.ch/content/dam/sgch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/formulare-und-wegleitungen/einkommens-und-vermoegenssteuer/2025-hs/Formular_32_NP_2025_HA.pdf).
- Crypto assets go in the securities list at the ESTV year-end price; private price gains are tax free (https://www.sg.ch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/kryptowaehrungen.html).
- The list of tax-exempt St.Gallen institutions for the donation deduction is on https://www.sg.ch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/zuwendungen-an-gemeinnuetzige-organisationen0.html (PDF Stand 1 October 2026; its file name changes with each update).

## Open points for the rules (packages/rules-sg, 2026-10-14)

Found while reading the downloaded documents for the cantonal, communal and church income and wealth tax of 2025 and 2026. Each cites the document id and page ("p." is the PDF's page; the guide prints the same numbers); the numbers live in the documents.

- The income tariff is the law's brackets (sg-2025-law-steuergesetz and sg-2026-law-steuergesetz Art. 50 Abs. 1 and 2, p. 26; 2026 changed lit. d, lit. f and Abs. 2), not a formula. scripts/sg-tariff.mjs --check reproduces every row of both tariff tables (sg-2025-tariff-einkommen-vermoegen and sg-2026-tariff-einkommen-vermoegen, p. 2 to 105: 5,185 and 5,191 rows, single and Vollsplitting, amounts and percentages) and their page 1 (the top amounts, Vollsplitting on half the income rounded down to 100 francs, 1.7 per mille on wealth).
- Rounding: the tariff's page 1 says "Die Rappen sind kaufmännisch gerundet". The Vollsplitting column shows this means the nearest 5 Rappen (50,100 gives 1,442.88 and the table prints 1,442.90; 60,700 gives 2,079.4257 and it prints 2,079.45); rounding to the Rappen misses rows. Not in the documents: whether the taxable income and wealth are taken in whole francs, and how the einfache Steuer times the canton's, the commune's and the church's multiplier is rounded (Merkblatt 8 prints 1,424.00 x 270 % = 3,844.80, sg-2026-guide-merkblatt-unterjaehrig p. 3, which fits any rounding). rules-sg takes whole francs and rounds each tax to the nearest 5 centimes, and says so (NOTE.rounding).
- Conflict, the guide's worked example: Ehepaar X with a taxable income of CHF 80,000 has an einfache Steuer of 3,516 and 9,493 at 270 % (sg-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 50). The 2025 tariff prints 3,488.00 for a couple at 80,000 (sg-2025-tariff-einkommen-vermoegen p. 16), and the law gives the same (the rate of 40,000, 1,744 / 40,000 = 4.36 %, Art. 50 Abs. 3). The example's wealth part (400,000: 680, and 1,836 at 270 %) matches. rules-sg follows the tariff and the law; its test keeps both numbers.
- Conflict, the rate per km for a motorbike when public transport can't be used: the ordinance takes the federal rates for a private vehicle (sg-na-law-steuerverordnung Art. 18 Abs. 3, p. 5), which are lower for motorbikes than for cars (ch-2026-ifd-progression-deductions p. 1), while the 2025 guide gives "70 Rappen je Fahrkilometer für Motorfahrzeuge" (sg-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 23), Form 4 has one line "km x Fr. 0.70" (sg-2025-form-berufskosten p. 1, 1.3) and the Steuerbuch gives "75 Rappen je Fahrkilometer für Motorräder und Autos" for 2026 (sg-na-deductions-fahrt-arbeitsort p. 3, Ziff. 2.5). rules-sg fails closed for that case (NOTE.motorbikeRate); cars are computed at the guide's and the Steuerbuch's rate.
- Conflict, the higher insurance maximum: the law raises the maximum "wenn keine Beiträge nach Bst. d und e dieser Bestimmung abgezogen werden" (Art. 45 Abs. 1 lit. g, p. 23), and lit. d names the AHV/IV contributions as well as the pension fund's; the guide and Form 6 say "wenn keine Beiträge für die berufliche Vorsorge (2. Säule) oder eine gebundene Selbstvorsorge (Säule 3a) abgezogen werden", for a couple both spouses (sg-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 27; sg-2025-form-versicherungspraemien-krankheitskosten p. 1, B). rules-sg follows the guide and says so whenever it gives the higher maximum (NOTE.insuranceExtra).
- Conflict, children's education costs with scholarships: the law reduces the education costs deduction by the state scholarships, at most down to the deduction for a child in training (Art. 48 Abs. 1 lit. a, p. 26), that is after the maximum; Form 10 takes the scholarships off the costs before the excess (sg-na-form-ausbildungs-kinderbetreuungskosten p. 1), and the Steuerbuch takes them off the actual costs (sg-na-deductions-kinderabzug p. 9, Ziff. 3.4). The two give different amounts only when the costs above the excess pass the maximum and there are scholarships; rules-sg fails closed then (NOTE.educationScholarships).
- Merkblatt 8 and party donations: the "New for 2026" list above says Merkblatt 8 raises the cap on party donations. The page as printed lists only gaming stakes, childcare and children's education costs (sg-2026-guide-merkblatt-unterjaehrig p. 4); its text layer holds a hidden "16.3 Parteispenden" heading with no amount, and the 2026 law keeps the 2025 amounts (Art. 45 Abs. 1 lit. i, p. 24). rules-sg uses the law.
- 2026 has no guide and no forms yet (Gaps above). rules-sg takes 2026 amounts from the 2026 law, the ordinance (Stand 2024, in force for both years), the Steuerbuch chapter on travel dated 1 January 2026 (sg-na-deductions-fahrt-arbeitsort p. 3 and 4), Merkblatt 8 section 11 and, where the ordinance adopts the federal flat rates (meals, Art. 19; side jobs, Art. 22), the federal circular of the year (ch-2026-ifd-progression-deductions p. 2). The flat CHF 400 for professional training without proof is in the 2025 guide only (sg-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 29); rules-sg does not apply it in 2026 and says so when it would have mattered. The 2026 return's line codes are taken to be the 2025 ones.
- Interpretation, a side job without a main job: the guide says such people have no right to the side job's flat costs and then "Ihnen stehen die Abzüge gemäss Ziffer 1 bis 5 zu" (sg-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 25), Ziffer 5 being the side job's own. rules-sg counts the job as the main job (1.1, Form 4, 1 to 3) and says so (NOTE.sideOnly).
- Church tax: the table gives each church area's (Kirchgemeinde's) Catholic and Evangelical rate in percent of the einfache Steuer, including a Zentralsteuer (footnotes 1 and 2), and one Christ Catholic rate for the canton (the remarks); the Jewish community, which StV Art. 1 names, has no rate in the library (rules-sg fails closed for 'other'). In a mixed marriage half the tax goes to each spouse's church (sg-na-law-steuerverordnung Art. 2 Abs. 1, p. 1); the ordinance does not say what happens to the half of a spouse with no church (rules-sg levies none and says so), and each spouse can ask for the whole family's affiliation instead (Abs. 2, not computed). Where a commune's areas levy different rates (or carry different footnotes) for the member's confession, the church tax needs the area: 21 communes in one year or both (St. Gallen, Au, Berneck in 2026, Balgach, Altstätten, Oberriet, Vilters-Wangs, Mels, Amden, Gommiswald, Wildhaus-Alt St. Johann, Nesslau in 2025, Wattwil, Neckertal, Mosnang, Oberuzwil, Oberbüren, Niederhelfenschwil, Gossau, Waldkirch, Gaiserwald). Footnote 3 (parts of the area belong to parishes of another canton) and footnote 4 (larger parts belong to other parishes) mark rates that are not everyone's there; rules-sg uses the printed rate and says so.
- Practice the questions must reflect: with joint custody and no child support, the child deduction goes to the parent who mainly bears the upkeep, and with equal shares the canton presumes it is the parent with the lower net income (sg-na-deductions-kinderabzug p. 7, Ziff. 2.3.3, after BGE 141 II 338), the opposite of Basel-Landschaft's practice. The childcare deduction follows the child deduction between two households (sg-na-deductions-kinderbetreuungskosten p. 2). A child becomes of school age on the 1 August after its fourth birthday (sg-na-deductions-kinderabzug p. 2, Ziff. 2.1.2).
- Not in the documents: the communes' federal numbers (BFS); the table lists the 75 political communes and their 111 church areas by name. A move inside the canton: the law applies the federal rules between cantons to communes (Art. 233 Abs. 1, p. 90), so the commune at the end of the year taxes the whole year.
- The federal tax: the return has one column and the tax office derives the federal tax from it (sg-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 49; the differences are in sg-2025-guide-merkblatt-dbst). rules-sg gives `ifd: null`, as Basel-Landschaft and Ticino do.
- Not covered by rules-sg: self-employment (2.1, 2.2), property (5, 15, 31, the Grundsteuer), capital benefits taxed apart (Art. 52), dividends from qualifying holdings (4.2 to 4.4), lottery winnings and their stakes, weekly stays away from home (Form 4, 4), shift work meals (Form 4, 2.3), disability costs (21.2), the deduction for supported persons (23.4, federal only), the flat 2 per mille for managed securities (16.1; the costs are taken as given), part-year liability, and the fire service levy (Feuerwehrabgabe, named in sg-na-law-steuerverordnung Art. 56 Abs. 3).
