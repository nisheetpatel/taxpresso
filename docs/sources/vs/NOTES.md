# Valais (VS) official sources for Taxpresso

Verified 2026-10-13 on vs.ch and lex.vs.ch. Each fact is followed by its source URL. Tariff numbers are omitted on purpose.

## Filing channels, tax year 2025
- VSTax is the free cantonal software; the Tell Tax app attaches supporting documents. (https://www.vs.ch/documents/d/scc/guide-2025_f_final)
- Option A: all documents sent online, no signature. Option B: partly online, paper for some documents, with a pre-addressed cover letter. Option C: full printing, which the guide advises against. (https://www.vs.ch/documents/d/scc/guide-2025_f_final)
- Fully online sending needs no printed or signed page; an electronic receipt is issued and must be kept. (https://www.vs.ch/fr/web/ext-cant-gouv-scc-vstax/faq)
- Standard online sending returns a transmission receipt to print and sign, sent to the tax office with any paper documents. (https://www.vs.ch/fr/web/ext-cant-gouv-scc-vstax/faq)
- A paper return must be signed personally; spouses sign jointly; paper goes to the SCC scanning centre in Sion. (https://www.vs.ch/documents/d/scc/guide-2025_f_final)
- VSTax login method is not stated; vs.ch has a general portal login and a "Login IAM" link. Gap. (https://www.vs.ch/fr/web/ext-cant-gouv-scc-vstax)
- VSTax technical hotline 058 660 22 11, weekdays 9:00-11:00 and 14:00-17:00. (https://www.vs.ch/fr/web/ext-cant-gouv-scc-vstax)

## Third-party and fiduciary software
- Tax representatives may prepare and file returns; the guide does not say how they submit electronically. (https://www.vs.ch/documents/d/scc/guide-2025_f_final)
- No approved-software list or third-party submission rule was found on the VSTax page or FAQ. Gap; ask the SCC. (https://www.vs.ch/fr/web/ext-cant-gouv-scc-vstax/faq)
- A tax representative can request an extension directly, without the payment slip. (https://www.vs.ch/documents/d/scc/guide-2025_f_final)

## Deadlines, tax year 2025
- Return with all annexes due 31 March 2026. (https://www.vs.ch/documents/d/scc/guide-2025_f_final)
- Extension: pay the CHF 20 fee with the payment slip before 31 March 2026; no written request needed. (https://www.vs.ch/documents/d/scc/guide-2025_f_final)
- Automatic extensions: 31 July 2026 without a main self-employed activity; 31 October 2026 with one. (https://www.vs.ch/documents/d/scc/guide-2025_f_final)
- Late filing: summons first, then an administrative fine, up to CHF 10,000 in repeat cases. (https://www.vs.ch/documents/d/scc/guide-2025_f_final)
- Correction window after fully online sending: 15 days per the guide and VSTax page, but 10 days per the FAQ. Conflict; confirm with SCC. (https://www.vs.ch/documents/d/scc/guide-2025_f_final ; https://www.vs.ch/fr/web/ext-cant-gouv-scc-vstax/faq)
- Up to 9 resends and corrections before the transmission reaches the tax office. (https://www.vs.ch/fr/web/ext-cant-gouv-scc-vstax/faq)

## Source tax (impôt à la source)
- TOU requests are filed online only; paper is not accepted. (https://www.vs.ch/web/scc/tou-2024)
- Deadline: 31 March of the year after the tax year, so 31 March 2026 for tax year 2025, which has passed. The page titled TOU 2025 gives 31 March 2025 for tax year 2024. (https://www.vs.ch/web/scc/tou-2024)
- A properly filed TOU request cannot be withdrawn. (https://www.vs.ch/web/scc/tou-2024)
- For residents, a TOU applies to later years until source-tax liability ends; a spouse in the household is also bound, including after separation or divorce. (https://www.vs.ch/web/scc/tou-2024)
- Rectification deadline: not on the TOU page; it may sit in the 2025 directives PDF, which could not be fetched. Gap. (https://www.vs.ch/web/scc/baremes-source)
- No link to the 2026 source-tax directives on the barème page. Gap. (https://www.vs.ch/web/scc/baremes-source)

## Tariffs and communal rates
- The ordinary cantonal barème is an annex to the Loi fiscale (RS 642.1-A1); the annex was not fetched. (https://lex.vs.ch/api/fr/versions/354/pdf_file)
- Cantonal indexation PDFs exist for 2025 and for 2026 (new tranches). (https://www.vs.ch/fr/web/scc/baremes-canton-communes)
- One communal coefficients and indexation PDF covers 2022 to 2027, so it includes 2025 and 2026. (https://www.vs.ch/fr/web/scc/baremes-canton-communes)
- The guide refers to the barème ordinaire but does not reproduce it. (https://www.vs.ch/documents/d/scc/guide-2025_f_final)

## Deductions
- The 2026 deductions overview (dated 7 Nov 2025) lists fixed deductions and caps, with a 2025 column. (https://www.vs.ch/documents/d/scc/deductions-forfaitaires-2026)
- Guide section 4 covers professional expenses (including telework), pension contributions, medical costs, passive interest, property costs and family deductions. (https://www.vs.ch/documents/d/scc/guide-2025_f_final)
- Caps apply to commuting costs under the federal tax and to pension and 3a contributions. (https://www.vs.ch/documents/d/scc/guide-2025_f_final)
- Passive crypto income (mining, staking, liquidity mining, lending) falls under wealth income. (https://www.vs.ch/documents/d/scc/guide-2025_f_final)

## What is new for 2025 and 2026
- The 2024 LF amendment requires debtors of taxable benefits to file statements electronically, changes lottery winnings rules, and adds a deduction for people over pension age who keep working. Entry into force was staged, no later than 1 January 2026; status not confirmed. (https://lex.vs.ch/frontend/materials/1124?locale=fr)
- The 2026 deductions overview says a change to the LF takes effect 1 January 2026. (https://www.vs.ch/documents/d/scc/deductions-forfaitaires-2026)
- The 2026 barème uses new tranches. (https://www.vs.ch/fr/web/scc/baremes-canton-communes)

## Legal basis for e-filing
- Loi fiscale, RS 642.1, fetched as lex.vs.ch version 354; the excerpt shows no état date, so confirm it is current. (https://lex.vs.ch/api/fr/versions/354/pdf_file)
- The 2024 amendment cites the federal law on electronic tax procedures of 18 June 2021; not fetched. (https://lex.vs.ch/frontend/materials/1124?locale=fr)
- No cantonal e-filing ordinance was found; the VSTax FAQ cites no legal basis. Gap. (https://www.vs.ch/fr/web/ext-cant-gouv-scc-vstax/faq)
