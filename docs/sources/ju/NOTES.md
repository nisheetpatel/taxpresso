## Filing channels, tax year 2025
- The 2025 return is completed on JuraTax Online, reached from the Guichet virtuel, "100% en ligne", signed electronically with SwissID (https://www.jura.ch/fr/Autorites/Administration/DFI/CTR/JuraTax/JuraTax.html)
- Login: SwissID account with verified identity; supporting documents can be sent from a phone; no software install needed (same URL)
- Alternative: JuraTax Logiciel 2025, a free desktop program (version 1.1.0, dated 22.01.2026); uploading needs an internet connection (https://guichet.jura.ch/fr/Juratax/Juratax-Logiciel.html)
- For older years the software page says the return can be printed and sent; it says nothing of the sort for 2025 (same URL)
- Whether a signed paper page is still required for 2025 is not stated on the fetched pages; confirm with the section (https://www.jura.ch/fr/Autorites/Administration/DFI/CTR/JuraTax/JuraTax.html)
- Electronic tax statements (eRelevés fiscaux) can pre-fill accounts, securities and debts (same URL as JuraTax Online)

## Third-party and fiduciary software

## Deadlines (tax year 2025)
- Ordinary filing deadline: 28 February 2026, "Délai de remise de la déclaration d'impôt" (https://www.jura.ch/fr/Autorites/Administration/DFI/CTR/Personnes-physiques/Calendrier-fiscal/Calendrier-fiscal.html)
- Later 2026 dates for 2025 returns: paper posting by Thursday 13 August 2026; reminders dated 21 August 2026; returns due by 18 September 2026 to avoid a sommation; extension to 31 October 2026 can be requested (https://www.jura.ch/fr/Autorites/Administration/DFI/CTR/Service-des-contributions-CTR.html)
- Read on 13 October 2026, only the 31 October 2026 extension date is still open (inference from the dates above)
- Extension form: requested deadline 31 10 2026; CHF 31 charged on the 2025 assessment; request sent to the Section des personnes physiques (https://www.jura.ch/Htdocs/Files/v/8302cd67c048a1eed78ecf8681035f7bfd23736f0d53d493315adc5349e37be9.pdf)
