# Aargau (AG) sources: notes for natural persons, tax years 2025 and 2026

Verified 2026-10-13. Each fact is followed by its official source. The documents are the source of truth; never copy a number from this file.

## Filing channels (tax year 2025)
- The online channel is eTAX AARGAU, a browser application at etax.ag.ch with no installation; it replaces EasyTax from tax period 2025 and opened on 26 January 2026 (https://www.ag.ch/de/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuererklaerung-einreichen/etax-aargau).
- Login: an AGOV account (AGOV access app or FIDO2 security key) plus the Adressnummer and Zugangscode printed on page 1 of the Steuererklärungsbogen; no E-ID or verified identity is needed (https://www.ag.ch/de/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuererklaerung-einreichen/etax-aargau).
- The Steuererklärungsbogen with the access data still comes by post, at the end of January (https://www.ag.ch/de/medien/medienmitteilungen?mm=etax-aargau-ist-jetzt-verfuegbar-632471b1-83c6-4f15-bb63-285d61384b5d_de); lost codes are reissued by the Gemeindesteueramt (https://www.ag.ch/de/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuererklaerung-einreichen/etax-aargau/etax-aargau-fachinfo).
- A return prepared in eTAX AARGAU can only be submitted electronically; printing it and mailing it to the Gemeindesteueramt is no longer allowed (https://www.ag.ch/de/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuererklaerung-einreichen/etax-aargau/etax-aargau-fachinfo).
- Receipts can go fully online, fully on paper with the Steuererklärungsbogen, or mixed (hybrid filing); eTAX flags required receipts in red (https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/etax-aargau/20260105-etax-belegeonline.pdf).
- Electronic release replaces the personal signature; where the law requires both spouses to sign, they release jointly (https://gesetzessammlungen.ag.ch/api/de/versions/3872/pdf_file).
- One definitive submission per Adressnummer and Zugangscode; after submitting, the return can be changed and resubmitted for 24 hours (https://www.ag.ch/de/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuererklaerung-einreichen/etax-aargau/etax-aargau-hilfe-anleitungen).
- After submission, eTAX offers a definitive printout, a transmission receipt (Übermittlungsquittung) and a provisional tax calculation for download (https://www.ag.ch/de/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuererklaerung-einreichen/etax-aargau/etax-aargau-hilfe-anleitungen).
- Paper filing remains possible: forms come from the Gemeindesteueramt, page 4 is signed (by both spouses or partners), and the return goes to the Gemeindesteueramt printed on the form (https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/steuererklaerung/wegleitung-zur-steuererkl-rung-2025-kanton-aargau.pdf).
- eTAX AARGAU is offered in German only (https://www.ag.ch/de/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuererklaerung-einreichen/etax-aargau).
- Sharing: the taxpayer can share the return with AGOV-registered third parties (code by e-mail and SMS), revoke it at any time, or export and import it as a file; shared users cannot share further and no two people can edit at once (https://www.ag.ch/de/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuererklaerung-einreichen/etax-aargau/etax-aargau-fachinfo).
- Prior-year data can be imported from an EasyTax 2024 file with extension .a24 (https://www.ag.ch/de/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuererklaerung-einreichen/etax-aargau/etax-aargau-hilfe-anleitungen).
- EasyTax online submission ended on 30 September 2026; a 2024 return can still be prepared in EasyTax but must be printed and filed on paper (https://www.ag.ch/de/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuererklaerung-einreichen/easytax).
- Data entered in eTAX stays in cantonal infrastructure; the canton says no tax data flows to AGOV, the Confederation or external suppliers (https://www.ag.ch/de/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuererklaerung-einreichen/etax-aargau).
- Legal persons keep the separate product eTAX AARGAU Juristische Personen (https://www.ag.ch/de/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuererklaerung-einreichen/etax-aargau).

## Third-party software
- The 2025 guide: with another PC program, print on the original forms; printouts on neutral paper are accepted only if identical to the originals; the securities schedule must be scannable (original form or approved PC printout marked "scantauglich"), and page 4 must be signed (https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/steuererklaerung/wegleitung-zur-steuererkl-rung-2025-kanton-aargau.pdf).
- VeVSt § 3 Abs. 1: the Kantonales Steueramt "kann" allow third-party declaration software to transmit electronically; no list of admitted providers, approval procedure or interface specification was found (https://gesetzessammlungen.ag.ch/api/de/versions/3872/pdf_file).
- No vendor is named for eTAX AARGAU on the pages read; the January 2026 press release only calls it an application already proven in many cantons (https://www.ag.ch/de/medien/medienmitteilungen?mm=etax-aargau-ist-jetzt-verfuegbar-632471b1-83c6-4f15-bb63-285d61384b5d_de).
- The former EasyTax software was made by msg systems ag, named as the developer on the EasyTax FAQ (https://www.ag.ch/de/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuererklaerung-einreichen/easytax).

## Deadlines
- Filing deadline: 31 March for employees and pensioners, 30 June for self-employed and shareholders of family companies (https://www.ag.ch/de/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuererklaerung-einreichen/steuererklaerung-einreichen).
- No reminders are sent before 30 June 2026, so no extension request is needed up to 30 June 2026 (https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/steuererklaerung/wegleitung-zur-steuererkl-rung-2025-kanton-aargau.pdf).
- Self-employed: extension requests up to 31 October 2026 are answered only if refused or cut; no answer means approval. Longer extensions need solid reasons and are decided in writing (same Wegleitung PDF, page 10).
- Extension online at www.ag.ch/fristerstreckung with the Adressnummer plus the Zugangscode or date of birth; the request must be made before the deadline (https://www.ag.ch/de/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuererklaerung-einreichen/frist-verlaengern).
- Legal basis for extensions: § 65 Abs. 3 StGV, request to the Gemeindesteueramt before the deadline; a final reminder period of at least 20 days follows a missed deadline (§ 65 Abs. 4) (https://gesetzessammlungen.ag.ch/api/de/versions/3915/pdf_file).
- No fee for an extension is stated; reminder and enforcement fees have applied since 2019, amounts in the guide (https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/steuererklaerung/wegleitung-zur-steuererkl-rung-2025-kanton-aargau.pdf).
- If eTAX AARGAU is unreachable on the last day, the deadline moves to the day after it is reachable again (VeVSt § 6) (https://gesetzessammlungen.ag.ch/api/de/versions/3872/pdf_file).
- The provisional tax bill for the current year is payable by the end of October; earlier payments earn interest (https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/steuererklaerung/wegleitung-zur-steuererkl-rung-2025-kanton-aargau.pdf).

## Source tax (Quellensteuer, NOV: nachträgliche ordentliche Veranlagung, and rectification)
- Two monthly tariff books per year, with church tax (Y) and without (N), covering codes A, B, C, H, G and L, M, N, P for German cross-border commuters; the tables include cantonal, communal and federal tax and the Feuerwehrsteuer (https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/quellensteuer/quellensteuer-arbeitgebende/qust-tarife-und-wegleitungen/quellensteuer-monatstarif-mit-kirchensteuer-ausgabe-2026-1.pdf).
- 2026 tables are valid from 1 January 2026; the index also has 2019 to 2025 and a yearly sheet with the calculation bases (https://www.ag.ch/de/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/qellensteuern-abwickeln/quellensteuertarife).
- NOV for residents is mandatory above an employment-income threshold (no request needed, spouses not added together) and for other income or wealth above set limits; otherwise it can be requested. Once a resident has requested NOV, ordinary assessment continues until source taxation ends (https://www.ag.ch/de/smartserviceportal/dienstleistungen?dl=nachtraegliche-ordentliche-veranlagung-beantragen-08fb882a-157a-4b9d-8a40-6c690d107e06_de).
- NOV deadline: 31 March of the following year (forfeiture deadline), or on deregistration when leaving Switzerland; the request is free. Quasi-residents abroad must request every year (same page).
- The paper NOV form was replaced on 1 January 2026 by the electronic eService NOV; a confirmation is e-mailed after sending (same page).
- Until 31 December 2026 only "NOV 2026 unterjährig" (residents moving abroad) can be filed; "NOV 2026 ganzjährig" opens on 1 January 2027 and must be filed electronically by 31 March 2027 (https://www.ag.ch/de/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/qellensteuern-abwickeln/quellensteuerpflichtige-personen).
- The NOV assessment itself is made by the communal Steuerkommission; source tax already paid is credited without interest (https://www.ag.ch/de/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/quellensteuern-abwickeln).
- Rectification (Neuberechnung der Quellensteuer) for wrong gross wage, rate-determining income or tariff code: by 31 March of the following year, free, by PDF form; no extra deductions (those go through NOV) (https://www.ag.ch/de/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/qellensteuern-abwickeln/quellensteuerpflichtige-personen?dl=neuberechnung-quellensteuer-beantragen-011d777b-cc66-4995-8eba-6f4f282076db_de).
- Aargau has no special agreement with France: French cross-border commuters working for an Aargau employer are taxed at source with the ordinary tariffs (https://www.ag.ch/de/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/qellensteuern-abwickeln/quellensteuerpflichtige-personen).
- Certificates of non-return days for German cross-border commuters are due by 31 March of the following year; late forms are not considered (same page).

## e-tax statements (eCH-0196) and other imports
- eTAX AARGAU imports the eSteuerauszug by upload in the Wertschriften- und Guthabenverzeichnis; categories A, B, DA-1 and RUS, precious metals and custody fees are read automatically; overdraft interest, debts and mortgages are typed by hand (https://www.ag.ch/de/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern-startseite/alles-zu-steuern?dc=e22c5f9c-d44e-447a-afb2-69f3f7d01148_de).
- Imported debts appear as "Hypothek" by default and individual statement lines cannot be deleted (https://www.ag.ch/de/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuererklaerung-einreichen/etax-aargau/etax-aargau-hilfe-anleitungen).
- Values may differ slightly from the bank summary; compare totals. If the import fails, enter the totals as a "steuerbewerteter Depotauszug" (https://www.ag.ch/de/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern-startseite/alles-zu-steuern?dc=e22c5f9c-d44e-447a-afb2-69f3f7d01148_de).
- Receipts can be photographed and uploaded with the free Snap.Share app (https://www.ag.ch/de/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuererklaerung-einreichen/etax-aargau/etax-aargau-hilfe-anleitungen).
- No mention of a 2D barcode on eTAX AARGAU output was found; the EasyTax 2024 notes mention barcodes on printouts (https://www.ag.ch/de/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuererklaerung-einreichen/easytax).

- 2026: the insurance and savings-interest flat rate is indexed to the mean health premium (https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/steuerthemen-natuerliche-personen/2025-09-24-bericht-anpassung-versicherungsabzug-an-praemienentwicklung.pdf).
- 2026: the deductions overview has its own 2026 column; the commuting per-km rate differs from 2025 (https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/steuerthemen-natuerliche-personen/bersicht-abz-ge-np-12-2025.pdf).
- 2026: StGV (https://gesetzessammlungen.ag.ch/api/de/versions/3915/pdf_file), QStV-AG (https://gesetzessammlungen.ag.ch/api/de/versions/3911/pdf_file) and the Progressionsverordnung 2025 (https://gesetzessammlungen.ag.ch/api/de/versions/3895/pdf_file) each have a version in force from 1 January 2026.
- Ahead: a Steuergesetz revision ("Steuergesetzrevision 2027") changing the income tariff goes to a cantonal vote on 29 November 2026; the bulletin also says the canton must introduce individual taxation by 2032 (https://www.ag.ch/de/medien/medienmitteilungen?mm=bulletin-zur-regierungsratssitzung-0d27de9b-e467-4b48-927b-36f30974225a_de).

## Tariffs, multipliers and deductions (pointers to documents)
- Mechanism: the tariffs give the einfache Steuer (simple tax) on income and on wealth; it is multiplied by the cantonal Steuerfuss, the communal Steuerfuss and, for church members, the church Steuerfuss. The guide's Anhang II has a worked example (https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/steuererklaerung/wegleitung-zur-steuererkl-rung-2025-kanton-aargau.pdf).
- The cantonal Steuerfuss is ordentliche Kantonssteuer plus Kantonssteuerzuschlag (and a Finanzausgleich supplement in some years), listed per year (https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/bezahlen-der-steuern-natuerliche-personen/steuerfuesse-zinssaetze-np-2026.pdf).
- Couples: Tarif B applies the rate for half the taxable income to the whole income (full splitting), also for single parents living with their children; Tarif A for everyone else (StG § 43 Abs. 2) (https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/steuerberechnung-tarife-natuerliche-personen/einkommenssteuertarif-2025.pdf).
- Income brackets are indexed by a Progressionsverordnung (StG § 57); for 2025 and 2026 read § 1 of the Progressionsverordnung 2025 and the 2025 tariff tables (https://gesetzessammlungen.ag.ch/api/de/versions/3895/pdf_file).
- Wealth tariff: § 55 StG and the 2025 tariff tables (https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/steuerberechnung-tarife-natuerliche-personen/verm-genssteuertarif-2025.pdf).
- Communal and church Steuerfüsse for every commune, 2025 (https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/steuerberechnung-tarife-natuerliche-personen/gemeinde-und-kirchensteuerf-sse-2025-v6.pdf) and 2026 (https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/steuerberechnung-tarife-natuerliche-personen/gemeinde-und-kirchensteuerf-sse-2026-v5.pdf).
- Pension capital payments are taxed separately at a fraction of the tariff with a minimum rate (StG § 45) (https://gesetzessammlungen.ag.ch/api/de/versions/4018/pdf_file).
- A separate Feuerwehrpflicht-Ersatzabgabe on taxable income applies to people of service age who do not serve; rules in Anhang III of the guide (https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/steuererklaerung/wegleitung-zur-steuererkl-rung-2025-kanton-aargau.pdf).
- All cantonal deductions with 2026 and 2025 columns (https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/steuerthemen-natuerliche-personen/bersicht-abz-ge-np-12-2025.pdf).
- Employee professional costs: the federal flat rates apply unless the StGV says otherwise (§ 12); commuting by private car gets the federal per-km rate up to a mileage threshold and a lower rate above it (§ 13) (https://gesetzessammlungen.ag.ch/api/de/versions/3915/pdf_file).
- Home office, meals, weekly stay and side-income rules: guide section 10 (https://www.ag.ch/de/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuererklaerung-einreichen/wegleitung-zur-steuererklaerung/abzuege/10-berufskosten-bei-unselbststaendiger-taetigkeit).
- Childcare (https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/merkblaetter-zu-steuern-natuerliche-personen/kinderbetreuungskosten.pdf), training (https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/merkblaetter-zu-steuern-natuerliche-personen/aus-weiterbildungs-und-umschulungskosten-2026.pdf), donations list (https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/merkblaetter-zu-steuern-natuerliche-personen/zuwendungen-spenden-an-institutionen-stp-2025.pdf), medical costs (https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/merkblaetter-zu-steuern-natuerliche-personen/2021-mb-krankheitskosten.pdf).
- An extra social deduction for low incomes (Zusätzlicher Sozialabzug für tiefe Einkommen) is guide section 24 (https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/steuererklaerung/wegleitung-zur-steuererkl-rung-2025-kanton-aargau.pdf).
- Official online tax calculator and all tariff files by year (https://www.ag.ch/de/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuern-berechnen/steuerberechnungen-neu).

## Open points for the rules (packages/rules-ag, 2026-10-14)

Found while reading the downloaded documents for the cantonal, communal and church income and wealth tax of 2025 and 2026. Each cites the document id and page ("p." is the PDF's page; the Aargau documents print the same numbers); the numbers live in the documents.

- How the tariffs work, checked: the einfache Einkommenssteuer is the tariff rounded down to the franc, and Tarif B ("der Steuersatz des halben steuerbaren Einkommens") is exactly twice the tax on half the income, rounded down (ag-2025-tariff-einkommenssteuer p. 1 and 2, 2., 3. and 5.); scripts/ag-tariff.mjs --check reproduces all 3,701 rows of each Tarif (p. 3 to 70) and all 4,200 wealth rows (ag-2025-tariff-vermoegenssteuer p. 3 to 37), and the steps printed on p. 1 of each.
- Small difference, the rate column: the tariff says the rates are derived from the tax amounts rounded down to the franc ("Steuersätze werden daraus abgeleitet", ag-2025-tariff-einkommenssteuer p. 2, 5. Rundung), but the tables derive them from the tax before rounding (Tarif A at 51,400: 2,496 printed with 4.8570 %, which is 2,496.50 / 51,400; p. 12). Only the rates differ, and rules-ag does not use them (they matter for partial tax liability and capital payments, which it does not compute).
- 2026 tariffs and child deductions: StG § 57 Abs. 5 says the wealth tariff and the child deductions decided on 3 December 2024 are first adjusted for inflation for 2026 (ag-na-law-steuergesetz p. 34), but the Progressionsverordnung's version of 1 January 2026 repeals its own wealth tariff and child deductions (§ 2, § 3 Abs. 1 lit. a to c) without new amounts and keeps the income steps of § 1 (ag-2026-tariff-progressionsverordnung p. 1 to 3); the canton's index still lists the 2025 tariffs as the newest, "gültig ab 2025" (ag-na-tariff-steuern-berechnen), and the table of deductions prints the same child deductions for 2026 as for 2025 (ag-2026-deductions-uebersicht-abzuege p. 2). rules-ag uses those for 2026. A human should confirm that no 2026 adjustment was published elsewhere.
- Stale footnote: StG § 43's footnote 18 points to the Progressionsverordnung 2024 (SAR 651.134) (ag-na-law-steuergesetz p. 28); the ordinance in force is the Progressionsverordnung 2025 (SAR 651.135).
- Not in the documents: how the cantonal, communal and church taxes are rounded. The guide's worked example prints 2,146.70 for 1,934 x 111 % (2,146.74) and 1,818.00 for 1,934 x 94 % (1,817.96) (ag-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 45, Anhang II): only rounding to the nearest 10 centimes gives both. rules-ag rounds each tax to 10 centimes and says so (NOTE.rounding). One tax bill or the official calculator would settle it.
- Conflict, the medical costs' own share: the law takes 5 % of the income less the deductions of §§ 35 to 40 (§ 40 Abs. 1 lit. i, ag-na-law-steuergesetz p. 25), which leaves out donations (§ 40a, whose own cap is on the income less §§ 35 to 40, p. 27); the guide computes it as "Nettoeinkommen gemäss Ziffer 20 x 5 : 95" (ag-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 31, 18.), and its form lists donations (15.3) before the medical costs (17), without saying whether Ziffer 20 includes them. rules-ag follows the law (medical costs first, then donations on the income after them) and says so when both are given (NOTE.medicalOrder). How the Selbstbehalt is rounded is not stated either: rules-ag takes it to the franc.
- Conflict, driving to work: StGV § 13 Abs. 1 gives the federal rate per km for the first 15,000 km a year and a rate 20 centimes lower beyond (ag-2026-law-steuerverordnung p. 4); the 2025 guide gives CHF 0.70 per km without the lower rate (ag-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 22), and the table of deductions gives 0.70 (2025) and 0.75 (2026) per km (ag-2026-deductions-uebersicht-abzuege p. 1). rules-ag applies the ordinance's lower rate beyond 15,000 km for a car (only a car: § 13 names the "Privatauto"); the motorbike rate for 2026 is the federal one (StGV § 12).
- Not in the documents: how the 100 % ceiling on the marginal burden at the steps of the low-income deduction is applied (§ 43 Abs. 3 with § 42 Abs. 1bis, ag-na-law-steuergesetz p. 28 and 29): which taxes count and how the tax is cut. rules-ag does not compute a household whose cantonal, communal and church income tax would rise by more than its income from the top of the step below (fail closed, NOTE.lowIncomeStep).
- Interpretation, the maximum burden: § 56 Abs. 1 cuts the income and wealth taxes of canton, commune and church to 70 % of the Reineinkommen, "jedoch höchstens auf die Hälfte der geschuldeten Vermögenssteuern" (ag-na-law-steuergesetz p. 33). rules-ag reads this as a cut of at most half of the wealth taxes, adds back pension fund buy-ins to the Reineinkommen as the paragraph says, and shows one cut to the centime; the rounding and how the cut is shared between canton, commune and church are not stated (NOTE.maxBurdenRounding).
- Not in the documents: how the church tax of a family of several confessions is shared. § 154 Abs. 3 shares it by the number of family members of each confession (ag-na-law-steuergesetz p. 70) but does not say whether members without a church and children count. rules-ag computes the church tax for a single person or for spouses of the same church (children taken to belong to it, said in the result) and fails closed for spouses of different churches or only one in a church.
- Further parishes (Zusatzkirchen): the communal lists name up to five further parishes per commune with their own rates (ag-2025-communal-steuerfuesse and ag-2026-communal-steuerfuesse p. 9), but not which streets or localities belong to them. rules-ag asks for the parish when the household's church has one in its commune. The lists carry no BFS numbers: communes are identified by their name as printed.
- Shared custody: the guide gives the child deduction to the parent who mainly bears the child's upkeep and, as a rule, none to the parent who can deduct child support (ag-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 32, 19.1); Tarif B goes with a child living with the parent who has the deduction (p. 44, Anhang I). The childcare Merkblatt (Stand 1 January 2026) caps each parent at half the per-child maximum for unmarried parents with joint authority or alternating custody (ag-2026-deductions-kinderbetreuungskosten p. 7, 4.7); the 2025 guide does not say it (p. 27, 15.0), and rules-ag applies it in 2025 too, as the cap it halves applies since 2025. A married couple with a child shared with a parent outside the household is not computed.
- Not in the documents: whether professional costs above the net salary are cut to it. rules-ag fails closed then (NOTE.costsAboveSalary). The flat amounts are for a full year (childcare: the guide cuts the cap for part of a year, p. 27; rules-ag takes a full year and says so).
- Conflict, the federal training cap: the training Merkblatt says the yearly cap is 18,000 for both the cantonal and the federal tax (ag-2026-deductions-weiterbildungskosten p. 7, 3.6); the 2025 guide gives 13,000 for the federal tax (ag-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 43). Only the federal side differs; rules-ag computes the cantonal tax.
- Social deductions the engine does not compute: the support, invalidity and care deductions (§ 42 Abs. 1 lit. b to d) print 2,400 and 3,000 in the StG (ag-na-law-steuergesetz p. 27 and 28) while the Progressionsverordnung and the guide give 2,600, 3,200 and 3,200 (ag-2026-tariff-progressionsverordnung p. 2, § 3 lit. d to f; ag-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 32): the ordinance adjusts them for inflation (§ 57 Abs. 1). The household input has no field for them yet.
- Form codes: the main form and its sheets (101.02 Berufskosten / Hilfsblatt für weitere Abzüge, 101.04 Schuldenverzeichnis / Renten- u. Ersatzeinkünfte, 101.05 Wertschriften- und Guthabenverzeichnis, 101.06 Krankheits- u. Unfallkosten, 101.07 Liegenschaftenverzeichnis: ag-2025-other-formularbestellung) are not published. rules-ag codes each line by the guide's section number (1.1 to 34), but the guide's own cross-references don't always match its sections ("Ziffer 22.1" for the child deduction of section 19.1, ag-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 25): the filing step must check the codes against the form or eTAX AARGAU.
- Not computed: the Feuerwehrpflicht-Ersatzabgabe (ag-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 46, Anhang III: the years of service count, in a way the guide does not detail), membership fees to professional associations (p. 24, 10.5), capital payments (§ 45), property, self-employment.
