# Zug (ZG) sources: notes for natural persons, tax years 2025 and 2026

Verified 2026-10-13. Each fact is followed by its official source. The documents are the source of truth; never copy a number from this file.

## Filing channels (tax year 2025)

- The canton's channel is **eTax.zug**, a web application run by the Steuerverwaltung. From tax period 2025 it is online only, in the browser, with no download (https://zg.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/etax).
- It replaced the downloadable eTax.zug software used for over 20 years. Returns for 2022 to 2024 still use the old download solution (https://zg.ch/de/news/2026/January/steuererklaerung-2025-ab-februar-2026-komplett-online-ausfuellen).
- Login to eTax.zug is **AGOV** only (AGOV Access App or a FIDO security key). AGOV is currently used in Zug only for eTax.zug (https://zg.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/etax/etax-registrieren).
- The legal basis is VES (BGS 632.12), in force 1 January 2026: eTax.zug lets you file legally online, authentication runs through AGOV, and paper filing stays allowed (https://bgs.zg.ch/api/de/versions/2686/pdf_file).
- Each year the Steuerverwaltung sends access data on paper: Pers ID, Geschäftsfallnummer (case number) and Sicherheitscode (security code). Couples taxed jointly get shared access data (VES § 2, https://bgs.zg.ch/api/de/versions/2686/pdf_file).
- Online filing needs no signature. For couples, the shared access code is enough, by decision of the Regierungsrat (webinar slide 33, https://cdn.zg.ch/dam/jcr:ce9d105c-06a1-4feb-bfc9-95fa05cb0c38/Webinar-Pr%C3%A4sentation-eTax-2026.pdf).
- Attachments are uploaded as PDF or image files, or photographed with the SNAP.SHARE app. After an online submission, send no paper documents (https://zg.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/etax/etax-einreichen).
- There are two paper routes. You can fill in eTax.zug and switch the submission type to "Ausdrucken und per Post einreichen", or fill in the paper forms by hand. Both go by post with copies of the supporting documents (https://zg.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/etax/etax-einreichen).
- For a printed eTax.zug return, send the full printout, the original form K (Hauptformular) and the signed Barcodeblatt. On paper, couples both sign form K and the WV form (Wegleitung 2025 pp. 3 and 12, https://cdn.zg.ch/dam/jcr:17c572af-908f-43a5-96b0-0fd25983f924/Wegleitung_2025_StKtZug_A4_20251119.pdf).
- You cannot mix the two routes: either everything goes online or everything goes on paper (https://cdn.zg.ch/dam/jcr:780f86fe-cfdf-48cb-8b1e-3b609aba828c/Webinar-Fragen-Antworten-2026.pdf).
- The eTax.zug interface is in German only. The English material is the "Instructions in English" PDF (https://cdn.zg.ch/dam/jcr:780f86fe-cfdf-48cb-8b1e-3b609aba828c/Webinar-Fragen-Antworten-2026.pdf, https://zg.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/etax/english).
- Other online services (account statement, provisional bill, instalments, extension) are on eSteuern Zug at steuern.zg.ch/private. Some of them need a ZUGLogin account (https://zg.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/natuerliche-personen/steuern-natuerliche-personen-online-verwalten).
- The 2025 return forms went out to individuals from 24 February 2026 (https://zg.ch/de/news/2026/January/steuererklaerung-2025-ab-februar-2026-komplett-online-ausfuellen).
- eTax.zug data is stored encrypted on a server run by the canton for five years. During the 120 hour correction window the Steuerverwaltung cannot see it (VES §§ 6 and 9, https://bgs.zg.ch/api/de/versions/2686/pdf_file).

## Third-party software

- To file electronically you must use eTax.zug or another declaration solution recognised by the Steuerverwaltung. The Steuerverwaltung says it lists recognised solutions on its website (VES § 3 Abs. 1, https://bgs.zg.ch/api/de/versions/2686/pdf_file).
- No such list was found on the pages read: eTax.zug, Treuhänder und Vertreter, Steuerrecht (https://zg.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/treuhaenderundvertreter). Ask the Steuerverwaltung before building any electronic transmission.
- The tax law allows filing "in anderer, von der kantonalen Steuerverwaltung autorisierten Form" and leaves the details of e-filing to the Regierungsrat (StG § 125 Abs. 2, https://bgs.zg.ch/api/de/versions/2956/pdf_file).
- Representatives work inside eTax.zug. The taxpayer shares the case (function «Teilen») or hands over the access data, and the representative needs their own AGOV account (VES § 8, https://bgs.zg.ch/api/de/versions/2686/pdf_file).
- eTax.zug cannot import returns made with Dr.Tax (https://cdn.zg.ch/dam/jcr:780f86fe-cfdf-48cb-8b1e-3b609aba828c/Webinar-Fragen-Antworten-2026.pdf).
- eTax.zug can import last year's eTax.zug data, local files from the old version, and "kompatibler Formate anderer Kantone" (VES § 5 Abs. 2, https://bgs.zg.ch/api/de/versions/2686/pdf_file).
- Vendor: technical support for eTax.zug is run for the canton by Ringler Informatik AG, and for AGOV by Callpoint AG (webinar slide 26, https://cdn.zg.ch/dam/jcr:ce9d105c-06a1-4feb-bfc9-95fa05cb0c38/Webinar-Pr%C3%A4sentation-eTax-2026.pdf).
- eTax.zug support uses the zg@etax.ch address and the help centre zg-support.etax.ch (https://zg.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/etax/etax-support).
- The canton says eTax.zug "baut auf einer standardisierten Lösung auf, die bereits erfolgreich in anderen Kantonen im Einsatz ist" (https://zg.ch/de/news/2026/January/steuererklaerung-2025-ab-februar-2026-komplett-online-ausfuellen).

## Deadlines

- The 2025 return is due by **30 April 2026**, filed electronically or with the Gemeindesteueramt (Wegleitung 2025 p. 9, https://cdn.zg.ch/dam/jcr:17c572af-908f-43a5-96b0-0fd25983f924/Wegleitung_2025_StKtZug_A4_20251119.pdf; https://zg.ch/de/news/2026/February/etax-zug-2025-neue-deklarationsloesung-fuer-natuerliche-personen-ist-online).
- Extensions are requested online with your Pers ID, or in writing to Steuerverwaltung, Postfach, 6301 Zug (https://zg.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/natuerliche-personen/fristerstreckung-steuererklaerung).
- Requests up to 31 December 2026 get a reply only if they are refused. No answer means the extension is granted, with no fee (Wegleitung 2025 pp. 9 and 10, https://cdn.zg.ch/dam/jcr:17c572af-908f-43a5-96b0-0fd25983f924/Wegleitung_2025_StKtZug_A4_20251119.pdf).
- Extensions past 31 December must be in writing with reasons, and the decision is always sent in writing. Each approval costs a processing fee of 35 francs, billed with the final tax bill (https://zg.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/natuerliche-personen/fristerstreckung-steuererklaerung).
- A late return is excused if military service, illness, absence abroad or another serious reason prevented filing, and you file within 30 days after the reason ends (StG § 125 Abs. 4, https://bgs.zg.ch/api/de/versions/2956/pdf_file).
- **Online timing trap:** submitted data stays correctable for 120 hours, then goes to the Steuerverwaltung, confirmed by a second email (VES § 6, https://bgs.zg.ch/api/de/versions/2686/pdf_file).
  - § 6 Abs. 4 says the deadline counts as met if the second email shows the forwarding happened on the last day of the deadline.
  - Read literally, an online return must be sent at least 5 days before the deadline. Confirm with the Steuerverwaltung.
- The eTax.zug pages describe the same 5 day correction window. After it, the return cannot be withdrawn or corrected (https://zg.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/etax/etax-einreichen).
- The sample form K printed in the Wegleitung says "innert 60 Tagen einzureichen", while the text sets 30 April. Treat 30 April (or the date on your own form) as the deadline (Wegleitung 2025, https://cdn.zg.ch/dam/jcr:17c572af-908f-43a5-96b0-0fd25983f924/Wegleitung_2025_StKtZug_A4_20251119.pdf).
- To have the provisional bill for the current year based on updated figures, report them by 31 May 2026. The provisional bill is due by 31 December 2026 (Wegleitung 2025 p. 10, same URL).
- An objection (Einsprache) against the assessment is due within 30 days, in writing. Email is not accepted (Wegleitung 2025 p. 46, same URL).

## Source tax (Quellensteuer, NOV: nachträgliche ordentliche Veranlagung, and rectification)

- The NOV request (form 250) is due by **31 March** of the following year. Spouses or registered partners both sign (https://cdn.zg.ch/dam/jcr:8c0b5334-bfc5-4d82-906f-27473b70777d/2026_form_250_NOV_Antrag_def.26.11.25.pdf).
- Swiss residents can request NOV only once. After that, NOV is mandatory until source taxation ends. People resident abroad (quasi-resident, Art. 99a DBG) must apply every year (same form).
- The 2025 and 2026 files of form 250 are byte-identical (https://zg.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/quellensteuer/quellensteuerpflichtige-person).
- Mandatory NOV cases, optional NOV, deadlines and the competent canton are in one table. Its thresholds are in the document (https://cdn.zg.ch/dam/jcr:ce209ab2-284a-4c1d-ad2a-bbaa70a0da8d/2026_info_250_NOV_Uebersicht_def.26.11.25.pdf).
- To correct the source tax itself, use the Antrag auf Neuberechnung der Quellensteuer (form 255). Grounds: wrong gross wage, wrong rate-determining income, or wrong tariff code (https://cdn.zg.ch/dam/jcr:1d78153a-7849-4c69-9093-8e80e9b2c600/2026_form_255_%20def.26.11.25.pdf).
  - It is due by 31 March of the year after the tax period.
  - This deadline cannot be extended (Verwirkungsfrist).
- People taxed at source who want a withholding tax refund must file the return forms with the Gruppe Quellensteuer. Exemptions depend on non-source income and wealth (Wegleitung 2025 p. 8, https://cdn.zg.ch/dam/jcr:17c572af-908f-43a5-96b0-0fd25983f924/Wegleitung_2025_StKtZug_A4_20251119.pdf).
- 2026 tariffs apply from 1 January 2026 and come as separate PDFs per code: AN, AY, BN, BY, CN, CY, HN, HY and G. N means without church tax, Y means with church tax (https://zg.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/quellensteuer/quellensteuertarife).
- The 2025 tariffs are on the publikationen-2025 sub-page (https://zg.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/quellensteuer/quellensteuertarife/publikationen-2025).
- The flat deductions built into the 2026 tariffs, per tariff code, are in one Merkblatt (https://cdn.zg.ch/dam/jcr:8aa4e09f-58c8-4a73-b2b7-38b24a228227/2026_merk_QST-Pauschalabz%C3%BCge-Tarife%20def.%209.12.25.pdf).

## e-tax statements (eCH-0196) and other imports

- eTax.zug accepts bank e-tax statements ("eSteuerauszug"). You can load several, one per bank or depot, and split one account between owners by percentage (https://cdn.zg.ch/dam/jcr:780f86fe-cfdf-48cb-8b1e-3b609aba828c/Webinar-Fragen-Antworten-2026.pdf).
- The pages and documents read do not name the eCH-0196 standard. The upload format is not specified, so it is unconfirmed whether the PDF barcode or the XML is read.
- The securities page recommends the bank's tax statement (Steuerauszug): carry the totals into one line of the Wertschriftenverzeichnis and attach the full statement (https://zg.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/verrechnungssteuer/wertschriftenkontendevisen).
  - Foreign values are converted with the ESTV Kursliste (ictax).
  - Crypto assets are declared at their 31 December balance.
- Paper returns printed from eTax.zug carry a Barcodeblatt, which must be signed (Wegleitung 2025 p. 12, https://cdn.zg.ch/dam/jcr:17c572af-908f-43a5-96b0-0fd25983f924/Wegleitung_2025_StKtZug_A4_20251119.pdf).
- When you choose a currency, eTax.zug looks up the ESTV rate list in the background (https://cdn.zg.ch/dam/jcr:780f86fe-cfdf-48cb-8b1e-3b609aba828c/Webinar-Fragen-Antworten-2026.pdf).
- A return already submitted through the old eTax.zug can be downloaded from steuererklaerung.zg.ch and imported (same FAQ).

## New for 2025 and 2026

- **Tax year 2025:** first year of the online-only eTax.zug with AGOV, plus the new VES ordinance (in force 1 January 2026) (https://bgs.zg.ch/api/de/versions/2686/pdf_file).
- From 11 May 2026 the oBeam app no longer works for periods 2022 to 2024 (https://zg.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/etax/perioden-2022-2024).
- The Wegleitung 2025 lists the main changes per 1 January 2025: taxation of pillar 3b life annuities, capital benefits, pillar 3a maximums, and the insurance, childcare, second-earner and social deductions. Pointer only; read the guide (https://cdn.zg.ch/dam/jcr:17c572af-908f-43a5-96b0-0fd25983f924/Wegleitung_2025_StKtZug_A4_20251119.pdf).
- **Tax year 2026:** voters accepted the «Mehrwert für alle» law change on 30 November 2025 (https://zg.ch/de/news/2025/November/steuergesetzaenderung-mehrwert-fuer-alle-angenommen). It brings:
  - a temporary cut of the cantonal Steuerfuss for 2026 to 2029;
  - higher deductions for health and other insurance premiums;
  - a pensioner deduction per person, with higher thresholds.
- The cut is fixed in StG § 2 Abs. 2b (https://bgs.zg.ch/api/de/versions/2956/pdf_file). The new deduction amounts are on the 2026 page of the Sozialabzüge PDF (https://cdn.zg.ch/dam/jcr:736ef31a-f3e3-48f3-ad5d-9870761a47c1/Sozialabz%C3%BCge%20ab%202001-2026.pdf).
- The 2026 Steuerfüsse table, dated January 2026, shows changes for several communes and church communities in 2026. Read the values in the PDF (https://cdn.zg.ch/dam/jcr:24fb8832-3383-4718-9a48-9461bc7501fc/Steuerf%C3%BCsse%202026_effektiv%2021.1.26.pdf).
- From tax year 2026, eTax.zug takes over last year's data automatically online (https://zg.ch/de/news/2026/January/steuererklaerung-2025-ab-februar-2026-komplett-online-ausfuellen).
- Already published for 2026: income and wealth tariffs, social deductions, communal multipliers, source tax tariffs and the law version in force from 1 January 2026. The 2026 Wegleitung is not out (see Gaps).

## Tariffs, multipliers and deductions (pointers to documents)

- **Einfache Steuer and Steuerfuss:** the tariffs give the "einfache Steuer" at a 100 percent Steuerfuss (StG § 2, https://bgs.zg.ch/api/de/versions/2956/pdf_file).
  - Canton, Einwohnergemeinde, Kirchgemeinde and in some communes the Bürgergemeinde each apply their own Steuerfuss as a percentage of it.
  - The multipliers for 2023 to 2026 are in one table (https://cdn.zg.ch/dam/jcr:24fb8832-3383-4718-9a48-9461bc7501fc/Steuerf%C3%BCsse%202026_effektiv%2021.1.26.pdf).
- **Communal rebates:** footnotes in the Steuerfüsse table give each rebate either as a percent of the Steuerfuss or as percentage points of the Einheitsansatz. The table cells already print the effective multiplier (same PDF).
- **Two income tariffs, no splitting divisor:** the Grundtarif is for single filers (StG § 35 Abs. 1). The Mehrpersonentarif, with wider brackets, is for married couples and for single parents who live with their children and mainly support them (StG § 35 Abs. 2) (https://bgs.zg.ch/api/de/versions/2956/pdf_file).
- **Indexation:** the Steuerverwaltung indexes the brackets and several deductions every year to the consumer price index at 30 June before the period (StG § 45). The amounts printed in StG §§ 35 and 44 are the legal base.
- Use the yearly tariff PDFs for the year's brackets:
  - Grundtarif: https://cdn.zg.ch/dam/jcr:96c7eef4-eb2f-4f8a-a4c4-dad209249598/Grundtarif%202001%20bis%202026.pdf
  - Mehrpersonentarif: https://cdn.zg.ch/dam/jcr:88fe7bb7-e1c0-4cf5-bf67-b2ccc633caae/Mehrpersonentarif%202001%20bis%202026.pdf
- **Wealth tax:** the wealth tariff is in StG § 44 and is shown, indexed, in the same two PDFs.
- **Capital benefits from pensions:** taxed separately at a fraction of the tariff (StG § 37). The yearly threshold is in the Sozialabzüge PDF (https://cdn.zg.ch/dam/jcr:736ef31a-f3e3-48f3-ad5d-9870761a47c1/Sozialabz%C3%BCge%20ab%202001-2026.pdf).
- **Deduction amounts per year** are on one page per year of the Sozialabzüge PDF (same URL): personal, child, pensioner, support, rent, self-care, insurance, second-earner, childcare and wealth deductions.
- **Professional costs:** the federal ordinance annex rates (SR 642.118.1) also apply to cantonal and communal tax (Steuerbuch 16.1, https://wissen.zg.ch/steuerbuch/erlaeuterung-zu-a7-25-berufskosten-bei-selbstaendiger-erwerbstaetigkeit/grundsaetzliches-und-hoehe-der-abzuege).
- **Commuting:** the cap differs between cantonal and federal tax (Steuerbuch 16.2.1, https://wissen.zg.ch/steuerbuch/erlaeuterung-zu-a7-25-berufskosten-bei-selbstaendiger-erwerbstaetigkeit/fahrkosten-zum-arbeitsort/grundsaetzliches-zu-fahrkosten-zum-arbeitsort).
- **Childcare:** the cantonal childcare deduction (Kinderdrittbetreuungskostenabzug, § 30 Bst. l StG) is explained in Steuerbuch 20.10.1.2 (https://wissen.zg.ch/steuerbuch/erlaeuterung-zu-a7-30-allgemeine-abzuege-unabhaengig-von-der-einkommenshoehe/kinderdrittbetreuungskosten/allgemeines/kantonssteuer-ab-steuerperiode-2024).
  - Zug also has a deduction for children cared for at home (Eigenbetreuungskostenabzug, § 33 Abs. 2 StG), explained in Steuerbuch 22.8 (https://wissen.zg.ch/steuerbuch/erlaeuterung-zu-a733-sozialabzuege/fremdbetreuungskostenabzug).
  - You cannot claim both deductions.
- **Medical costs** (§ 31 Bst. a StG): Steuerbuch 21.1 (https://wissen.zg.ch/steuerbuch/erlaeuterung-zu-a7-31-allgemeine-abzuege-abhaengig-von-der-einkommenshoehe/abzug-von-krankheits-und-unfallkosten).
- **Pillar 3a** follows federal BVV3. **Donations** are in StG § 31. Both are explained in Steuerbuch chapters 20.5 and 21.2 (https://wissen.zg.ch/steuerbuch).
- **Forms (2025):** form codes are K (Hauptformular), WV (securities), BA (Berufsauslagen), VZ (Versicherungsprämien), KI (children), SV (debts and other deductions), LVZ (property summary) and GB (self-employed) (https://cdn.zg.ch/dam/jcr:3cf9b0e6-3706-4446-a7ea-06c943df00c7/TaxReturn-English-2025.pdf, https://cdn.zg.ch/dam/jcr:17c572af-908f-43a5-96b0-0fd25983f924/Wegleitung_2025_StKtZug_A4_20251119.pdf).
- **Federal direct tax:** the Wegleitung refers to the ESTV for DBST tariffs (Wegleitung 2025 p. 47, same URL).
