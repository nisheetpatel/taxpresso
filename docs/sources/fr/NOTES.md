# Fribourg (FR), natural persons: verified notes (checked 2026-10-13)

Verified 2026-10-13, second pass (gap check) the same day. Each fact is followed by its official source. The documents are the source of truth; never copy a number from this file.

## Filing channels, tax year 2025
- FriTax is the SCC's free desktop program, installed on the computer (Windows 64-bit 10 or later, macOS 10.15 or later, Ubuntu with a graphical interface). Installers for 2019 to 2025 are linked from fr.ch but hosted by the vendor at webupdate.ringler.ch. (https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/fritax/telecharger-fritax)
- Electronic route: FriTax sends the return encrypted to the SCC, and the process replaces the handwritten signature. Paper route: print, sign all printed pages, insert them in the original form, and post them with the barcode sheet and supporting documents to SCC, Case postale, 1701 Fribourg. (https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/fritax ; https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/fritax/fritax-faq)
- The guide lists three options: electronic via FriTax, plain paper with barcode sheet, or original forms completed by hand. (https://www.fr.ch/document/57721)
- No account login is described. Internet transmission uses a 6-character uppercase control code ("Code FriTax") printed on the A4 folder "Déclaration d'impôt". (https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/fritax/fritax-faq)
- For internet transmission only the FriTax software may be used; each tax period has its own version. (https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/fritax/fritax-faq)
- The e-filing ordinance (RSF 631.15) is the legal basis: electronic filing must use FriTax downloaded from the SCC site (art. 2 al. 2), the personal FriTax code replaces the signature (art. 3), the return can be corrected within 72 hours of the receipt (art. 5), it counts as filed when the receipt arrives (art. 6), and the data wait encrypted on a server outside the State network for those 72 hours (art. 8). (https://bdlf.fr.ch/api/fr/versions/5292/pdf_file)
- FriTax is free and holds up to ten declarations. (https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/fritax)
- Electronic filing is needed for the SCC to speed up assessments requested for subsidies or scholarships. (https://www.fr.ch/document/57721)
- Supporting documents: FriTax determines which are needed; they must be kept and produced on request even when not sent, in principle for 10 years. (https://www.fr.ch/dfin/scc/actualites/frchfritax-ladresse-pour-faire-sa-declaration-dimpot-en-ligne ; https://www.fr.ch/document/57721 ; https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/fritax/fritax-faq)
- Correction: a resubmission is possible within 72 hours of the electronic acknowledgement; after that the return counts as filed. (https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/fritax/fritax-faq)

## Third-party software and representatives
- No checked official page covers third-party, fiduciary or professional submission, and no approved list is published. The FAQ only warns to download FriTax from the SCC site, since some sites bundle it with their own products. (https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/fritax/fritax-faq)
- Electronic filing by any program other than FriTax is not provided for: the e-filing ordinance names FriTax as the program to use (art. 2 al. 2). Other software can only lead to a paper return. (https://bdlf.fr.ch/api/fr/versions/5292/pdf_file)
- A representative receives assessments only if a written general power of attorney is attached to the return. (https://www.fr.ch/document/57721)
- The power-of-attorney PDF is linked (listed, not opened): https://www.fr.ch/document/492491 (https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/impot-des-personnes-physiques-themes-particuliers)

## Deadlines, tax year 2025
- Filing deadline: 31 March 2026, or the date printed on the return. (https://www.fr.ch/document/57721)
- Paid extension, no reason needed: 20 CHF via the QR bill on the return; the payment is the proof and no confirmation is sent. Reasoned extension: written request to the SCC, answered in writing, also 20 CHF. (https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/obtenir-un-delai-pour-le-depot-de-sa-declaration)
- No extension is granted after 15 December; up to four extensions in total. (https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/obtenir-un-delai-pour-le-depot-de-sa-declaration)
- Late filing: a reminder gives 10 days, then an ex officio assessment and a fine of up to 1,000 CHF. Electronic filing is technically possible until 31 December of the year after the tax period. (https://www.fr.ch/document/57721 ; https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/fritax/fritax-faq)

## Source tax (impôt à la source)
- The source-tax ordinance (RSF 631.32) is versioned per year: 2025 (https://bdlf.fr.ch/api/fr/versions/8085/pdf_file) and 2026 (https://bdlf.fr.ch/api/fr/versions/8651/pdf_file). The older entry fr-na-ordonnance-impot-source-631-32 is the 2022 version.
- TOU applies automatically above the gross employment income threshold in art. 11, until source liability ends; for married couples the test is per spouse. (https://bdlf.fr.ch/api/fr/versions/8651/pdf_file)
- TOU on request: the form goes to the SCC by 31 March of the following year, and once filed it cannot be withdrawn (art. 12). Quasi-residents: written request by the same deadline, also irrevocable (art. 16). The 2026 version keeps these rules. (https://bdlf.fr.ch/api/fr/versions/8651/pdf_file)
- Church tax withheld at source can be refunded to people outside the recognised churches on a form filed by 31 March of the following year (art. 4). (https://bdlf.fr.ch/api/fr/versions/8651/pdf_file)
- The 2025 TOU form on fr.ch is titled for non-resident Swiss persons; no resident version was found. (https://www.fr.ch/document/542591)
- Rectification: the 2026 ordinance has no separate rectification article; the LICD articles it cites (73a, 73b) and art. 171 LICD were not checked. Source tax is collected despite contestations. (https://bdlf.fr.ch/api/fr/versions/8651/pdf_file)
- Tables A, B, C, G and H and the cantonal calculation bases are published for 2025 and 2026; "Autres barèmes dès 2025" covers the other tariffs. (https://www.fr.ch/impots/impot-a-la-source/baremes-et-calculs-de-limpot-a-la-source ; https://www.fr.ch/document/542216 ; https://www.fr.ch/document/574991 ; https://www.fr.ch/document/542596)
- The annual coefficient law also fixes the coefficient for the source tax. (https://bdlf.fr.ch/api/fr/versions/8359/pdf_file)
- The PIS taxpayer guide and the employer instructions are 2024 versions; no 2026 version was found. (https://www.fr.ch/document/431746 ; https://www.fr.ch/document/517411)

## Tariffs, communal rates and deductions
- Income tariff: title says valid from 2023 onward. Wealth tariff: from 2021 onward. Both are listed as "Barèmes actuels" and the SCC 2026 calculator still uses them, so they apply to 2025 and 2026. (https://www.fr.ch/document/491766 ; https://www.fr.ch/document/434631 ; https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/calculer-ses-impots ; https://www.fr.ch/document/583511)
- Annual coefficient: the tariffs are stated at 100 percent and the Grand Conseil sets a coefficient each year (LICD art. 2), separately for income tax, wealth tax and source tax. 2025: https://bdlf.fr.ch/api/fr/versions/8110/pdf_file ; 2026: https://bdlf.fr.ch/api/fr/versions/8359/pdf_file. The 2025 guide also states the 2025 coefficient. (https://www.fr.ch/document/57721)
- Consolidated LICD: the version in force since 1 January 2025 is current, with no later version listed on 13 October 2026, so it governs 2025 and 2026 (income tariff art. 37, wealth tariff art. 62, social deductions art. 36). (https://bdlf.fr.ch/api/fr/versions/8109/pdf_file ; index https://bdlf.fr.ch/api/fr/texts_of_law/631.1)
- Communal tax: each commune sets a coefficient in percent of the cantonal base tax, the same for income and wealth, within the limits of the communal tax law (LICo). (https://bdlf.fr.ch/api/fr/versions/8653/pdf_file)
- Communal coefficients 2025 for all communes: CSV export from the cantonal open data portal (dataset 18_03_coefficients, publisher Service des communes). (https://opendata.fr.ch/api/explore/v2.1/catalog/datasets/18_03_coefficients/exports/csv?select=annee,commune,commune_id,district,coefficients,valeur,unite&refine=annee:2025&delimiter=%3B)
- The SCom interactive report covers coefficients by year from 2024 but works only through a POST form, so it cannot be fetched as a file. (https://www.fr.ch/diaf/scom/statistiques-financieres-des-collectivites-perequation)
- Parish (church) coefficient PDFs exist for 2025 and 2026; they are not communal rates. (https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/coefficients-communaux-et-paroisses)
- Added 2026-10-14: the 2025 parish PDFs (fr-2025-parish-coefficients-catholic, https://www.fr.ch/document/570376 ; fr-2025-parish-coefficients-reformed, https://www.fr.ch/document/570381), per commune and parish, income and wealth coefficients in percent of the cantonal base tax, no federal commune numbers; and the open data exports of the parish datasets for 2026 with commune_id (fr-2026-parish-coefficients-catholic-opendata, fr-2026-parish-coefficients-reformed-opendata; datasets 18_03_coefficients_impots_catholiques and 18_03_coefficients_impots_reformes, which hold 2026 only). The 2026 Catholic export gives the same coefficients as the SCC's 2026 PDF (https://www.fr.ch/document/602756), with names spelled differently ("St-" and "Saint-", "Fribourg / Freiburg" and "Fribourg"). (https://opendata.fr.ch/explore/dataset/18_03_coefficients_impots_catholiques/ ; https://opendata.fr.ch/explore/dataset/18_03_coefficients_impots_reformes/)
- Deductions: the 2025 guide explains each deduction code (professional expenses, insurance, pillar 3a, childcare and others). (https://www.fr.ch/document/57721)
- Employee professional expenses: DFIN ordinance with flat rates for travel, meals, stays away from home, other costs, telework and side activities; version in force since 1 January 2023. (https://bdlf.fr.ch/api/fr/versions/7574/pdf_file)
- Health and accident insurance premiums: flat deductible amounts per category, ordinance in force since 1 January 2021. (https://bdlf.fr.ch/api/fr/versions/6598/pdf_file)
- Unusual mechanic: the child deduction depends on net income (LICD art. 36 al. 1 let. a and b); the SCC calculators show the income limit and steps for 2025 and 2026. (https://www.fr.ch/document/520166 ; https://www.fr.ch/document/583526)
- Calculator spreadsheets (XLSX, XLSM, XLS) for tax years 1997 to 2026 are listed; the 2025 and 2026 tax calculators are XLSM files, a format the manifest does not accept, so they are not entries. (https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/calculer-ses-impots ; https://www.fr.ch/document/546616 ; https://www.fr.ch/document/583511)

## Open points for the rules (packages/rules-fr, 2026-10-14)
Found while reading the downloaded documents for the cantonal, communal and church income and wealth tax of 2025 and 2026. Each cites the document id and page ("p." is the PDF's page; the 2025 guide prints one less); the numbers live in the documents.
- Checked, income scale: LICD art. 37 al. 1 gives each class's rates at both ends and leaves the steps to the SCC's detailed tariff (fr-2025-law-licd-consolidee p. 27 and 28). A fixed step per CHF 100 inside each class gives every rate of the detailed tariff, all 4,091 rows and both columns (fr-2025-bareme-revenu and fr-2026-bareme-revenu, the same file, p. 1 to 106); the second column is the rate of half the income in whole CHF 100 with the minimum rate kept (art. 37 al. 3, p. 28). scripts/fr-tariff.mjs --check repeats the comparison.
- Conflict within the tariff, rounding of exact halves: the tax printed is income x rate to the nearest 5 centimes, except that 56 figures (rows and columns) fall exactly on a half, and the table rounds 37 of them up and 19 down (for example CHF 52,500 single, p. 13; CHF 55,000 both columns, p. 14). The guide's extract prints the same figures (fr-2025-guide-instructions-generales p. 45 and 46: 55,000, 75,000). packages/rules-fr follows the table on those 19 rows (`roundedDown`); the SCC's assessment may differ by 5 centimes there. Ask the SCC which rounding the assessment uses.
- Not in the documents, rounding of the cantonal (96 %), communal and church taxes: the guide says only that they are percentages of the base tax (p. 45 and 46). packages/rules-fr rounds each line to the nearest 5 centimes and says so in `unverifiedRules`. The SCC's tax calculators (XLSM, not in the library, https://www.fr.ch/document/583511) do the same, and call themselves approximate.
- 2026: no communal coefficients (see Gaps). Every other 2026 value is set by a text in force in 2026: the LICD version from 2025 (current on the BDLF), the two ordinances, the 2026 coefficient law, the tariffs listed as current, and the SCC's 2026 child deduction calculator and source-tax bases (fr-2026-deductions-enfants-calculette, fr-2026-bases-calcul-cantonales p. 1), which print the same amounts. The 2026 guide is not published.
- Conflict, alternating custody (garde alternée): the law splits the child deduction in proportion when several taxpayers bear the child's upkeep (fr-2025-law-licd-consolidee art. 36 al. 3, p. 27), without saying in what proportion; the SCC's source-tax documents give the child (child deductions, single-parent tariff) to the parent who receives child support, none to the one who pays it, and without child support the single-parent tariff to the parent with the higher income (fr-2024-pis-guide p. 7, question 18; fr-2024-instructions-employeurs-source p. 11 and 12, ch. 4.3.4; 2024 versions, no later found). packages/rules-fr applies the source-tax practice with child support (received: the child counts in full; paid: not at all, the support deducted) and says so in `unverifiedRules`; without child support it does not compute the tax. Ask the SCC how the ordinary assessment shares the child deduction and the reduced rate.
- Not in the documents, the modest income deduction (code 7.110) for a married couple where one spouse receives AVS/AI benefits and the other does not: art. 36 al. 2 (p. 26 and 27) and the guide's tables (p. 37 and 38) speak of "tout bénéficiaire de prestations AVS/AI". packages/rules-fr computes both and does not compute the tax when they differ.
- Not in the documents, church tax for spouses of different confessions (or one without a church): the guide gives the parish coefficients only (p. 46). packages/rules-fr does not compute the tax then.
- Not in the documents, the flat professional costs (code 2.130) when the net salary is under the CHF 2,000 minimum: the ordinance and the guide give the minimum without saying it stops at the salary (fr-na-deductions-frais-professionnels art. 6 al. 1, p. 4; guide p. 13), while the side-job flat does (art. 9; guide p. 13). packages/rules-fr does not compute the tax then.
- Interpretation, political parties (code 4.410) and training (4.420): the law and the guide give one amount each (art. 34 al. 1 let. i and j, p. 24; guide p. 33) without saying whether spouses have one or two. packages/rules-fr applies the parties' amount once per couple and the training amount per spouse, and says so when it matters.
- Interpretation, health insurance (code 4.110): a flat amount per insured person by category, whatever the premiums paid, less the premium subsidy (fr-na-deductions-primes-assurance art. 1, p. 1 and 2; guide p. 24). Private accident and loss-of-earnings premiums are either in that flat amount or under code 4.120 (guide p. 24 names daily-allowance insurance there); the canton interface refuses `accidentPremiums` rather than guess.
- Interpretation, the young adult category of the flat health amount is "de 18 ans révolus à 25 ans" in training and at the taxpayer's charge; Geneva's age band "young" in the shared household input is 19 to 25. The wiring must give Fribourg's band.
- Not in the guide: which of codes 1.510 to 1.530 takes which loss-of-earnings allowance (guide p. 10). packages/rules-fr puts them all on 1.510; the paper return form is not in the library (Gaps).
- Not covered by packages/rules-fr: a move between Fribourg communes during the year (the communal tax is shared by time, fr-na-law-lico art. 9 al. 2, p. 3; the result assumes the whole year in one commune), unlisted Swiss shares (reduced wealth tax rate, art. 62 al. 3, p. 37; code 3.250), property and rental values (annex 04), self-employment and farming, capital benefits taxed separately (art. 39), lottery winnings and stakes, weekly stays away from home, the deductions for needy dependents (6.120), taxpayers in training (6.130), wheelchair users and orphans (6.140) and home care (6.145), disability costs (4.370), a dependent child over 25, a married couple with a child in alternating custody from another relationship, part-year liability, and the federal tax for Fribourg's return (`ifd: null`). Fribourg has no communal personal tax for individuals in the LICo (art. 6 to 8 repealed).
- Commune list: the 2025 open data export lists 121 communes with federal numbers; the 2026 parish exports list 119, with Fétigny-Ménières (2056) instead of Fétigny and Ménières, and without Ulmiz. No document of the library says where Ulmiz went.

## Wiring Fribourg in the app (2026-10-14)
Found while giving rules-fr its federal tax and wiring Fribourg as a preview. Each cites the document id and page ("p." is the PDF's page); the numbers live in the documents.
- Federal tax: there is no federal return. "Le/la contribuable n’a pas de déclaration à remplir pour l’impôt fédéral direct. L’autorité détermine le revenu soumis à cet impôt en tenant compte des différences mentionnées ci-après à titre purement informatif" (fr-2025-guide-instructions-generales p. 47, chapter XI); the differences are listed code by code on p. 47 to 49: 2.110, 2.510, 3.120 (old pensions), 3.210 and 4.320 (lottery), 3.310, 4.110 with 4.150, 4.160, 4.380, 4.410, 4.420, 6.110, 6.120, 6.150, the reduction of the tax per child, capital payments. packages/rules-fr (src/ifd.ts) keeps the return's lines with their cantonal amounts and gives the listed ones the federal amounts of packages/rules-ch for the tax year, each on its code. The medical costs (5.110) and donations (5.120) keep their rule and take the federal net income as base. The modest income deduction (7.110) is cantonal only. 3.120's old pensions, lottery, property, 4.160 and 6.120 are not covered, as for the cantonal tax.
- Insurance (4.110 and 4.150 for the federal tax): the chapter says the deduction is "les primes et cotisations d’assurances mentionnées dans la déclaration, ainsi que les intérêts de capitaux d’épargne indiqués dans l’annexe 01" (p. 48), up to the federal maximum. The return mentions no health premium paid: 4.110 is a flat amount per insured person less the subsidy (p. 24). rules-fr takes the 4.110 flat amount, the 4.120 premiums and the savings interest given, up to the federal maximum, so the premiums paid don't matter and no attestation of them is asked. The flat amount is above every federal maximum unless a subsidy brings it under; pinned in packages/rules-fr/test/federal.test.ts. Ask the SCC to confirm the flat amount is what the assessment counts for the federal tax.
- Where that gives another federal tax than Geneva's form for the same household (pinned in the same test; a seeded test of 2,000 households of 2025 is equal to Geneva's, Zurich's and Lucerne's otherwise, and of 2,000 of 2026, through the federal column alone, to Geneva's): the insurance above; daily allowances are not in the base of the 3 % flat for other professional costs (2.130 is the salary's, fr-na-deductions-frais-professionnels art. 6 al. 1, p. 4; guide p. 13), as Zurich's column and not Geneva's form; a side job's flat (2.140, guide p. 13) kept for the federal tax, as the chapter doesn't list it, which the 2025 federal circular doesn't give either (as Lucerne's 2025 overview); childcare for a child of 14 or more counts for neither tax (LIFD art. 33 al. 3; "par enfant", p. 48), where Geneva's engine adds it within the cap of the younger children.
- Conflict, the federal insurance maximum for married couples without 2nd pillar or 3a: the guide prints 5,500 (p. 48), the federal circular 5,550 for 2025 (ch-2025-ifd-frais-professionnels-progression p. 2). The circular is the federal authority and the guide calls its list "purement informatif": packages/rules-ch's circular amount is used. Every other federal amount the guide prints for 2025 equals the circular's (pinned in the same test). A human could tell the SCC.
- Tax year 2026: no communal coefficients (Gaps). rules-fr now computes the 2026 lines of the return all the same (the result's `form`), so the questions, documents and values to type work; the estimate, the final check and the plans fail closed and say so in English, French and German (NOTE.communes2026, which names scripts/fr-communes.mjs). The church tax and the federal tax wait with them. When a table is published: add it to the manifest, fetch it, extend scripts/fr-communes.mjs to read it (it reads the 2026 parish exports today) and regenerate src/2026/communes.ts; the extension's plans then compute (cantons.ts, FRIBOURG.plan.noTax lists 2026: remove it).
- Savings interest above the accounts' income: the deduction "ne peut en aucun cas dépasser les rendements figurant sous les codes 3.210, 3.220, 3.240 et 3.250" (p. 26). rules-fr capped it with an input error; with the answer of the questionnaire it now counts at most that income and says so.
- Limits for the final check: the cantonal rules record each cap, threshold, minimum and flat-or-actual choice; on the lines the federal tax takes as they are (2.120, 2.130, 2.140, 4.130, 4.140, 4.210) a limit is both taxes'; the others have each tax's own.
- Childcare given for the household (the invoices of a crèche): the limit is per child under 14 (LICD art. 34 al. 3, fr-2025-law-licd-consolidee p. 24; p. 32). It counts where it can be told apart per child: one such child, or a total within one child's limit. Otherwise the questionnaire asks the amount per child (fr.child_care), and the tax is not computed without it. Childcare for a child of 14 or more is not counted and the result says so.
- The children: the questionnaire asks each child's age band (under 14, 14 to 17, 18 to 25 in training, over 25): the flat health amount of 4.110 has a category for a child up to 18 and a young adult in training up to 25 (fr-na-deductions-primes-assurance art. 1 al. 1 and 2, p. 1 and 2), and childcare counts under 14. Over 25 is said not supported (rules-fr's NOTE.adultChild). Alternating custody without child support is said not supported (the documents differ, Open points above).
- The church tax: each adult's church (Roman Catholic, Reformed or none), and in the 15 communes of 2026 whose parishes of one church have different coefficients, the parish (fr.parish, offered per commune and church; the options are rules-fr's parishOptions(2026)). Spouses of different churches stay not computed (Open points).
- The commune: by its federal number, from the 2026 parish exports (119 communes). A move between Fribourg communes is said not supported (LICo art. 9 al. 2, fr-na-law-lico p. 3); a move from another canton is supported: Fribourg taxes the whole year (guide p. 4) and the commune of arrival collects the communal tax (LICo art. 9 al. 3). The plans assume the city of Fribourg when no commune is answered.
- The return: not in the sources library (Gaps). packages/filing-fr maps it from the guide, code by code (p. 8 to 39), with the last page's rubrics A to E and annexes 01 to 06; both years are flagged provisional.
