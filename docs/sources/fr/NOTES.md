# Fribourg (FR), natural persons: verified notes (checked 2026-10-13)

Verified 2026-10-13, second pass (gap check) the same day. Each fact is followed by its official source. The documents are the source of truth; never copy a number from this file.

## Filing channels, tax year 2025
- FriTax is the SCC's free desktop program, installed on the computer (Windows 64-bit 10 or later, macOS 10.15 or later, Ubuntu with a graphical interface). Installers for 2019 to 2025 are linked from fr.ch but hosted by the vendor at webupdate.ringler.ch. (https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/fritax/telecharger-fritax)
- Electronic route: FriTax sends the return encrypted to the SCC, and the process replaces the handwritten signature. Paper route: print, sign all printed pages, insert them in the original form, and post them with the barcode sheet and supporting documents to SCC, Case postale, 1701 Fribourg. (https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/fritax ; https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/fritax/fritax-faq)
- The guide lists three options: electronic via FriTax, plain paper with barcode sheet, or original forms completed by hand. (https://www.fr.ch/document/57721)
- No account login is described. Internet transmission uses a 6-character uppercase control code ("Code FriTax") printed on the A4 folder "Déclaration d'impôt". (https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/fritax/fritax-faq)
- For internet transmission only the FriTax software may be used; each tax period has its own version. (https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/fritax/fritax-faq)
- The e-filing ordinance (RSF 631.15) is the legal basis: electronic filing must use FriTax downloaded from the SCC site (art. 2 al. 2), the personal FriTax code replaces the signature (art. 3), the return can be corrected within 72 hours of the receipt (art. 5), it counts as filed when the receipt arrives (art. 6), and the data wait encrypted on a server outside the State network for those 72 hours (art. 8). (https://bdlf.fr.ch/api/fr/versions/5292/pdf_file)
- FriTax is free and holds up to ten declarations. (https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/fritax)
- Electronic filing is needed for the SCC to speed up assessments requested for subsidies or scholarships. (https://www.fr.ch/document/57721)
- Supporting documents: FriTax determines which are needed; they must be kept and produced on request even when not sent, in principle for 10 years. (https://www.fr.ch/dfin/scc/actualites/frchfritax-ladresse-pour-faire-sa-declaration-dimpot-en-ligne ; https://www.fr.ch/document/57721 ; https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/fritax/fritax-faq)
- Correction: a resubmission is possible within 72 hours of the electronic acknowledgement; after that the return counts as filed. (https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/fritax/fritax-faq)

## Third-party software and representatives
- No checked official page covers third-party, fiduciary or professional submission, and no approved list is published. The FAQ only warns to download FriTax from the SCC site, since some sites bundle it with their own products. (https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/fritax/fritax-faq)
- Electronic filing by any program other than FriTax is not provided for: the e-filing ordinance names FriTax as the program to use (art. 2 al. 2). Other software can only lead to a paper return. (https://bdlf.fr.ch/api/fr/versions/5292/pdf_file)
- A representative receives assessments only if a written general power of attorney is attached to the return. (https://www.fr.ch/document/57721)
- The power-of-attorney PDF is linked (listed, not opened): https://www.fr.ch/document/492491 (https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/impot-des-personnes-physiques-themes-particuliers)

## Deadlines, tax year 2025
- Filing deadline: 31 March 2026, or the date printed on the return. (https://www.fr.ch/document/57721)
- Paid extension, no reason needed: 20 CHF via the QR bill on the return; the payment is the proof and no confirmation is sent. Reasoned extension: written request to the SCC, answered in writing, also 20 CHF. (https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/obtenir-un-delai-pour-le-depot-de-sa-declaration)
- No extension is granted after 15 December; up to four extensions in total. (https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/obtenir-un-delai-pour-le-depot-de-sa-declaration)
- Late filing: a reminder gives 10 days, then an ex officio assessment and a fine of up to 1,000 CHF. Electronic filing is technically possible until 31 December of the year after the tax period. (https://www.fr.ch/document/57721 ; https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/fritax/fritax-faq)

## Source tax (impôt à la source)
- The source-tax ordinance (RSF 631.32) is versioned per year: 2025 (https://bdlf.fr.ch/api/fr/versions/8085/pdf_file) and 2026 (https://bdlf.fr.ch/api/fr/versions/8651/pdf_file). The older entry fr-na-ordonnance-impot-source-631-32 is the 2022 version.
- TOU applies automatically above the gross employment income threshold in art. 11, until source liability ends; for married couples the test is per spouse. (https://bdlf.fr.ch/api/fr/versions/8651/pdf_file)
- TOU on request: the form goes to the SCC by 31 March of the following year, and once filed it cannot be withdrawn (art. 12). Quasi-residents: written request by the same deadline, also irrevocable (art. 16). The 2026 version keeps these rules. (https://bdlf.fr.ch/api/fr/versions/8651/pdf_file)
- Church tax withheld at source can be refunded to people outside the recognised churches on a form filed by 31 March of the following year (art. 4). (https://bdlf.fr.ch/api/fr/versions/8651/pdf_file)
- The 2025 TOU form on fr.ch is titled for non-resident Swiss persons; no resident version was found. (https://www.fr.ch/document/542591)
- Rectification: the 2026 ordinance has no separate rectification article; the LICD articles it cites (73a, 73b) and art. 171 LICD were not checked. Source tax is collected despite contestations. (https://bdlf.fr.ch/api/fr/versions/8651/pdf_file)
- Tables A, B, C, G and H and the cantonal calculation bases are published for 2025 and 2026; "Autres barèmes dès 2025" covers the other tariffs. (https://www.fr.ch/impots/impot-a-la-source/baremes-et-calculs-de-limpot-a-la-source ; https://www.fr.ch/document/542216 ; https://www.fr.ch/document/574991 ; https://www.fr.ch/document/542596)
- The annual coefficient law also fixes the coefficient for the source tax. (https://bdlf.fr.ch/api/fr/versions/8359/pdf_file)
- The PIS taxpayer guide and the employer instructions are 2024 versions; no 2026 version was found. (https://www.fr.ch/document/431746 ; https://www.fr.ch/document/517411)

## e-tax statements (eCH-0196) and other imports
- FriTax imports the previous year's FriTax file (.FRNP). No import of bank e-tax statements (eCH-0196 barcodes) is described on the pages checked; the guide asks for the bank tax statement to be attached when securities are held. (https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/fritax/fritax-faq ; https://www.fr.ch/document/57721)

## Tariffs, communal rates and deductions
- Income tariff: title says valid from 2023 onward. Wealth tariff: from 2021 onward. Both are listed as "Barèmes actuels" and the SCC 2026 calculator still uses them, so they apply to 2025 and 2026. (https://www.fr.ch/document/491766 ; https://www.fr.ch/document/434631 ; https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/calculer-ses-impots ; https://www.fr.ch/document/583511)
- Annual coefficient: the tariffs are stated at 100 percent and the Grand Conseil sets a coefficient each year (LICD art. 2), separately for income tax, wealth tax and source tax. 2025: https://bdlf.fr.ch/api/fr/versions/8110/pdf_file ; 2026: https://bdlf.fr.ch/api/fr/versions/8359/pdf_file. The 2025 guide also states the 2025 coefficient. (https://www.fr.ch/document/57721)
- Consolidated LICD: the version in force since 1 January 2025 is current, with no later version listed on 13 October 2026, so it governs 2025 and 2026 (income tariff art. 37, wealth tariff art. 62, social deductions art. 36). (https://bdlf.fr.ch/api/fr/versions/8109/pdf_file ; index https://bdlf.fr.ch/api/fr/texts_of_law/631.1)
- Communal tax: each commune sets a coefficient in percent of the cantonal base tax, the same for income and wealth, within the limits of the communal tax law (LICo). (https://bdlf.fr.ch/api/fr/versions/8653/pdf_file)
- Communal coefficients 2025 for all communes: CSV export from the cantonal open data portal (dataset 18_03_coefficients, publisher Service des communes). (https://opendata.fr.ch/api/explore/v2.1/catalog/datasets/18_03_coefficients/exports/csv?select=annee,commune,commune_id,district,coefficients,valeur,unite&refine=annee:2025&delimiter=%3B)
- The SCom interactive report covers coefficients by year from 2024 but works only through a POST form, so it cannot be fetched as a file. (https://www.fr.ch/diaf/scom/statistiques-financieres-des-collectivites-perequation)
- Parish (church) coefficient PDFs exist for 2025 and 2026; they are not communal rates. (https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/coefficients-communaux-et-paroisses)
- Deductions: the 2025 guide explains each deduction code (professional expenses, insurance, pillar 3a, childcare and others). (https://www.fr.ch/document/57721)
- Employee professional expenses: DFIN ordinance with flat rates for travel, meals, stays away from home, other costs, telework and side activities; version in force since 1 January 2023. (https://bdlf.fr.ch/api/fr/versions/7574/pdf_file)
- Health and accident insurance premiums: flat deductible amounts per category, ordinance in force since 1 January 2021. (https://bdlf.fr.ch/api/fr/versions/6598/pdf_file)
- Unusual mechanic: the child deduction depends on net income (LICD art. 36 al. 1 let. a and b); the SCC calculators show the income limit and steps for 2025 and 2026. (https://www.fr.ch/document/520166 ; https://www.fr.ch/document/583526)
- Calculator spreadsheets (XLSX, XLSM, XLS) for tax years 1997 to 2026 are listed; the 2025 and 2026 tax calculators are XLSM files, a format the manifest does not accept, so they are not entries. (https://www.fr.ch/impots/personnes-physiques/calculer-ses-impots ; https://www.fr.ch/document/546616 ; https://www.fr.ch/document/583511)
