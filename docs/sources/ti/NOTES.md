# Ticino (TI) filing notes, natural persons

Verified 2026-10-13 from official ti.ch, m3.ti.ch and m4.ti.ch pages only, with a second pass (gap check) the same day. Rates and tariff numbers are left out on purpose; read the documents. Never copy a number from this file.

## Filing channels, tax year 2025
- The return is filed with eTax PF, the free cantonal program, with Windows, macOS and Linux downloads. (https://www4.ti.ch/dfe/dc/dichiarazione/dichiarazione-elettronica)
- The eTax PF 2025 user guide names Information Factory SA as the company that produces the program, and says it is meant for the taxpayer's own return and direct transmission to the tax authority. (https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DC/DOC-IPF/2025/Guida_al_programma-2025.pdf)
- Login: registry number (n. di registro) and password, both on the first page of Modulo 1. (https://www4.ti.ch/dfe/dc/dichiarazione/dichiarazione-elettronica)
- Option 1: all forms and documents sent electronically; the return is complete on submission and eTax creates a confirmation sheet. (https://www4.ti.ch/tich/area-media/comunicati/dettaglio-comunicato?NEWS_ID=257983&cHash=155880c40e5f8328a3c7c401c6afa133 ; https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DC/DOC-IPF/2025/Guida_al_programma-2025.pdf)
- Option 2: electronic data plus mailed paper documents with the accompanying sheet; the return counts as received only when the paper arrives. (https://www4.ti.ch/tich/area-media/comunicati/dettaglio-comunicato?NEWS_ID=257983&cHash=155880c40e5f8328a3c7c401c6afa133)
- A signed receipt no longer has to be printed and mailed. (https://www4.ti.ch/dfe/dc/dichiarazione/dichiarazione-elettronica)
- Electronic submission can be done only once. (https://www4.ti.ch/dfe/dc/dichiarazione/dichiarazione-elettronica/faq-etax-pf ; https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DC/DOC-IPF/2025/Guida_al_programma-2025.pdf)
- Paper filing, including eTax printouts posted, remains valid. (https://www4.ti.ch/tich/area-media/comunicati/dettaglio-comunicato?NEWS_ID=257983&cHash=155880c40e5f8328a3c7c401c6afa133)
- Modulo 1 has no Italian PDF; only a German auxiliary translation is linked. (https://www4.ti.ch/dfe/dc/dichiarazione/moduli/moduli-pf-attuale)
- The 2025 instructions are now available as a PDF (56 pages) and explain the filing options on pp. 8 and 9. (https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DC/DOC-IPF/2025/Istruzioni/Istruzioni_PF_2025.pdf)

## Deadlines
- Ordinary deadline for 2025 returns: must reach the Ufficio circondariale di tassazione by 30 April 2026. (https://www4.ti.ch/tich/area-media/comunicati/dettaglio-comunicato?NEWS_ID=257983&cHash=155880c40e5f8328a3c7c401c6afa133)
- That date had passed on 2026-10-13. Check whether an extension was granted; no fetched page gives an extension deadline. (https://www4.ti.ch/tich/area-media/comunicati/dettaglio-comunicato?NEWS_ID=257983&cHash=155880c40e5f8328a3c7c401c6afa133)
- Extension: online request at www.ti.ch/pf-proroga, identified by registry number and date of birth, with a PDF confirmation. (https://www4.ti.ch/index.php?id=135721)

## Source tax
- TOU request: due by 31 March of the year after the tax year; once filed it cannot be withdrawn (directive point 2.5). The 2026 directive (December 2025) keeps the same rule. (https://m4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DC/DOC-IF/Direttive/Direttiva_UIF__vers._1.0_del_01.01.2025_.pdf ; https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DC/DOC-IF/Direttive/Direttiva_UIF__vers._1.0_del_01.01.2026_.pdf)
- TOU and rectification requests go through Online Request 2 on pform2.ti.ch. (https://www4.ti.ch/index.php?id=127378)
- TOU is mandatory for residents whose employment income is above the threshold on that page, for those with other ordinarily assessed income, and for those who get a C permit during the year. Others may opt in. (https://www4.ti.ch/index.php?id=127378)
- Italian cross-border workers under the 2020 Switzerland-Italy agreement can no longer request TOU. (https://www4.ti.ch/index.php?id=127378)
- Rectification: simple recalculation for B-permit holders, deadline 31 March of the year after taxation; Online Request 1 covers rate or table errors. (https://www4.ti.ch/index.php?id=127378)
- Source-tax tables for 2025 and 2026 are indexed on one page: A, B, C, H for residents and "vecchi frontalieri", R, S, T, U for "nuovi frontalieri", G and V for insurers, and for 2025 the VIC tables. (https://www4.ti.ch/dfe/dc/dichiarazione/imposte-alla-fonte-1/tabelle-di-calcolo-dellimposta-alla-fonte)
- Tables A, B, C (2025 and 2026) and H (2025 and 2026) are entries; each PDF states its edition year. (https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DC/DOC-IF/Aliquote_2026/Ticino_tabella_A_2026.pdf ; https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DC/DOC-IF/Aliquote_2025/Ticino_tabella_A_2025.pdf)
- The yearly executive decree on natural persons tells the Divisione to build the source-tax tables (art. 12). (https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DC/DOC-TRIBUTARIA/2026/DE_Persone_fisiche_2026_7_10_2026.pdf)
- The 2025 table H and the 2025 directive are on m4.ti.ch, while the index now links www4.ti.ch; both hosts serve the files. (https://m4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DC/DOC-IF/Aliquote_2025/Ticino_tabella_H_2025.pdf)

## Open points for the rules (packages/rules-ti, 2026-10-14)
Found while reading the downloaded documents for the cantonal and communal income and wealth tax of 2025 and 2026. Each cites the document id and page ("p." is the PDF's page: the instructions print the same numbers, the executive decrees print the page of the official bulletin instead); the numbers live in the documents. Two entries were added for this work: the main return Modulo 1, published only as a German translation (ti-2025-form-modulo-1-de), for its line numbers (cifre) and subtotals, and Modulo 5 (ti-2025-form-modulo-5), debts and donations.
- Conflict, the income tariff between the amounts the law prints: LT art. 35 gives, for each category, a rate with three decimals and the tax due at its top (ti-2025-tariff-decreto-esecutivo-pf p. 7, ti-2026-tariff-decreto-esecutivo-pf p. 5 and 6), and the two do not always agree: single tariff, 20.00 at 12,500 plus 4,900 at 5.232 % is 276.37, the law prints 276.40 at 17,400; family tariff, 23,196.85 at 227,800 plus 76,100 at 14.302 % is 34,080.67, the law prints 34,080.35 at 303,900. The canton's own 2025 table (ti-2025-guide-istruzioni-pf-pdf p. 51) fits neither reading exactly: at 60,000 (single) it prints 4,063.00 where both readings give 4,063.05; above 380,600 its amounts are the income times an effective rate cut to four decimals of a percent (700,000: 93,200.80, the top rate gives 93,201.10); and at 303,900 (family) it prints 34,080.25 where the law prints 34,080.35. No document gives the canton's method. rules-ti keeps every amount the law prints and draws a straight line between two of them (the top rate above the last), to 5 centimes; 80 of the table's 235 amounts differ from it, by at most 10 centimes below CHF 380,600 and 50 above, and test/tariffs.test.ts pins them. Every result says so in `unverifiedRules`. Ask the Divisione delle contribuzioni for the method; one assessment at an income between two printed amounts would settle it.
- Gap, 2026: the 2026 instructions, deduction table and Modulo 1 are not published. The 2025 instructions limit travel to 220 working days a year for a full-time job (ti-2025-guide-istruzioni-pf-pdf p. 23; ti-2025-form-modulo-4 p. 1); neither the 2026 decree nor the law repeats it, so rules-ti computes no driving costs for 2026 (public transport and bikes are not affected). rules-ti reads 2026 returns on the 2025 form's lines.
- Gap, communes: the table of multipliers (ti-2025-communal-rates, ti-2026-communal-rates) lists today's communes only, with an empty cell for a year in which a commune did not exist. LEMA has a multiplier for 2026 only, and the page does not name the communes it was formed from, so a 2025 resident of one of them cannot be computed (rules-ti refuses the commune). A commune merged into an existing one would have no row at all. The table gives no BFS numbers; rules-ti finds communes by their name as printed.
- Not in the documents: how the communal tax (multiplier times base tax) is rounded, and how the 5 % threshold of illness costs and the 20 % cap of donations are rounded. rules-ti rounds the communal tax to 5 centimes and the shares of income to the franc, and says so in `unverifiedRules`.
- Interpretation, the base of the illness costs' threshold: LT art. 32b says 5 % "dei proventi imponibili, dopo le deduzioni di cui agli articoli da 25 a 32b", Modulo 6 (ti-2025-form-modulo-6 p. 2) and the instructions (p. 30) say the net income II (cifra 21, after childcare). rules-ti uses cifra 21.
- Not in the documents: how the flat amount for other professional costs combines its two cuts for part of the year: halved under 6 months or a work rate under 50 % (ti-2025-tariff-decreto-esecutivo-pf art. 7 cpv. 3, p. 3; from 2026 LT art. 25 cpv. 2) and "proporzionalmente" for activity during part of the year (instructions p. 23). rules-ti does not compute part of a year of work, and assumes a whole year when not told (said in the result). It also fails closed when professional costs would exceed the salary, or the side-job flat amount (800) the side salary: no document says whether they can.
- Interpretation, the bike: "fino a 700 franchi l'anno, ma al massimo il costo del mezzo di trasporto pubblico" (decree art. 3 cpv. 1 lett. b) is read as a ceiling on the amount claimed, not a flat amount; rules-ti deducts the amount given, at most 700, and nothing without an amount.
- Not in the documents: which income caps the deduction when both spouses work, "fino a concorrenza del minore dei due redditi" (instructions p. 29; LT art. 32 cpv. 2 "reddito lavorativo"): the net salary, the salary after its costs, or with daily allowances. rules-ti computes the three and no tax when they differ.
- Not in the documents: childcare when one spouse has no paid work (LT art. 32a asks for a direct link with the work, training or incapacity "del contribuente"), and how the CHF 26,200 limit is shared in shared custody. rules-ti computes childcare for a couple only when both have a salary, and in shared custody without child support up to half the limit; otherwise no tax.
- Not in the documents, shared custody without child support: the law halves the child deduction (LT art. 34 cpv. 1 lett. a) but says nothing of the CHF 1,200 added per child to the insurance cap (art. 32 cpv. 1bis: "per ogni figlio ... per cui il contribuente può far valere la deduzione"), of the wealth deduction per minor child (art. 48 lett. b, CHF 30,000), or of the tariff (art. 35 cpv. 2: living with children whose upkeep one provides). rules-ti adds the 1,200 in full and applies the family tariff when the child lives with the parent (both said in `unverifiedRules`), and computes the wealth deduction only when half and all give the same tax. A parent who pays child support and lives with the child: the tariff is not settled, no tax.
- Rule found in the documents, not in these notes before: a spouse who dies during the year ends both spouses' liability that day, and the survivor is taxed from the next day (LT art. 53 cpv. 3; instructions p. 11): part-year liability, which rules-ti does not compute. Divorce or separation during the year: taxed apart for the whole year, by the family tariff for that year (art. 35 cpv. 5; instructions p. 11).
- Rule found in the documents: the exemption for AVS and AI pensioners (LT art. 34 cpv. 2; instructions p. 32) falls by 1,000 "per ogni 3'000 franchi di reddito supplementare"; the instructions' table reads it per 3,000 begun (21,001: 7,000), and rules-ti follows the table. It is not adjusted for cold progression (art. 39 cpv. 1).
- The communal personal tax (imposta personale, LT art. 290): a fixed amount for every person liable at the end of the year who is 18 or older, each spouse included. Church tax: the tax law lists the communes' taxes (art. 274: income and wealth, profit and capital, property, personal) and no church tax for natural persons; no document of the library gives one. rules-ti computes none and says so.
- The cantonal coefficient: 100 % for 2025 (instructions p. 51) and in the law in force on 1 January 2026 (LT art. 1b cpv. 1; art. 300a set 97 % for 2020 to 2023 only). The Gran Consiglio can change it with each year's budget (art. 1b cpv. 2); no document of the library shows a change for 2026, and rules-ti uses 100 %.
- The other professional costs' flat amount of 3,500 from 2026 comes from the law of 10 June 2026 (ti-2026-deductions-lt-art25 p. 1), subject to an optional referendum when published (BU 28/2026); the consolidated law has it in force from 1 January 2026 (ti-na-law-lt-640-100, note to art. 25).
- The return has no federal column: the authority derives the federal tax from the cantonal return (instructions p. 5). rules-ti's result has `ifd: null`; the federal tax for a Ticino household is packages/rules-ch's.
- The brake on the wealth tax (LT art. 49a; instructions p. 38) is granted on request: rules-ti fails closed when the cantonal and communal income and wealth taxes exceed 60 % of the taxable income.
- Not covered by rules-ti: property (cifra 5, 30), self-employment (2, 31), capital benefits taxed apart (art. 38), lottery winnings (6.2), children in education (25.2), dependants (25.3), disability costs (15.1), permanent charges and annuities paid (14.3, 14.4), weekly stays and shift work (Modulo 4, 3.2 and 4), the 3 per mille flat for securities costs on a global statement (13.1: actual costs only), part-year liability, source tax.
