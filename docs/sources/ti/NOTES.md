# Ticino (TI) filing notes, natural persons

Verified 2026-10-13 from official ti.ch, m3.ti.ch and m4.ti.ch pages only, with a second pass (gap check) the same day. Rates and tariff numbers are left out on purpose; read the documents. Never copy a number from this file.

## Filing channels, tax year 2025
- The return is filed with eTax PF, the free cantonal program, with Windows, macOS and Linux downloads. (https://www4.ti.ch/dfe/dc/dichiarazione/dichiarazione-elettronica)
- The eTax PF 2025 user guide names Information Factory SA as the company that produces the program, and says it is meant for the taxpayer's own return and direct transmission to the tax authority. (https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DC/DOC-IPF/2025/Guida_al_programma-2025.pdf)
- Login: registry number (n. di registro) and password, both on the first page of Modulo 1. (https://www4.ti.ch/dfe/dc/dichiarazione/dichiarazione-elettronica)
- Option 1: all forms and documents sent electronically; the return is complete on submission and eTax creates a confirmation sheet. (https://www4.ti.ch/tich/area-media/comunicati/dettaglio-comunicato?NEWS_ID=257983&cHash=155880c40e5f8328a3c7c401c6afa133 ; https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DC/DOC-IPF/2025/Guida_al_programma-2025.pdf)
- Option 2: electronic data plus mailed paper documents with the accompanying sheet; the return counts as received only when the paper arrives. (https://www4.ti.ch/tich/area-media/comunicati/dettaglio-comunicato?NEWS_ID=257983&cHash=155880c40e5f8328a3c7c401c6afa133)
- A signed receipt no longer has to be printed and mailed. (https://www4.ti.ch/dfe/dc/dichiarazione/dichiarazione-elettronica)
- Electronic submission can be done only once. (https://www4.ti.ch/dfe/dc/dichiarazione/dichiarazione-elettronica/faq-etax-pf ; https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DC/DOC-IPF/2025/Guida_al_programma-2025.pdf)
- Paper filing, including eTax printouts posted, remains valid. (https://www4.ti.ch/tich/area-media/comunicati/dettaglio-comunicato?NEWS_ID=257983&cHash=155880c40e5f8328a3c7c401c6afa133)
- Modulo 1 has no Italian PDF; only a German auxiliary translation is linked. (https://www4.ti.ch/dfe/dc/dichiarazione/moduli/moduli-pf-attuale)
- The 2025 instructions are now available as a PDF (56 pages) and explain the filing options on pp. 8 and 9. (https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DC/DOC-IPF/2025/Istruzioni/Istruzioni_PF_2025.pdf)

## Third-party software
- No official page found that allows or governs third-party or fiduciary software submitting a PF return. eTax, its FAQ and the 2026 press release name only the cantonal program. (https://www4.ti.ch/dfe/dc/dichiarazione/dichiarazione-elettronica ; https://www4.ti.ch/dfe/dc/dichiarazione/dichiarazione-elettronica/faq-etax-pf)
- RPEAF has no rule on third-party software and no approval or certified list. The personal code is issued by the authority, and programs are downloaded from the Divisione site (art. 3). (https://m3.ti.ch/CAN/RLeggi/public/index.php/raccolta-leggi/pdfatto/atto/10605)
- The RPEAF was amended for 2026 (BU 39/2025); the published PDF of that amendment has no extractable text, so its content is unverified, and the RPEAF entry above is the version before it (created November 2024). (https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DC/DOC-TRIBUTARIA/2026/Modifica_regolamento_procedure_elettroniche.pdf)
- The "File per Software House" page covers source-tax support files only, with no approval list. (https://www4.ti.ch/dfe/dc/dichiarazione/imposte-alla-fonte-1/software-house)

## Deadlines
- Ordinary deadline for 2025 returns: must reach the Ufficio circondariale di tassazione by 30 April 2026. (https://www4.ti.ch/tich/area-media/comunicati/dettaglio-comunicato?NEWS_ID=257983&cHash=155880c40e5f8328a3c7c401c6afa133)
- That date had passed on 2026-10-13. Check whether an extension was granted; no fetched page gives an extension deadline. (https://www4.ti.ch/tich/area-media/comunicati/dettaglio-comunicato?NEWS_ID=257983&cHash=155880c40e5f8328a3c7c401c6afa133)
- Extension: online request at www.ti.ch/pf-proroga, identified by registry number and date of birth, with a PDF confirmation. (https://www4.ti.ch/index.php?id=135721)
- No extension fee appears in the press release or on the request page. (https://www4.ti.ch/tich/area-media/comunicati/dettaglio-comunicato?NEWS_ID=257983&cHash=155880c40e5f8328a3c7c401c6afa133)
- Under RPEAF art. 2a, the first extension request must be electronic; later ones are granted on paper only in exceptional, justified cases. (https://m3.ti.ch/CAN/RLeggi/public/index.php/raccolta-leggi/pdfatto/atto/10605)
- Legal basis for extensions: LT art. 192 cpv. 1 and art. 198 cpv. 2 and 2bis. (https://www4.ti.ch/tich/area-media/comunicati/dettaglio-comunicato?NEWS_ID=247871)

## Source tax
- TOU request: due by 31 March of the year after the tax year; once filed it cannot be withdrawn (directive point 2.5). The 2026 directive (December 2025) keeps the same rule. (https://m4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DC/DOC-IF/Direttive/Direttiva_UIF__vers._1.0_del_01.01.2025_.pdf ; https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DC/DOC-IF/Direttive/Direttiva_UIF__vers._1.0_del_01.01.2026_.pdf)
- TOU and rectification requests go through Online Request 2 on pform2.ti.ch. (https://www4.ti.ch/index.php?id=127378)
- TOU is mandatory for residents whose employment income is above the threshold on that page, for those with other ordinarily assessed income, and for those who get a C permit during the year. Others may opt in. (https://www4.ti.ch/index.php?id=127378)
- Italian cross-border workers under the 2020 Switzerland-Italy agreement can no longer request TOU. (https://www4.ti.ch/index.php?id=127378)
- Rectification: simple recalculation for B-permit holders, deadline 31 March of the year after taxation; Online Request 1 covers rate or table errors. (https://www4.ti.ch/index.php?id=127378)
- Source-tax tables for 2025 and 2026 are indexed on one page: A, B, C, H for residents and "vecchi frontalieri", R, S, T, U for "nuovi frontalieri", G and V for insurers, and for 2025 the VIC tables. (https://www4.ti.ch/dfe/dc/dichiarazione/imposte-alla-fonte-1/tabelle-di-calcolo-dellimposta-alla-fonte)
- Tables A, B, C (2025 and 2026) and H (2025 and 2026) are entries; each PDF states its edition year. (https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DC/DOC-IF/Aliquote_2026/Ticino_tabella_A_2026.pdf ; https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DC/DOC-IF/Aliquote_2025/Ticino_tabella_A_2025.pdf)
- The yearly executive decree on natural persons tells the Divisione to build the source-tax tables (art. 12). (https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DC/DOC-TRIBUTARIA/2026/DE_Persone_fisiche_2026_7_10_2026.pdf)
- The 2025 table H and the 2025 directive are on m4.ti.ch, while the index now links www4.ti.ch; both hosts serve the files. (https://m4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DC/DOC-IF/Aliquote_2025/Ticino_tabella_H_2025.pdf)

## e-tax statements (eCH-0196) and other imports
- The eTax PF 2025 guide describes importing the previous year's return and an online ICTAX lookup for securities by valor number; it does not describe importing bank e-tax statements (eCH-0196). (https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DC/DOC-IPF/2025/Guida_al_programma-2025.pdf)

## Tariffs, multipliers and deductions (pointers to documents)
- Cantonal coefficient: LT art. 1a and 1b define the "imposta cantonale base" and the coefficient applied to it; the Gran Consiglio can change the coefficient yearly with the budget. (https://m3.ti.ch/CAN/RLeggi/public/index.php/index/nuovafinestra/atto/580/volume/numLegge)
- 2025 income and wealth tax tables (imposta cantonale base) are printed in the 2025 instructions, pp. 51 and 52, with a note on the coefficient reintroduced from 2024. (https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DC/DOC-IPF/2025/Istruzioni/Istruzioni_PF_2025.pdf)
- Unusual mechanic: the income tariff in LT art. 35 was updated for 2025 and again for 2026 for cold progression (art. 39 LT) through the annex of the yearly executive decree on natural persons. 2025 decree: https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DC/DOC-TRIBUTARIA/2025/D-BU_036-DE_Imposizione_PF_2025.pdf ; 2026 decree (adopted 7 October 2026, retroactive to 1 January 2026): https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DC/DOC-TRIBUTARIA/2026/DE_Persone_fisiche_2026_7_10_2026.pdf
- The consolidated LT html is marked "stato 1° gennaio 2026" and includes the 2026 changes; there is no current PDF of the LT (the RLeggi PDF of act 580 dates from 2018). (https://m3.ti.ch/CAN/RLeggi/public/index.php/index/nuovafinestra/atto/580/volume/numLegge ; https://m3.ti.ch/CAN/RLeggi/public/index.php/raccolta-leggi/pdfatto/atto/580)
- Communal multipliers: the communal tax is the multiplier applied to the imposta cantonale base; the official table lists every commune for 2021 to 2026, separately for natural (PF) and legal persons (PG). It exists only as an html page; the SEL data page links to the same table. (https://www4.ti.ch/dfe/dc/sportello/moltiplicatori-comunali ; https://www4.ti.ch/di/sel/comuni/dati-finanziari)
- Deductions 2025: comparison table cantonal versus federal (https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DC/DOC-IPF/2025/Istruzioni/Tabella_deduzioni_PF_2025.pdf) and the 2025 executive decree, arts 2 to 11 (professional expenses, training costs, children in education) (https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DC/DOC-TRIBUTARIA/2025/D-BU_036-DE_Imposizione_PF_2025.pdf)
- Deductions 2026: the 2026 executive decree, arts 2 to 11 (https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DC/DOC-TRIBUTARIA/2026/DE_Persone_fisiche_2026_7_10_2026.pdf), and the LT art. 25 cpv. 2 change on other professional expenses, retroactive to 1 January 2026 (https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DC/DOC-TRIBUTARIA/2026/BU_028.pdf)
- Practice circulars in force (for example n. 9/2023 on medical and disability costs, n. 18/2020 on family taxation, n. 4/2008 on children in education) are listed on the circulars page. (https://www4.ti.ch/dfe/dc/basi-legali/circolari/circolari-attuale)
- All yearly legal changes with their BU references are listed by year. (https://www4.ti.ch/dfe/dc/basi-legali/modifiche-alla-legislazione-tributaria-cantonale)

## What is new
- 2025: the first extension request must be filed electronically. (https://www4.ti.ch/tich/area-media/comunicati/dettaglio-comunicato?NEWS_ID=247871)
- 2025: the taxable share of life annuities now follows a formula; LT art. 21 cpv. 3. (https://www4.ti.ch/tich/area-media/comunicati/dettaglio-comunicato?NEWS_ID=247871)
- 2025: cross-border workers without Swiss domicile can be taxed at source on telework from their home state, within annual caps; LT art. 4 cpv. 1 lett. abis, art. 110 cpv. 3; RFLT art. 5a. (https://www4.ti.ch/tich/area-media/comunicati/dettaglio-comunicato?NEWS_ID=247871)
- 2025: no deduction changes reported; interest rates unchanged from 2024. (https://www4.ti.ch/tich/area-media/comunicati/dettaglio-comunicato?NEWS_ID=247871)
- 2026: communal multipliers for 2026 are published on the same page as 2025. (https://www4.ti.ch/dfe/dc/sportello/moltiplicatori-comunali)
- 2026: source-tax tables for 2026 are published. (https://www4.ti.ch/dfe/dc/dichiarazione/imposte-alla-fonte-1/tabelle-di-calcolo-dellimposta-alla-fonte)
- 2026: the executive decree for 2026 was adopted on 7 October 2026 and published in BU 34/2026 on 9 October 2026, retroactive to 1 January 2026. (https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DC/DOC-TRIBUTARIA/2026/DE_Persone_fisiche_2026_7_10_2026.pdf)
- 2026: LT art. 25 cpv. 2 on other professional expenses changed by the law of 10 June 2026 (BU 28/2026), retroactive to 1 January 2026. (https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DC/DOC-TRIBUTARIA/2026/BU_028.pdf)
- 2026: official property estimates rise from tax year 2026 (BU 7/2026); the SEL says rental values are not affected. (https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DI/DI_DI/SEL/Comuni/DatiFinanziari/NOT20260925-Modifiche_legislazione_tributaria-DEF.pdf)
- Coming, not for 2025 or 2026: an extraordinary cantonal wealth tax surcharge (new LT art. 309i) listed under 2027, higher insurance deductions (LT art. 32 cpv. 1 lett. g) listed under 2028, both in BU 28/2026, and a child deduction limited to residents from 2027 (not yet final on 25 September 2026). (https://www4.ti.ch/dfe/dc/basi-legali/modifiche-alla-legislazione-tributaria-cantonale ; https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DI/DI_DI/SEL/Comuni/DatiFinanziari/NOT20260925-Modifiche_legislazione_tributaria-DEF.pdf)

- The official instructions index lists 2025 only; no 2026 instructions as of 2026-10-13. (https://www4.ti.ch/dfe/dc/dichiarazione/istruzioni-pf/istr-pf-attuale)
