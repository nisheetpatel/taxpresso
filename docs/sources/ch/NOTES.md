# Swiss federal (CH) source notes

Checked 2026-10-13. Amounts and rates are left out on purpose: read them from the linked documents.

## IFD assessment and collection
- ESTV tariff circulars are addressed to the cantonal IFD administrations, which points to the cantons assessing and collecting IFD (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/mljRXo4VcR0p/2-216-D-2025-f.pdf)
- The ESTV forms page says other taxpayers get their declarations from their cantonal tax authority (https://www.estv.admin.ch/fr/formulaires-et-instructions-impot-federal-direct)
- Inference, not from a source: treat the IFD result as a federal computation that feeds the cantonal filing, and confirm the filing channel per canton

## Tariffs and cold-progression compensation
- The 2026 IFD tariff PDF groups married and single-parent families in one column (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/gnde9CmEsalK/dbst-tairfe-58c-2026-dfi.pdf)
- The 2026 instructions describe a separate parental schedule, so check the PDF before coding the split (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/oQ0wWcDmCJTS/2a-2026-fr.pdf)
- Tariff PDF text extraction is jumbled: read figures from the PDF and cross-check with the ESTV calculator (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/ZbaRlMm2u6u2/dbst-tarife-58c-2025-fr.pdf)
- Each year the AFC issues a cold-progression circular: 2025 (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/zkUbFt0fCQiy/dbst-rs-2-210-d-2024-fr.pdf), 2026 (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/vFK3ntWLQ4s4/2-215-D-2025-f.pdf), 2027 (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/sqbIsYvx0tZg/2-220-D-2026-fr.pdf)
- The 2026 compensation rests on a federal ordinance of 10 Sep 2025 (RO 2025 579) (https://www.estv.admin.ch/fr/lettres-circulaires-impot-federal-direct)
- Tax year 2027 tariffs and deductions were published on 22 Sep 2026, so 2026 values must not be reused for 2027 (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/sqbIsYvx0tZg/2-220-D-2026-fr.pdf)
- The 2026 circular shows 2025 and 2026 deduction amounts side by side; car travel per km is one item that changed (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/vFK3ntWLQ4s4/2-215-D-2025-f.pdf)

## Deductions and professional expenses
- Deduction tables for individuals sit in the yearly circulars; the 2026 instructions do not repeat them (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/vFK3ntWLQ4s4/2-215-D-2025-f.pdf)
- Professional expenses rest on the DFF ordinance of 10 Feb 1993, appendix amended 6 Mar 2015 (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/zkUbFt0fCQiy/dbst-rs-2-210-d-2024-fr.pdf)
- The 2026 instructions offer a flat-rate professional expense method, or actual costs using the professional expenses annex, form 9 (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/oQ0wWcDmCJTS/2a-2026-fr.pdf)
- The 2026 instructions state the pillar 3a maximums for 2026, with and without pension fund affiliation (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/oQ0wWcDmCJTS/2a-2026-fr.pdf)
- The BSV key-figures PDF lists the pillar 3a limits in section 9, citing OPP 3 art. 7 para. 1 (https://www.bsv.admin.ch/dam/bsv/fr/dokumente/bv/anleitungen/masszahlen-2025-2026.pdf.download.pdf/masszahlen-2025-2026.pdf)
- The BSV extraction shows the same values for 2025 and 2026, so the 2025 pillar 3a cap is not confirmed by an official source here (same URL)

## Source tax

## Withholding tax refund (impot anticipe)
- Residents use Form 21 for advance refunds and Form 25 for refunds (https://www.estv.admin.ch/fr/remboursement-impot-anticipe)
- A Form 25 request can be filed only after the calendar year in which the benefit fell due (same URL)
- The refund right lapses if no request is filed within three years after the end of that calendar year (same URL)
- Swiss-resident individuals apply to the canton of domicile at year-end (same URL)

## Conflicts and open points found while building the tax engine (2026-10-14)
- Single scale 2026, step at CHF 76,200: the consolidated LIFD (ch-na-law-lifd art. 36 al. 1, p. 29) gives a tax 5 centimes higher than the ESTV circular (ch-2026-ifd-progression-deductions p. 4) and Form. 58c 2026, whose rows all follow the circular's figure. The engine follows Form. 58c (every row is pinned by a test). Ask the ESTV which is right.
- Pillar 3a 2025: the engine takes the 2025 limits from the Geneva 2025 guide (ge-2025-guide-declaration-pp p. 11 and 18), not from the BSV table.
- Health insurance subsidies and the IFD: LIFD art. 24 let. d exempts subsidies; the Geneva guide (p. 27) declares them as income at code 16.30 without saying how the IFD column treats them. The engine leaves them out of IFD income and deducts only the premiums left to pay (LIFD art. 33 al. 1 let. g), and flags the rule as not confirmed.
- Married couple deduction (art. 35 al. 1 let. c): a social deduction in the LIFD, so after the medical costs (art. 33 al. 1 let. h) and donations (art. 33a) thresholds; the Geneva form takes it among the other deductions (59.20, ge-2025-guide-declaration-pp p. 13 and 31), before them. The engine follows the Geneva form and flags it.
- Shared custody: which parent gets the parental scale and the 263 reduction was not settled by the documents in the library. Settled since by ch-na-circulaire-30-epoux-famille (see the next section); the engine asks about child support and, without it, who earns more, and still computes no IFD when the parent doesn't know.

## Travel, meals and shared custody (added 2026-10-14)
- Added ch-na-circulaire-30-epoux-famille: ESTV circular 30 of 21 December 2010 on the taxation of spouses and the family, the only circular on the subject listed on https://www.estv.admin.ch/fr/imposition-du-couple-et-famille. It settles the open point above: with shared custody and no child support, the parental scale goes to the parent with the higher net income (p. 24, ch. 13.4.2; p. 32, ch. 14.4.2), each parent has half the child deduction and half the insurance amount per child (p. 19, ch. 10.2; p. 32, ch. 14.4.1); with child support, the parent who receives it has the parental scale, the child deduction and the insurance amount per child, the payer the base scale (p. 33, ch. 14.5). The scale can't be split: its parent has the whole reduction per child (p. 23, ch. 13.4.1). Its amounts are those of 2010 (reduction 251, childcare 5,050 and 10,100): read amounts in the yearly circulars, rules here.
- Code fixed by it: the engine used to add the full 700 per child in shared custody to the insurance limit (an assumption it flagged); the circular says half (ch. 14.4.1), which the engine now applies.
- Open point, not changed: in shared custody, each parent may deduct proven childcare "au maximum 5'050 francs", half of the 10,100 maximum of the time (ch. 14.4.1 and 14.5.2). The law's maximum is now 25,800 per child (ch-2026-ifd-progression-deductions p. 2), and no document gives today's half. The engine still allows the full maximum per child in shared custody. Ask the ESTV or the AFC whether half of 25,800 applies.
- Travel costs, IFD 2026: actual costs up to 3,300; public transport paid; a bike, a moped or a motorbike with a yellow plate up to 700 a year; a car or a motorbike with a white plate, the price of the public transport available, or per kilometre (0.75 car, 0.40 motorbike) only when public transport can't be used (disability, distance from the nearest stop, hours), the noon trip home counting only as a meal (ch-2026-ifd-instructions-personnes-physiques p. 6; ch-2026-ifd-progression-deductions p. 1). The 2025 car rate was 0.70 (same circular, 2025 column; ge-2025-guide-declaration-pp p. 20).
- Meals, IFD 2026: 15 per main meal taken out (as a rule lunch), 3,200 a year; half (7.50, 1,600) with meal vouchers or a canteen (box G); nothing when the meal costs the taxpayer 10 francs or less (ch-2026-ifd-instructions-personnes-physiques p. 6; ch-2026-ifd-progression-deductions p. 2). The instructions also say deductions are "réduites de manière appropriée" for part-year or part-time work, without saying how: the meals follow the days given, the public transport the cost paid; the bike flat of 700 is not reduced.
- The DFF ordinance on professional expenses (RS 642.118.1) is still not in the library; the instructions and the circular above state its 2026 rules and amounts.

## Pension capital (added 2026-10-14 by the pension capital agent)
- The rule: lump sums from the 2nd pillar and pillar 3a are taxed apart, a full year's tax for the year they are received, at rates of a fifth of the scales of art. 36 al. 1, 2 and 2bis first sentence, with no social deduction (ch-na-law-lifd art. 38, p. 32); the spouses' income is added up (art. 9 al. 1, p. 5), so are their lump sums. Form. 58c says its scales apply to these lump sums too (ch-2025- and ch-2026-ifd-tarif-personnes-physiques p. 1). packages/rules-ch, ifd/capital.ts.
- Wording conflict, the law wins: the instructions say the tax is "un taux représentant le cinquième de celui des barèmes 2026 (barème de base et barème parental)" (ch-2026-ifd-instructions-personnes-physiques p. 9), naming neither the married scale nor the limit to 2bis's first sentence, while their own definition of the parental scale includes the reduction of 263 francs per child (p. 9). The law applies the married scale to couples and the parental scale without the reduction per child (art. 38 al. 2). Taxpresso follows the law.
- Not spelled out: whether the CHF 25 minimum of art. 36 al. 3 applies (art. 38 al. 2 names the scales only). Between 0 and CHF 25 the federal tax on the capital is not computed (fail closed); at CHF 0 it is 0. How the fifth is rounded: Taxpresso rounds it down to 5 centimes, as note 2 of Form. 58c does for a year's tax, and flags it.
- The ESTV's capital tariff for source tax (annexe ch. 3 of the source-tax ordinance; Geneva publishes it as "Barème IFD de l'impôt à la source sur les prestations en capital") rounds the amount to the thousand and differs from art. 38: it is for people abroad only and is not in the library.

## Property abroad (added 2026-10-14 by the property abroad agent)
- The rule: property abroad is outside the federal tax (ch-na-law-lifd art. 6 al. 1, p. 4) and counts for the rate of the whole income (art. 7 al. 1, p. 5); losses abroad only for the rate, debts and debt interest in proportion to where the assets lie (art. 6 al. 3); general and social deductions in proportion to the net income (art. 35 al. 3, p. 29). packages/rules-ch, abroad/property.ts, shared with every canton that will add it.
- No federal document in the library spells out the method beyond the law: the federal instructions (Form. 2a) say nothing about property abroad. Taxpresso uses the Zurich tax manual 5.3 (zh-na-zstb-5-3 p. 1 and 2), which sets out the federal rules with the Federal Supreme Court's decisions; it is cited as the cantonal reading of the federal law, not as a federal document.
- Not spelled out: the reduction per child (art. 36 al. 2bis) when the tax is at the rate of the whole income (Taxpresso doesn't compute the IFD then), and how the rate is rounded (the scale's tax on 95.00, both amounts without the fraction under CHF 100, the tax rounded down to 5 centimes: Form. 58c notes 1 and 2).

## Childcare of a child of 14 or more (added 2026-10-14 by the childcare agent)
- The law grants the deduction "si l'enfant a moins de 14 ans" (LIFD art. 33 al. 3, ch-na-law-lifd p. 27), the instructions "pour chaque enfant (jusqu'à 14 ans)" (ch-2025-ifd-instructions-personnes-physiques and ch-2026-ifd-instructions-personnes-physiques p. 8), and circular 30 up to the 14th birthday (ch-na-circulaire-30-epoux-famille p. 13, 8.2). Every canton's engine now leaves the costs given for a child of 14 or more out of the federal sum before its maximum (packages/rules-ch, `federalChildcare`) and says so (IFD_NOTE.childcareAge); Geneva, Zurich, Bern, Basel-Landschaft, Basel-Stadt, Lucerne, St. Gallen, Vaud, Ticino and Zug used to add them. An amount given for the household, which can't be told apart per child, is still taken for the children under 14.
- Not covered: only each child's age at the end of the year is known, so the care before a 14th birthday during the year is left out too; the note says the costs "are not counted here".
- Nothing else changed, checked against main (80cbff0, and the app again after merging 7c4f4b1: the same records) on 14 October 2026. The engines: 36,000 seeded synthetic households (every canton, 2025 and 2026, 1,500 each), each result hashed before and after: the 32,537 without childcare for a child of 14 or more are identical; of the 3,463 with it, 3,110 differ only by the notes above and, in 635 of them, a higher tax (628 a higher federal tax, the rest Geneva's cantonal one), never a lower one; a federal "both spouses must work" note gives way to the age note where only an older child had costs. The other 353 are not computed, or the older child counts for the other parent. The app: extension/test/record-years.test.ts (TAXPRESSO_RECORD_HASH=1, 60 households per canton and the synthetic ones, every canton): 50,829 records for 2026 and 43,179 for 2025. 2025 is the same; in 2026, 11 households differ, each with an older child's childcare (Aargau 4, Zug 5, Lucerne 1, Ticino 1): 9 gain the federal note only, 2 in Zug also lose the older child's costs from the federal deduction (federal tax 39 and 118 francs higher), and in Ticino the age note replaces the "both spouses must work" one.
