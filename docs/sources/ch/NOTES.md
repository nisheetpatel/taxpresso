# Swiss federal (CH) source notes

Checked 2026-10-13. Amounts and rates are left out on purpose: read them from the linked documents.

## IFD assessment and collection
- ESTV tariff circulars are addressed to the cantonal IFD administrations, which points to the cantons assessing and collecting IFD (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/mljRXo4VcR0p/2-216-D-2025-f.pdf)
- The ESTV forms page says other taxpayers get their declarations from their cantonal tax authority (https://www.estv.admin.ch/fr/formulaires-et-instructions-impot-federal-direct)
- Inference, not from a source: treat the IFD result as a federal computation that feeds the cantonal filing, and confirm the filing channel per canton

## Tariffs and cold-progression compensation
- The 2026 IFD tariff PDF groups married and single-parent families in one column (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/gnde9CmEsalK/dbst-tairfe-58c-2026-dfi.pdf)
- The 2026 instructions describe a separate parental schedule, so check the PDF before coding the split (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/oQ0wWcDmCJTS/2a-2026-fr.pdf)
- Tariff PDF text extraction is jumbled: read figures from the PDF and cross-check with the ESTV calculator (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/ZbaRlMm2u6u2/dbst-tarife-58c-2025-fr.pdf)
- Each year the AFC issues a cold-progression circular: 2025 (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/zkUbFt0fCQiy/dbst-rs-2-210-d-2024-fr.pdf), 2026 (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/vFK3ntWLQ4s4/2-215-D-2025-f.pdf), 2027 (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/sqbIsYvx0tZg/2-220-D-2026-fr.pdf)
- The 2026 compensation rests on a federal ordinance of 10 Sep 2025 (RO 2025 579) (https://www.estv.admin.ch/fr/lettres-circulaires-impot-federal-direct)
- Tax year 2027 tariffs and deductions were published on 22 Sep 2026, so 2026 values must not be reused for 2027 (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/sqbIsYvx0tZg/2-220-D-2026-fr.pdf)
- The 2026 circular shows 2025 and 2026 deduction amounts side by side; car travel per km is one item that changed (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/vFK3ntWLQ4s4/2-215-D-2025-f.pdf)

## Deductions and professional expenses
- Deduction tables for individuals sit in the yearly circulars; the 2026 instructions do not repeat them (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/vFK3ntWLQ4s4/2-215-D-2025-f.pdf)
- Professional expenses rest on the DFF ordinance of 10 Feb 1993, appendix amended 6 Mar 2015 (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/zkUbFt0fCQiy/dbst-rs-2-210-d-2024-fr.pdf)
- The 2026 instructions offer a flat-rate professional expense method, or actual costs using the professional expenses annex, form 9 (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/oQ0wWcDmCJTS/2a-2026-fr.pdf)
- The 2026 instructions state the pillar 3a maximums for 2026, with and without pension fund affiliation (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/oQ0wWcDmCJTS/2a-2026-fr.pdf)
- The BSV key-figures PDF lists the pillar 3a limits in section 9, citing OPP 3 art. 7 para. 1 (https://www.bsv.admin.ch/dam/bsv/fr/dokumente/bv/anleitungen/masszahlen-2025-2026.pdf.download.pdf/masszahlen-2025-2026.pdf)
- The BSV extraction shows the same values for 2025 and 2026, so the 2025 pillar 3a cap is not confirmed by an official source here (same URL)

## Source tax
- The 2026 source-tax circular covers schedules A, B, C, H and cross-border schedules L to U (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/mljRXo4VcR0p/2-216-D-2025-f.pdf)
- Schedule G is not adjusted for 2026, and capital-benefit tariffs are unchanged (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/mljRXo4VcR0p/2-216-D-2025-f.pdf)
- Barème E is set in LIFD art. 37a, per the 2026 circular (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/mljRXo4VcR0p/2-216-D-2025-f.pdf)
- The 2026 circular's annex 5 is issued under the OIS, RS 642.118.2 (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/mljRXo4VcR0p/2-216-D-2025-f.pdf)
- The 2026 calculation bases list the annual social-deduction parameters and the per-child reduction used by the schedules (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/nuQbhdzXDPm0/qst-berechnungsgrundlagen-2026-fr.pdf)
- The calculation-bases overview does not cover schedules D, E and G (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/nuQbhdzXDPm0/qst-berechnungsgrundlagen-2026-fr.pdf)
- A 2022 FAQ on circular 45 covers working days abroad, the 13th salary, multiple jobs, family allowances and expatriates (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/estv/fr/dokumente/dbst/kreisschreiben/dbst-ks-2022-1-045-d-faq-fr.pdf.download.pdf/dbst-ks-2022-1-045-d-faq-fr.pdf)
- The FAQ says a more recent OFAS circular on source tax prevails over circular 45 for disability pensions (same URL)

## Withholding tax refund (impot anticipe)
- Residents use Form 21 for advance refunds and Form 25 for refunds (https://www.estv.admin.ch/fr/remboursement-impot-anticipe)
- A Form 25 request can be filed only after the calendar year in which the benefit fell due (same URL)
- The refund right lapses if no request is filed within three years after the end of that calendar year (same URL)
- Swiss-resident individuals apply to the canton of domicile at year-end (same URL)

## Electronic filing and calculator
- The ESTV portal is estvportal.estv.admin.ch, linked from the refund page (https://www.estv.admin.ch/fr/remboursement-impot-anticipe)
- The ESTV simulator covers federal and cantonal levels per its page, with no supported years stated; use it for cross-checks only (https://www.estv.admin.ch/fr/simulateur-fiscal-calculer-vos-impots)
- The ESTV crypto working paper is dated 14 Dec 2021, reflects practice to end 2020, and states no tax years (https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/HgzNhzz7q7YD/dbst-arbeitspapier-kryptowaehrungen-fr.pdf)
- The ESTV crypto page lists no crypto circular (https://www.estv.admin.ch/fr/cryptomonnaies-fiscalite)
- Fedlex pages show only a JavaScript notice to these tools, and fedlex-data PDFs are blocked for automated fetch (https://www.fedlex.admin.ch)

## Conflicts and open points found while building the tax engine (2026-10-14)
- Single scale 2026, step at CHF 76,200: the consolidated LIFD (ch-na-law-lifd art. 36 al. 1, p. 29) gives a tax 5 centimes higher than the ESTV circular (ch-2026-ifd-progression-deductions p. 4) and Form. 58c 2026, whose rows all follow the circular's figure. The engine follows Form. 58c (every row is pinned by a test). Ask the ESTV which is right.
- Pillar 3a 2025: the engine takes the 2025 limits from the Geneva 2025 guide (ge-2025-guide-declaration-pp p. 11 and 18), not from the BSV table.
- Health insurance subsidies and the IFD: LIFD art. 24 let. d exempts subsidies; the Geneva guide (p. 27) declares them as income at code 16.30 without saying how the IFD column treats them. The engine leaves them out of IFD income and deducts only the premiums left to pay (LIFD art. 33 al. 1 let. g), and flags the rule as not confirmed.
- Married couple deduction (art. 35 al. 1 let. c): a social deduction in the LIFD, so after the medical costs (art. 33 al. 1 let. h) and donations (art. 33a) thresholds; the Geneva form takes it among the other deductions (59.20, ge-2025-guide-declaration-pp p. 13 and 31), before them. The engine follows the Geneva form and flags it.
- Shared custody: which parent gets the parental scale and the 263 reduction is not settled by the documents in the library (the ESTV circular on family taxation is missing). The engine does not compute the IFD of a single parent with shared custody unless told.
