# Basel-Stadt (BS) sources: notes for natural persons, tax years 2025 and 2026

Verified 2026-10-13. Each fact is followed by its official source. The documents are the source of truth; never copy a number from this file.

## Filing channels, tax year 2025
- BalTax Online 2025 runs in the eSteuern.BS portal; available since 2 Feb 2026; standalone BalTax download no longer offered (https://www.bs.ch/themen/arbeit-und-steuern/steuererklaerung/steuererklaerung-als-natuerliche-person-einreichen)
- Login: one-time ePortal registration, sign-in via AGOV (in use since June 2024) (https://www.bs.ch/themen/arbeit-und-steuern/steuererklaerung/steuererklaerung-natuerliche-person/steuerportal-und-baltax-online)
- Private-person portal: https://esteuern.bs.ch/private (https://www.bs.ch/themen/arbeit-und-steuern/steuererklaerung/steuererklaerung-als-natuerliche-person-einreichen)
- Fully electronic: return and attachments can be sent without signature (https://www.bs.ch/themen/arbeit-und-steuern/steuererklaerung/steuererklaerung-als-natuerliche-person-einreichen)
- Electronic submission uses the 16-digit submission code printed on the forms and in the personal code letter (https://media.bs.ch/original_file/909f72222b7511334f4dc4d1b9aeb74a9f5cc54c/15500-fw-np-ste-25-05-wegleitung-bf-1.pdf, p. 3)
- Paper filing is still described as an option; whether paper needs a signature was not confirmed (https://media.bs.ch/original_file/909f72222b7511334f4dc4d1b9aeb74a9f5cc54c/15500-fw-np-ste-25-05-wegleitung-bf-1.pdf)
- Forms marked Ansichtsexemplar must not be used; file via BalTax (https://www.bs.ch/themen/arbeit-und-steuern/steuererklaerung/steuererklaerung-natuerliche-person/wegleitungen-und-formulare-fuer-natuerliche-personen)
- Clients or relatives can be filed for from your own AGOV login via case management (https://www.bs.ch/themen/arbeit-und-steuern/steuererklaerung/steuererklaerung-natuerliche-person/steuerportal-und-baltax-online)
- Help: BalTax hotline +41 61 267 67 67, baltax@bs.ch; AGOV hotline +41 61 267 31 31 (https://www.bs.ch/themen/arbeit-und-steuern/steuererklaerung/steuererklaerung-natuerliche-person/steuerportal-und-baltax-online)
- Tax liability ending in 2026 (departure abroad, death): file with BalTax 2025 (Beendigung 2026) and the B 2026 forms (https://www.bs.ch/themen/arbeit-und-steuern/steuererklaerung/steuererklaerung-natuerliche-person/wegleitungen-und-formulare-fuer-natuerliche-personen)

## Third-party software
- Dr. Tax is named as a route for electronic submission with the same submission code (https://www.bs.ch/themen/arbeit-und-steuern/steuererklaerung/steuererklaerung-natuerliche-person/steuerportal-und-baltax-online)
- No approval list, certification or API rules found on bs.ch (https://www.bs.ch/themen/arbeit-und-steuern/steuererklaerung/steuererklaerung-natuerliche-person/steuerportal-und-baltax-online)
- The BalTax tips PDF calls BalTax the official tool and names no third-party software, which conflicts with the Dr. Tax mention; confirm with baltax@bs.ch (https://media.bs.ch/original_file/bfbf34b4727f4f55d7664f5bd2977ae7073a90db/hinweise-und-tipps-zu-baltax-2025.pdf)
- The Steuerverordnung, checked 2026-10-13, has a section on electronic delivery of decisions and invoices but no rule on third-party filing software (https://www.gesetzessammlung.bs.ch/api/de/versions/6658/pdf_file)

## Deadlines, tax year 2025
- Filing deadline: 31 March 2026 (https://www.bs.ch/themen/arbeit-und-steuern/steuererklaerung/steuererklaerung-als-natuerliche-person-einreichen)
- Free extension online until 30 September 2026. This date has passed as of 13 Oct 2026 (https://www.bs.ch/themen/arbeit-und-steuern/steuererklaerung/steuererklaerung-als-natuerliche-person-einreichen)
- Further extension requests cost CHF 40; the page does not say whether they are accepted after 30 Sep 2026 (https://www.bs.ch/themen/arbeit-und-steuern/steuererklaerung/steuererklaerung-als-natuerliche-person-einreichen)
- Late return: reminder with CHF 40 fee; after a second reminder, ex officio assessment (https://www.bs.ch/themen/arbeit-und-steuern/steuererklaerung/steuererklaerung-als-natuerliche-person-einreichen)
- Return was mailed early February 2026 (https://www.bs.ch/themen/arbeit-und-steuern/steuererklaerung/steuererklaerung-als-natuerliche-person-einreichen)
- The old steuerverwaltung.bs.ch Abgabetermin page now redirects to the filing page above, anchor #abgabetermine (https://www.steuerverwaltung.bs.ch/steuererklaerung/natuerliche-personen/abgabetermin.html)
- Tax period 2026: cantonal taxes fall due 31 May 2027, federal tax 1 March 2027 (https://media.bs.ch/original_file/d2ea08f7875b1987c47f5323b96446582a81f30c/15500-fw-np-ste-26-01-b-wegleitung-bf.pdf, p. 9)

## Source tax (Quellensteuer, NOV and recalculation)
- NOV request: by 31 March of the following year, for residents and non-residents; cannot be withdrawn (https://media.bs.ch/original_file/445bc8b2afa6166c46741bdfda12f44792e12703/19000-mb-qst-a-26-wegleitung-und-tariftabellen.pdf, pp. 5, 11, 12)
- Recalculation (the guide's term is Neuberechnung): by 31 March of the year after the benefit fell due; only for wrong gross wage, wrong rate-determining income or wrong tariff; irrevocability not stated (https://media.bs.ch/original_file/445bc8b2afa6166c46741bdfda12f44792e12703/19000-mb-qst-a-26-wegleitung-und-tariftabellen.pdf, p. 12)
- Forms: Antrag auf nachträgliche Veranlagung (p. 85) and Antrag auf Neuberechnung (p. 86) (https://media.bs.ch/original_file/445bc8b2afa6166c46741bdfda12f44792e12703/19000-mb-qst-a-26-wegleitung-und-tariftabellen.pdf)
- Contact: steuerverwaltung@bs.ch, Fischmarkt 10, 4001 Basel (https://media.bs.ch/original_file/445bc8b2afa6166c46741bdfda12f44792e12703/19000-mb-qst-a-26-wegleitung-und-tariftabellen.pdf, p. 2)
- Mandatory ordinary assessment for residents whose gross earnings (Bruttoeinkünfte) in a calendar year exceed the threshold in the guide; it stays for the following years even if earnings drop. Earlier notes called it a taxable-income threshold, which was wrong (https://media.bs.ch/original_file/40246fa5532f29efb46e23a14ac580c4c6add64e/19000-mb-qst-a-25-wegleitung-und-tariftabellen-0.pdf, p. 11)
- Residents with income or assets not subject to source tax get a mandatory NOV (same page)
- 2025 and 2026 source-tax tables are inside the two 19000 guides (same URLs)
- bs.ch source-tax info page has no NOV or recalculation rules (https://www.bs.ch/themen/arbeit-und-steuern/quellensteuer/informationen-zur-quellensteuer)

## e-tax statements (eCH-0196) and other imports
- BalTax Online can import the eSteuerauszug (PDF) provided by financial institutions; paper receipts can be photographed and uploaded (https://media.bs.ch/original_file/909f72222b7511334f4dc4d1b9aeb74a9f5cc54c/15500-fw-np-ste-25-05-wegleitung-bf-1.pdf, p. 3)
- No eCH standard number, barcode specification or import rules for third-party files were found on bs.ch (https://www.bs.ch/themen/arbeit-und-steuern/steuererklaerung/steuererklaerung-natuerliche-person/steuerportal-und-baltax-online)

## New for 2025 and 2026
- Life annuities are taxed under a flexible method from 1 Jan 2025 (https://media.bs.ch/original_file/909f72222b7511334f4dc4d1b9aeb74a9f5cc54c/15500-fw-np-ste-25-05-wegleitung-bf-1.pdf)
- Some deductions and tariffs were inflation-adjusted from 1 Jan 2025 (https://media.bs.ch/original_file/909f72222b7511334f4dc4d1b9aeb74a9f5cc54c/15500-fw-np-ste-25-05-wegleitung-bf-1.pdf)
- Crypto Merkblatt dated 7 Jan 2026 applies to tax year 2025 (https://media.bs.ch/original_file/8c06838fade72c9930ac1c73967eca9ad0e70707/18023-mb-np-kryptowaehrungen-cd-bf.pdf)
- From tax year 2026 an additional retroactive pillar 3a purchase is possible, first in 2026 for 2025 (https://media.bs.ch/original_file/d2ea08f7875b1987c47f5323b96446582a81f30c/15500-fw-np-ste-26-01-b-wegleitung-bf.pdf, p. 3)
- Merkblatt Versicherungsleistungen (pensions, lump sums, daily allowances), 8th edition dated 21 Jan 2026, for cantonal and federal tax (https://media.bs.ch/original_file/fb16020828ee7cb0b1070c01ae88813f5fda0bfe/18007-mb-np-versicherungsleistungen-ab-2026.pdf)
- Voters approved a direct deduction of taxes from salaries on 12 June 2026; a pilot would start 1 Jan 2029 at the earliest, pending a Federal Supreme Court appeal. No effect on tax years 2025 and 2026 (https://www.bs.ch/medienmitteilungen/fd/2026-direktabzug-der-steuern-vom-lohn-soll-zwei-phasen-umgesetzt-werden)

## Tariffs, multipliers and deductions (pointers to documents)
- Income and wealth tariffs for tax year 2025 in one PDF (https://media.bs.ch/original_file/47a483fc991f91d59b9a86e4fbbbc0a9de77bd79/17000-steuertarife-2025.pdf)
- Income tariff overview covers 2018 to 2026 (https://media.bs.ch/original_file/d8cf2817b481307cd7943afa4f80b99a8878297a/einkommenssteuertarife-2019-2026.pdf)
- 2026 cantonal income tariffs A and B (pp. 44 to 47) and wealth tariffs A and B (pp. 48 to 49) as tables in the Wegleitung B 2026 (https://media.bs.ch/original_file/d2ea08f7875b1987c47f5323b96446582a81f30c/15500-fw-np-ste-26-01-b-wegleitung-bf.pdf)
- The wealth tariff rates are also in StG § 50, last changed with effect from 1 Jan 2023 per the change table (https://www.gesetzessammlung.bs.ch/api/de/versions/6745/pdf_file_with_annexes)
- No 17000 tariff book for 2026 is listed yet (https://www.bs.ch/themen/arbeit-und-steuern/steuererklaerung/steuererklaerung-natuerliche-person/merkblaetter-und-tariftabellen-natuerliche-personen)
- Mechanics: City of Basel residents pay full cantonal tax and no communal tax; Riehen and Bettingen residents pay a reduced cantonal income and wealth tax plus a communal tax computed from the cantonal tax and the communal Steuerfuss (https://media.bs.ch/original_file/d2ea08f7875b1987c47f5323b96446582a81f30c/15500-fw-np-ste-26-01-b-wegleitung-bf.pdf, p. 9)
- Riehen and Bettingen Steuerfüsse and the cantonal share (Steuerschlüssel) for 2025 and 2026 are in the 18000 tables; the 2025 tariff book also states the 2025 Steuerfüsse (https://media.bs.ch/original_file/138a8e2fb06d920cb89292256f1f3bee8aa06acb/18000-mb-np-ansaetze-devisenkurse-abzuege-freibetraege-v2025.pdf; https://media.bs.ch/original_file/533ad44a44ceaf9be3374016c8f8bfecd7ab184a/18000-mb-np-ansaetze-devisenkurse-abzuege-freibetraege-v2026.pdf)
- Bettingen 2026 rates were set by the municipal assembly on 9 Dec 2025 (https://www.bs.ch/bettingen/gemeinde/steuern-und-finanzen)
- Riehen publishes its rates on riehen.ch, outside the domains named in the brief, so it is not in the manifest (https://www.riehen.ch/verwaltung/dienstleistungen/173_steuerfuss)
- Deduction amounts for 2025 and 2026 (professional cost lump sums, commuting and meal caps, pillar 3a, insurance deduction, childcare, child and second-earner deductions) are rows of the 18000 tables, one column per year; some 2026 values are marked provisional (https://media.bs.ch/original_file/533ad44a44ceaf9be3374016c8f8bfecd7ab184a/18000-mb-np-ansaetze-devisenkurse-abzuege-freibetraege-v2026.pdf)
- Deduction rules: Wegleitung 2025 and Formular B (Berufskosten) and K/Z/F (illness, donations, childcare) (https://media.bs.ch/original_file/909f72222b7511334f4dc4d1b9aeb74a9f5cc54c/15500-fw-np-ste-25-05-wegleitung-bf-1.pdf; https://media.bs.ch/original_file/630cae1c108bb2f52c9d0b4e45506b4f22e695d5/15020-fw-np-ste-25-02-berufskosten-ansichtsexemplar.pdf; https://media.bs.ch/original_file/b40d5308954ad20f3d8b2e099f1eabbabe2046f8/15030-fw-np-ste-25-02-krankheitskosten-zuwendungen-kinderbet.pdf)
- Implementing rules for professional costs (StV §§ 19 to 29) and illness costs (§ 40); the childcare paragraph § 44 is repealed. Some caps in the ordinance text are not the indexed yearly amounts; use the 18000 tables (https://www.gesetzessammlung.bs.ch/api/de/versions/6658/pdf_file)
- bs.ch lists no separate Merkblatt on professional costs (only one for expatriates, 2021), the insurance premium deduction or childcare (https://www.bs.ch/themen/arbeit-und-steuern/steuererklaerung/steuererklaerung-natuerliche-person/merkblaetter-und-tariftabellen-natuerliche-personen)
- Deductible donation recipients, 2025 list (https://media.bs.ch/original_file/9562c945e5b6156eb0db6fac8cdbd6111cc0b235/18003-mb-np-spendenliste-internet-2025.pdf)
- Crypto: held privately it is wealth, shown by a wallet printout at year end; private gains are tax-free and losses not deductible; mining, staking and lending income is Vermögensertrag (https://media.bs.ch/original_file/8c06838fade72c9930ac1c73967eca9ad0e70707/18023-mb-np-kryptowaehrungen-cd-bf.pdf)
- Crypto is declared in the Wertschriftenverzeichnis, section B (https://media.bs.ch/original_file/07ff9ff885bd49287097c681c0bafe39b232923f/15005-fw-np-ste-25-01-wertschriftenverzeichnis-ansichtsexemp.pdf)
