# Basel-Stadt (BS) sources: notes for natural persons, tax years 2025 and 2026

Verified 2026-10-13. Each fact is followed by its official source. The documents are the source of truth; never copy a number from this file.

## Filing channels, tax year 2025
- BalTax Online 2025 runs in the eSteuern.BS portal; available since 2 Feb 2026; standalone BalTax download no longer offered (https://www.bs.ch/themen/arbeit-und-steuern/steuererklaerung/steuererklaerung-als-natuerliche-person-einreichen)
- Login: one-time ePortal registration, sign-in via AGOV (in use since June 2024) (https://www.bs.ch/themen/arbeit-und-steuern/steuererklaerung/steuererklaerung-natuerliche-person/steuerportal-und-baltax-online)
- Private-person portal: https://esteuern.bs.ch/private (https://www.bs.ch/themen/arbeit-und-steuern/steuererklaerung/steuererklaerung-als-natuerliche-person-einreichen)
- Fully electronic: return and attachments can be sent without signature (https://www.bs.ch/themen/arbeit-und-steuern/steuererklaerung/steuererklaerung-als-natuerliche-person-einreichen)
- Electronic submission uses the 16-digit submission code printed on the forms and in the personal code letter (https://media.bs.ch/original_file/909f72222b7511334f4dc4d1b9aeb74a9f5cc54c/15500-fw-np-ste-25-05-wegleitung-bf-1.pdf, p. 3)
- Paper filing is still described as an option; whether paper needs a signature was not confirmed (https://media.bs.ch/original_file/909f72222b7511334f4dc4d1b9aeb74a9f5cc54c/15500-fw-np-ste-25-05-wegleitung-bf-1.pdf)

- Clients or relatives can be filed for from your own AGOV login via case management (https://www.bs.ch/themen/arbeit-und-steuern/steuererklaerung/steuererklaerung-natuerliche-person/steuerportal-und-baltax-online)
- Help: BalTax hotline +41 61 267 67 67, baltax@bs.ch; AGOV hotline +41 61 267 31 31 (https://www.bs.ch/themen/arbeit-und-steuern/steuererklaerung/steuererklaerung-natuerliche-person/steuerportal-und-baltax-online)
- Tax liability ending in 2026 (departure abroad, death): file with BalTax 2025 (Beendigung 2026) and the B 2026 forms (https://www.bs.ch/themen/arbeit-und-steuern/steuererklaerung/steuererklaerung-natuerliche-person/wegleitungen-und-formulare-fuer-natuerliche-personen)

## Source tax (Quellensteuer, NOV and recalculation)
- NOV request: by 31 March of the following year, for residents and non-residents; cannot be withdrawn (https://media.bs.ch/original_file/445bc8b2afa6166c46741bdfda12f44792e12703/19000-mb-qst-a-26-wegleitung-und-tariftabellen.pdf, pp. 5, 11, 12)
- Recalculation (the guide's term is Neuberechnung): by 31 March of the year after the benefit fell due; only for wrong gross wage, wrong rate-determining income or wrong tariff; irrevocability not stated (https://media.bs.ch/original_file/445bc8b2afa6166c46741bdfda12f44792e12703/19000-mb-qst-a-26-wegleitung-und-tariftabellen.pdf, p. 12)
- Forms: Antrag auf nachträgliche Veranlagung (p. 85) and Antrag auf Neuberechnung (p. 86) (https://media.bs.ch/original_file/445bc8b2afa6166c46741bdfda12f44792e12703/19000-mb-qst-a-26-wegleitung-und-tariftabellen.pdf)
- Contact: steuerverwaltung@bs.ch, Fischmarkt 10, 4001 Basel (https://media.bs.ch/original_file/445bc8b2afa6166c46741bdfda12f44792e12703/19000-mb-qst-a-26-wegleitung-und-tariftabellen.pdf, p. 2)
- Mandatory ordinary assessment for residents whose gross earnings (Bruttoeinkünfte) in a calendar year exceed the threshold in the guide; it stays for the following years even if earnings drop. Earlier notes called it a taxable-income threshold, which was wrong (https://media.bs.ch/original_file/40246fa5532f29efb46e23a14ac580c4c6add64e/19000-mb-qst-a-25-wegleitung-und-tariftabellen-0.pdf, p. 11)
- Residents with income or assets not subject to source tax get a mandatory NOV (same page)
- 2025 and 2026 source-tax tables are inside the two 19000 guides (same URLs)
- bs.ch source-tax info page has no NOV or recalculation rules (https://www.bs.ch/themen/arbeit-und-steuern/quellensteuer/informationen-zur-quellensteuer)

## Open points for the rules (packages/rules-bs, 2026-10-14)
Found while reading the downloaded documents for the cantonal and communal income and wealth tax of 2025 and 2026. Each cites the document id and page ("p." is the PDF's page; the guides print the same numbers); the numbers live in the documents.
- Conflict, the yield factor of accounts and securities (Kapitalisierungssatz Wertschriften) for 2025: the 2025 table prints 193 for 2025, in italics, that is provisional (bs-2025-communal-ansaetze-abzuege p. 2); the 2026 table prints 345 for 2025, final, and 345 for 2026 in italics (bs-2026-communal-ansaetze-abzuege p. 2); the 2025 guide (bs-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 40) and form W (bs-2025-form-w-wertschriftenverzeichnis p. 4, line 14) use 345. rules-bs uses 345 for 2025, and 345 for 2026 flagged as an estimate everywhere it is used (`bs-2026-provisional`): the canton sets it from the rates at the end of September of the tax period (bs-na-law-stv § 49 al. 3, p. 13). The 2026 guide B also prints 345 (bs-2026-guide-wegleitung-b p. 40), but it serves returns for a liability ending in 2026, for which the previous year's factors apply (StV § 49 al. 4), so it does not settle 2026. The same two tables differ on the "Eigenkapitalverzinsung für Ermässigung der Vermögenssteuer" of 2025 (1.00 provisional, then 0.50); no document says what it is used for (see § 52 below).
- Rule found in the documents, not in these notes before: accounts and securities are taxed at their market value or, when lower, at the mean of the market value and the yield value (gross income times 345) (bs-na-law-stg § 46 al. 2, p. 20; bs-na-law-stv § 48 lit. d and § 49, p. 13; form W p. 4, lines 11 to 16; guide p. 40). With today's low interest, a bank account is often taxed well under its balance. rules-bs needs the gross income of every account and security for this, and computes nothing without it.
- Conflict inside the guide's worked example (the Winter-Sommer family, bs-2025-guide-wegleitung p. 11 to 17, the same in bs-2026-guide-wegleitung-b): form K starts from a net income (709) of 115,886 in the first box and 115,866 in the second, with nothing subtracted between them, and of 112,998 for the federal tax, while the main form's 709 is 115,484 and 112,596 (402 less in both columns); its threshold of 5 % is 5,794, which is 115,886 at the nearest franc or 115,866 rounded up, so the rounding of the threshold is not settled either. Form W's line 14 prints 137,416, while its line 4 (797) times 345 is 274,965, and its line 15 (212,888) is not line 13 plus line 14; the value carried to the main form (the market value, 90,865) is right under any of these. rules-bs's tests check each rule against its own form's numbers and the main form's totals against its lines.
- Conflict, the wealth tax reduction for a low income (bs-na-law-stg § 51, p. 21): the law says a taxable income of "nicht mehr als" CHF 14,000 (20,000 for spouses and parents), the guide "weniger als" (guide p. 43); neither says whether the bands (up to 100,000, 200,000, 400,000) are of the net or the taxable wealth, nor whether the taxable income counts before or after its rounding to CHF 100. rules-bs computes the reduction only when every reading gives the same, and nothing otherwise.
- Not in the documents, wealth with a low yield (§ 52, p. 21; guide p. 43): how the "Einkommenssteuer auf dem Vermögensertrag" is measured (proportional share or marginal tax; gross or net yield; with or without the communal tax), and whether the table's "Eigenkapitalverzinsung für Ermässigung der Vermögenssteuer" (0.50 % for 2025 and 2026) enters it. Interpretation: "ermässigt ... höchstens aber auf 5‰" is read as a floor (the reduced tax is never under 5 per mille of the taxable wealth), so the reduction can only apply when the tariff tax is above 5 per mille; rules-bs computes the tax only at or below that line (taxable wealth up to about CHF 333,000 on tariff A and 533,000 on tariff B) and fails closed above it. Ask the Steuerverwaltung for the method.
- Conflict, both spouses work (660): the law excludes replacement income from the deduction (bs-na-law-stg § 32 al. 2, p. 15), the guide counts "Erwerbsausfallentschädigungen" with the earnings (guide p. 35). rules-bs computes both and no tax when they differ.
- Conflict, the deduction for single retirees (770): the ordinance gives it to those whose "Einkommen" is at least half pensions of the first, second and third pillar (bs-na-law-stv § 43 al. 2, p. 12); the guide to those whose "Einkommen aus Renten und Erwerb" is at least half pensions of the AHV/IV/UV/MV, the pension fund or pillar 3a (guide p. 39): another base, and accident and military pensions named. rules-bs computes the four readings and decides only when they agree (or the reference age is reached).
- Not in the documents: whether the wealth allowance of CHF 15,000 per minor child (§ 49 al. 1 lit. c, p. 20) is shared between parents with joint custody and no child support, as the child deduction is (§ 35 al. 1 lit. a, p. 16); whether each parent with shared custody has the whole childcare limit (CHF 26,000, § 32 al. 1 lit. i) or half; whether the training limit (CHF 19,100, § 32 al. 1 lit. k) is per spouse or per couple. rules-bs computes where every reading gives the same tax (childcare up to half the limit, the allowance when the taxable wealth is zero either way) and fails closed otherwise.
- Not in the documents: how the canton's share (50 %) and the communal tax of Riehen and Bettingen are rounded (the tariff tables print the tariff tax to the centime); how the 5 % threshold of the illness costs, the 20 % cap of donations and the mean of form W are rounded. rules-bs rounds the shares of the tax to 5 centimes, the shares of income to the franc and the mean down to the franc, and says so in `unverifiedRules`. One assessment of a Riehen or Bettingen resident would settle the first.
- Interpretation, the communal tax: Riehen and Bettingen levy their tax "in Prozenten (Steuerfuss) der nach diesem Gesetz berechneten Steuern" (bs-na-law-stg § 2 al. 2, p. 1) and the canton its 50 % of the same tax (§ 228 al. 1, p. 73); rules-bs takes the wealth tax after the reduction of § 51 as that tax, and says so when it matters.
- Not in the documents for a part of the year: the flat professional costs (CHF 4,200) are "angemessen zu kürzen" for work during part of the year (§ 27 al. 2, p. 12), and the guide gives only the rule for part-time work (proportional at a work rate of 60 % or less, at least CHF 840, guide p. 30). rules-bs assumes a full year of work and says so.
- Gap in the forms: the 2026 table of deductions labels both rows "Abzug für Säule 3a mit 2. Säule (maximal)" as "Bund" (bs-2026-communal-ansaetze-abzuege p. 2); the first is presumably the cantonal one. Both print 7,258, the federal limit.
- Interpretation, the insurance deduction (630/631): the law grants it "im Pauschalbetrag" (§ 32 al. 1 lit. g, p. 14) and the guide as an amount anyone can deduct (p. 35, the worked example deducts 8,400 without listing premiums); rules-bs gives the flat amount whatever was paid.
- The tax law keeps the figures of its last revision (§ 27, § 32, § 35, § 36: for example CHF 18,500 for a single person and tariff A's first bracket up to CHF 201,500); the canton adjusts them each year (§ 37, p. 17) and prints the adjusted ones in its tables (18000), the tariff book (17000, 2025) and the guides. No conflict; rules-bs uses the adjusted amounts. The 2026 amounts equal 2025's (no adjustment for 2026).
- Single parents pay wealth tax by tariff A (bs-na-law-stg § 50, p. 21; guide p. 9) while their income tax is by tariff B (§ 36 al. 2). Tariff B in § 36 al. 2 does not exclude a cohabiting parent, while the deduction for single parents (765) and the wealth allowance of 150,000 do (form p. 1, section 3; guide p. 39 and 43; StV § 42): rules-bs does not compute a single parent living in a cohabiting couple (Konkubinat, with the partner support deduction 757).
- Church tax: the recognised churches levy it on their members (bs-na-law-stg § 2a, p. 1); its rates are in no document of the library except inside the source-tax tariffs. rules-bs does not compute it and says so.
- No document of the library gives the communes' federal numbers (BFS); rules-bs finds Basel, Riehen and Bettingen by name.
- Not covered by rules-bs: property (479, 821, the rental value), self-employment, capital benefits taxed apart (§ 39), the support deduction (755) and the partner support (757), disability costs and their flat amounts, vehicles and collections (the guide exempts vehicles of daily use, p. 40), lottery winnings, weekly stays away from home and part-year liability. The federal tax of the Bund column is computed since the wiring (next section).

## Wiring Basel-Stadt in the app (2026-10-14)
Found while giving rules-bs its federal column and wiring Basel-Stadt as a preview. Each cites the document id and page.
- Federal column: the main form prints the Bund column next to the Kanton one from 369 down (bs-2025-form-hauptformular p. 2 and 3), forms B, K, Z and F each have a Bund column (bs-2025-form-b-berufskosten p. 1 and 2; bs-2025-form-kzf-krankheit-zuwendung-betreuung p. 1 and 2). rules-bs puts each federal deduction of packages/rules-ch on that line (`packages/rules-bs/src/ifd.ts`): the illness costs (725) and donations (Z) on the Bund 709, the parties before it, the children (750) and the couple deduction (760) after 739, as Zurich's form does. A seeded test (packages/rules-bs/test/federal.test.ts) shows the federal tax equals Geneva's and Zurich's for 2,000 households without a married couple's illness costs or donations, and Zurich's for 1,000 more; Geneva's form takes its couple deduction before the illness costs, so a married couple's 5 % threshold differs by 5 % of the couple deduction (pinned in the test; the same open point as Zurich's).
- Childcare, federal: form F gives the limit per child in both columns ("für die Betreuung eines Kindes", bs-2025-form-kzf-krankheit-zuwendung-betreuung p. 2). The shared federal rule (packages/rules-ch, `childcareCosts`) pools the costs against the number of children times the limit, as Geneva's and Zurich's federal columns do. The two give the same deduction unless one child's costs pass the limit while another's stay under it; rules-bs keeps the shared rule so that the federal tax stays the same in every canton. Which reading the federal law means is a question for the lead.
- Childcare, cantonal: the limit is per child too (form F; bs-na-law-stg § 32 al. 1 lit. i). Childcare given for the whole household counts when there is one eligible child or the total is within one child's limit; otherwise the questionnaire asks it per child (`bs.child_care`), and the tax is not computed until then.
- Federal side job (Nebenerwerb, form B, 516): 2026 is computed with the federal flat (ch-2026-ifd-instructions-personnes-physiques p. 7); no 2025 document of the library gives the 2025 rate, so a 2025 return with a side job has no federal tax (fail closed), as for Bern.
- Accounts and securities: the shared reading of the état des titres gives a value without income for an account whose statement shows no interest. rules-bs needs each line's gross income for the mean of form W (lines 11 to 16, bs-2025-form-w-wertschriftenverzeichnis p. 4); the adapter reads a missing income as zero only when the reading is complete, and passes an incomplete reading on, so the wealth tax is not computed from a guess.
- Insurance premiums (630/631): the cantonal deduction is a flat amount whatever was paid (see above), but the federal one is what was paid, capped; the adapter keeps the premiums, the health insurance subsidy, life insurance premiums and savings interest for the federal column only.
- Questions beyond the engine's six facts (work rate, reference age, cohabiting, main upkeep, each child under 18, church): childcare per child (above) and payments to political parties (form Z, `bs.party`, household), as Bern asks them. The church answer only says whether the tax would be due: the church tax is not computed (no rates in the library, see above).
- 2026 forms: not published; the copy panel uses the 2025 main form and forms B, K, Z, F and W (packages/filing-bs), flagged provisional for 2026. No official PDF check and no reading of last year's Basel-Stadt return yet.
- Asking for more time to file: "Fristerstreckung", requested online (bs-2025-efiling-einreichen-page, section Fristerstreckung); the final check links that page. The official software is BalTax Online, in the eSteuern.BS portal (bs-2025-efiling-steuerportal; bs-2025-efiling-baltax-tipps p. 1). No document of the library says it is free to use; the shared final check calls each canton's software "the canton’s free software".
- Plans before 31 December: Basel-Stadt's flat professional costs depend on each earner's work rate (guide p. 30), which the plans read with the rest of the ledger, and the shared reading gives nothing until the commune is known. So a Basel-Stadt plan shows no saving until the commune and the work rate are answered (fail closed), and the default commune (Basel) is not used in practice. Reading the canton's own answers without the commune would let the plans assume the commune as for Zurich and Bern; that changes the shared reading, so it is left to the lead.
- The work rate is asked when the answers say there is a salary (income_sources), like the other questions about a salary. A salary certificate added without that answer gets no work rate question, and the estimate says what to answer instead of computing the flat professional costs (fail closed; seen in a seeded fuzz of 600 synthetic households, which raised no error). Asking it from the documents too would need the questionnaire to read documents.
