# Official sources library

Official tax documents for Geneva, its neighbours and the large cantons (eighteen folders: sixteen cantons, federal tax and the eCH data standards). Collected 13 and 14 October 2026 by one agent per canton, every URL opened and checked against its title and tax year. Official government sites only.

## How these documents are used

They are not a rulebook to transcribe. They are the authority you go back to:

- whenever there is any doubt about a rule, a value, a deadline or a form label;
- at every final check: a phase's acceptance check while building, and the pre-filing check at runtime.

When a document and the code, the spec or Claude's own knowledge disagree, the document wins. Stop, record the conflict, and ask the human. Every tax constant in the rules engine cites the id of the entry it comes from (SPEC.md section 7).

## Layout

- `<canton>/manifest.json`: verified documents for that canton (edit these).
- `<canton>/NOTES.md`: filing channels, third-party software rules, deadlines, source tax (TOU/NOV), what's new, and gaps, each fact with its source URL.
- `<canton>/files/`: the downloaded documents, named `<id>.<format>` (created by the fetch script).
- `manifest.json`: all entries merged. Regenerate with `node scripts/merge-manifests.mjs`.
- `lock.json`: size, sha256 and retrieval time of every downloaded file (created by the fetch script).

Folders: `ge` `vd` `vs` `fr` `ne` `ju` `be` `ti` `bs` `bl` `zh` `lu` `ag` `sg` `zg`, plus `ch` (federal: IFD tariffs and deductions, source tax, withholding tax) and `standards` (eCH-0196, eCH-0270, eCH-0278, eCH-0119, salary certificate guide).

## Getting the files

The documents were found from an environment whose network policy blocks downloads from the cantonal sites, so the files are not in this folder yet. On a normal connection:

```
node scripts/fetch-sources.mjs            # everything (a few hundred MB at most)
node scripts/fetch-sources.mjs --core     # core documents only
node scripts/fetch-sources.mjs --canton ge,ch,standards
node scripts/fetch-sources.mjs --check    # report documents whose content changed since lock.json
```

The script checks that each file really is what the manifest says (a PDF starts with `%PDF`, and so on), so a canton serving an HTML error page instead of a PDF is caught. It exits non-zero if any core document fails. The files are not committed: they belong to their publishers. `lock.json` records the size and sha256 of every file, so `--check` tells you when a canton changed or moved a document (they do: Geneva's 2025 communal arrêté already moved).

## Known conflicts and open points (check before relying on them)

- Lucerne: the childcare cap of StG § 40 Abs. 1l reads higher than the canton's overviews and guide, while § 42 Abs. 1b and the Steuerbuch count the same maximum with own care included and agree with them (packages/rules-lu follows § 42 and flags it); the law text on life annuities differs from the 2025 guide; Steuerbuch § 145 still describes the old downloadable software. The law takes the base of medical costs and donations before training, the form after it; the guide's examples round the tax ties both ways, its tariff tables misprint five rows (the law and the Berechnungstabelle agree on all 20,902 rows, scripts/lu-tariff.mjs --check), and its sample return keeps a few 2024 amounts. The units are generated by scripts/lu-communes.mjs. The federal tax follows the optional Ergänzungsblatt, whose 2025 edition prints two federal amounts otherwise than the federal circular and the overview (the guide's page on the differences prints one of them as the sheet does). See `lu/NOTES.md`, "Open points for the rules" and "Wiring Lucerne in the app".
- Aargau: the eTAX start date (26 January vs 1 February 2026) and the change window after submitting (24 vs 48 hours) differ between official pages; the 2025 Progressionsverordnung differs from the StG. For the rules: no document says how the cantonal, communal and church taxes are rounded (the guide's example implies 10 centimes); the law and the guide's form differ on whether donations come before the medical costs' own share; StGV § 13 lowers the rate per km beyond 15,000 km where the guide gives one rate; the 100 % ceiling at the low-income deduction's steps and the sharing of church tax between confessions are not explained; no 2026 tariff or adjustment was published (the 2025 ones are listed as valid from 2025); the main form is not published, and the guide's own references (Ziffer 22.1, Ziff. 30.6) and eTAX AARGAU's list of receipts (Ziffer 30.1, 30.3) number lines differently from the guide's sections. Wiring: the return has no federal column and no document says how the tax office knows the insurance premiums for the federal deduction; the other cantons' federal columns count a child of 14 or more's childcare in the capped total, the federal instructions only children up to 14. The tariffs are checked by scripts/ag-tariff.mjs --check, the communes generated by scripts/ag-communes.mjs. See `ag/NOTES.md`, "Open points for the rules" and "Wiring Aargau in the app".
- St. Gallen: Steuerbuch chapters dated 2021 quote older caps than the 2025 guide; use them for conditions only. For the rules: the guide's worked example (p. 50) prints an einfache Steuer the 2025 tariff and the law don't give; the ordinance's federal rate per km for motorbikes differs from the guide's and the Steuerbuch's single rate; the law and the guide word the higher insurance maximum differently (AHV contributions); the law and Form 10 take scholarships off children's education costs at different steps; no document says how the multiplied taxes are rounded (the tariff rounds the einfache Steuer to 5 Rappen); no 2026 guide or forms yet. The tariff is checked by scripts/sg-tariff.mjs --check, the communes generated by scripts/sg-communes.mjs. See `sg/NOTES.md`, "Open points for the rules".
- Zurich: the 2026 cantonal multiplier differs between the Kantonsrat decision and the 2026 source-tax parameter sheet; the rounding of taxable amounts and taxes is in none of the documents; some communes have several multipliers by part. See `zh/NOTES.md`, "Open points for the rules".
- Bern: the communal xlsx and the finance administration's document 556.6 give different church tax rates for seven communes or parts (2025 and 2026); the StG and the canton's practice page differ on whether pension fund buy-ins reduce the cap of the two-earner deduction; the Kirchensteuergesetz is not in the library. See `be/NOTES.md`, "Open points for the rules".
- Zug: tariff headers give different index dates (the same index 110.2 with two bases), while the 2025 and 2026 brackets are the same; a sample form says 60 days while the guide says 30 April; the law, the table of amounts and the guide word the higher insurance deduction differently (without contributions under § 30 Bst. d and e, to the 2nd pillar, to the 2nd pillar or 3a); no document says how the einfache Steuer and each Steuerfuss of it are rounded, and no worked tax example exists (the guide's sample return stops at the taxable income); the 2026 guide is not published, so the 2026 professional costs come from the federal annex (Steuerbuch 16.1) and no 2026 limit on days driven is known. The tariffs are checked by scripts/zg-tariff.mjs --check and the Steuerfüsse generated by scripts/zg-communes.mjs. See `zg/NOTES.md`, "Open points for the rules" and "Wiring Zug in the app".
- Basel-Landschaft: six baselland.ch HTML pages refuse scripted downloads (Cloudflare challenge); open them in a browser. The Steuerbuch (34 Nr. 3) says a Vollsplitting income whose half is under the tax-free limit stays tax-free, while the law (§ 34 Abs. 2) and the 2025 and 2026 tariff tables apply the minimum rate; its worked example of the formula rounds down where the rules of application and the tables round to the nearest 5 centimes; the parishes' church rates are published for 2026 only, and the Reformed and Christ Catholic churches' rules are not in the library. The guide and the Steuerbuch compute the federal threshold on illness costs as the Reineinkommen x 5 : 95 (5 % of the net income after the deduction), where the federal instructions take 5 % of the net income before it. The tariff is read by scripts/bl-tariff.mjs --check and the communes by scripts/bl-communes.mjs. See `bl/NOTES.md`, "Open points for the rules" and "Wiring Basel-Landschaft in the app".
- Barcode compression: the eCH-0196 barcode annex describes ZLIB-compressed XML, while eCH-0270 says "ZIP". The decoder handles both (and GZIP and raw deflate) and reports which one a statement used; still open until a real bank statement settles it (docs/spikes/S2.md).
- Salary certificate 2D barcode: not on the ESTV or CSI pages, but specified by Swissdec (std-swissdec-elm5-lohnausweis-richtlinien-fr, TaxAccountingOverview_f.pdf p. 35-37, and std-swissdec-elm6-barcode-annexe5): PDF417, 14 control bytes, an Info-ZIP archive with one entry "txab", the TxAB XML. It does not follow eCH-0270. The schema requires a Company element; Taxpresso accepts a certificate without it (it carries no box) and notes it (docs/spikes/S2.md).
- eCH-0278 status: the cover says approved 2026-02-05, but the body mentions a later decision and calls it a proposal. Confirm before building on it. Its XSD and Excel annexes weren't found.
- Shared custody, IFD: ESTV circular 30 (ch-na-circulaire-30-epoux-famille, added 2026-10-14) gives the parental scale to the parent who receives child support or, without it, earns more. Its amounts date from 2010; its half childcare maximum per parent in shared custody has no current figure in any document (see `ch/NOTES.md`).
- Travel costs, ICC: the Geneva guide caps them at 534 whatever the means of transport but prices them only for the IFD; Taxpresso uses the federal pricing and flags it (see `ge/NOTES.md`).
- Federal law texts: the LIFD is in `ch` (ch-na-law-lifd, Fedlex filestore PDF). The LHID, the source-tax ordinance and the professional-expense ordinance are still missing: the fedlex.admin.ch pages only render in a browser, but their filestore PDFs download directly.
- Pillar 3a 2025 maximum: the federal key-figures table extracted the same value for 2025 and 2026. Read it from the PDF before hard-coding anything.
- Federal 2027 cold-progression circular is already published: never reuse 2026 amounts for 2027.
- Geneva: the published approved-software list stops at tax year 2024 (Dr. Tax). Ask the AFC how approval works today.
- Geneva TOU: the AFC's TOU page lists a married couple with one spouse without activity as a compulsory TOU case; the 2026 source-tax directives (p. 51) and the law (LISP art. 4) don't. Taxpresso shows it as "to check with the AFC". See ge/NOTES.md.
- Valais: the 2025 guide says 15 days to correct after online filing, the VSTax FAQ says 10.
- Vaud: the law (LI art. 42a al. 1) and the guide's text give the family deduction only to spouses and single parents with the part of 1.3, while the guide's example 3 and the family regulation (RIFam) give its amount per child to other parents too; the guide and the deduction tables describe the cantonal tax without the LRIPP reduction that the LRIPP and the guide's own worked examples apply; the 2026 guide is not published, so some 2026 amounts are missing. The communal tables (.xls) are read by scripts/vd-communes.mjs, and the LI and RDVE PDFs read fine. See `vd/NOTES.md`, "Open points for the rules".
- Basel-Stadt: the 2025 and 2026 tables of deductions print different yield factors for securities for 2025 (193 provisional, then 345); the guide's worked example is not consistent between its main form and forms K and W; the law and the guide differ on the reduction for a low income (§ 51), the deduction when both spouses work (replacement income) and the deduction for single retirees (StV § 43); the method of the reduction for wealth with a low yield (§ 52) is in no document. Form F gives the childcare limit per child in both columns, while the shared federal rule pools the costs of all children against their limits. See `bs/NOTES.md`, "Open points for the rules" and "Wiring Basel-Stadt in the app".
- Fribourg: no document gives the 2026 communal coefficients (the open data table stops at 2025), so packages/rules-fr does not compute 2026; the detailed income tariff rounds 19 exact halves down and 37 up; for alternating custody the law splits the child deduction in proportion (LICD art. 36 al. 3) while the SCC's source-tax documents give the child to one parent; how the cantonal, communal and church taxes are rounded is not stated. The tariff and the commune and parish tables are read by scripts/fr-tariff.mjs and scripts/fr-communes.mjs (unpdf for the PDFs). See `fr/NOTES.md`, "Open points for the rules".
- Ticino: the income tariff's rates (three decimals) and the tax it prints at each category's top don't always agree (LT art. 35), and the canton's 2025 table (Istruzioni p. 51) follows neither exactly: packages/rules-ti keeps the law's amounts and draws straight lines between them, within 10 centimes of the table below CHF 380,600 and 50 above, and the table prints 34,080.25 where the law prints 34,080.35 (family tariff at 303,900). The 2026 instructions are not published (no 220-day limit for driving in 2026); the multipliers table lists today's communes only (LEMA from 2026, the communes it replaced not named); Modulo 1 exists only as a German translation (ti-2025-form-modulo-1-de). See `ti/NOTES.md`, "Open points for the rules".
- Several agents could read PDF text only through automated summaries. Never copy a number from a NOTES.md file; read it in the document.

## Legal

These are official publications, but redistributing copies inside a commercial product needs a check (Swiss copyright law exempts laws, ordinances and official decisions, not necessarily every guide). Until a lawyer confirms, the shipped extension bundles extracted text with source links, not the original PDFs (SPEC.md section 7b).
