# Zurich (ZH) sources: notes for natural persons, tax years 2025 and 2026

Verified 2026-10-13. Each fact is followed by its official source. The documents are the source of truth; never copy a number from this file.

## Filing channels, tax year 2025
- ZHprivateTax is the online channel; the offline Private Tax software ended with tax year 2025 (https://www.zh.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuern-natuerliche-personen/steuererklaerung-natuerliche-personen.html).
- Login is AGOV with strong two-factor authentication by app or security key (https://www.zh.ch/de/news-uebersicht/medienmitteilungen/2026/01/digitale-steuererklaerung-kanton-zuerich-setzt-auf-den-online-kanal-zhprivatetax.html).
- Online filing needs no printing, signature or postal dispatch; paper is still possible, signed, to the Gemeinden Scan-Center, Postfach 8010 Zürich (https://www.zh.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuern-natuerliche-personen/steuererklaerung-natuerliche-personen.html).
- A third party can be given delegated access in AGOV, or a representative can receive an access code, sent within about 24 hours (same page).
- The e-filing ordinance LS 631.121 covers the canton's own applications: the return counts as filed when it is sent and confirmed electronically, spouses confirm together, attachments go in electronically, and a corrected return can be opened and sent after submission (sections 8 and 9) (https://www.notes.zh.ch/appl/zhlex_r.nsf/WebView/D02E162A60423BC6C1258CB400259461/$File/631.121_18.10.11_129.pdf).

## Third-party software
- Still allowed according to the 6 Jan 2026 press release, with no conditions stated (https://www.zh.ch/de/news-uebersicht/medienmitteilungen/2026/01/digitale-steuererklaerung-kanton-zuerich-setzt-auf-den-online-kanal-zhprivatetax.html).
- Until 31 December 2026 (so for the 2025 return) the 2005 Merkblatt applies: private PC programs may be used, the canton does not certify (homologieren) them, and it checks printouts for scanning if sent by 30 November (https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/vertreter/steuerbuch/zstb-nr-109c-3/ZStB-Nr-109c.3_g%C3%BCltig-bis-31_12_2026.pdf).
- From 1 January 2027 (so for the 2026 return) a new Merkblatt (ZStB 109c.3, 30 September 2026) applies. Printed third-party forms must match the official forms, carry name, AHV number and the provider's name, and may not be stapled (https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/vertreter/steuerbuch/zstb-nr-109c-3/ZStB-Nr-109c-3.pdf).
- Same Merkblatt: returns made with third-party solutions are recognised and may be sent electronically, but there is no automatic right to access. Conditions: valid XML per eCH-0119 or eCH-0276, Zurich tax logic, and the Belegregelwerk; providers apply to the cantonal tax office by the email address given in the document (same PDF).
- The e-filing ordinance LS 631.121 itself does not mention third-party software (https://www.notes.zh.ch/appl/zhlex_r.nsf/WebView/D02E162A60423BC6C1258CB400259461/$File/631.121_18.10.11_129.pdf).

## Deadlines
- Ordinary deadline for the 2025 return: 31 March 2026 (https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/natuerlichepersonen/2025/est-wegleitungen/305_Wegleitung_ZH_2025_HA%20bf%20DEF.pdf).
- The official call to file in 2026 (ZStB 133.1) lists who must file a 2025 return, the special cases and the extension rules (https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/vertreter/steuerbuch/zstb-nr-133-1/ZStB-Nr-133-1_Aufforderung-2026.pdf).
- The 2026 return is due 31 March 2027, online or to the Scan-Center; the Gemeindesteueramt sends the forms in January 2027, and anyone who gets none must ask for them (https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/vertreter/steuerbuch/zstb-nr-133-1/ZStB-Nr-133-1_Aufforderung-2027.pdf).
- Extension: request before the deadline at the Gemeindesteueramt; a timely first request is tacitly granted; latest 30 November (https://www.zh.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/treuhaender/steuerbuch/steuerbuch-definition/zstb-132-1.html).
- Automatic extension to 30 September for self-employed, branch-assigned and non-resident (L and B) cases (same ZStB 132.1 page).
- No extension fee is stated; reminders may not be charged fees (same ZStB 132.1 page).
- Corrections after filing: the portal says they are possible online, by sending again, while the assessment is not final (30 days after receipt) (https://www.zh.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuern-natuerliche-personen/steuererklaerung-natuerliche-personen.html).

## Source tax (Quellensteuer, NOV and rectification)
- NOV request: online via ZHServices by 31 March of the following year; not extendable. If you deregister earlier, by the deregistration date (https://www.zh.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/quellensteuer/nachtraegliche-ordentliche-veranlagung-oder-quellensteuerkorrekt.html).
- Irrevocability of the NOV request is not stated on that page (same page).
- Quellensteuerkorrektur: same 31 March deadline, online via ZHServices, not extendable (https://www.zh.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/quellensteuer/quellensteuerkorrektur-beantragen.html).
- 2026 source-tax tariffs are valid from 1 January 2026; codes A, B, C and H come with and without church tax, G is one table, L, M, N, P and Q are for German cross-border commuters (https://www.zh.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/quellensteuer/quellensteuer-tarife.html).
- The 2025 tables (valid from 1 January 2025) are still listed on the same index page, each as its own PDF, with and without church tax (same page).
- The deductions and municipal multipliers built into the tables are in one sheet per year: 2026 (https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/quellensteuer/quellensteuertarif/2026/grundlagen_und_berechnungsparameter_2026.pdf) and 2025 (https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/quellensteuer/quellensteuertarif/grundlagen_und_berechnungsparameter_2025.pdf).
- Who must file an NOV return for 2026 (gross income, wealth and other-income thresholds, and anyone with a valid NOV request) is set out in the 2027 call to file; once NOV applies it continues in later years until source tax ends. The NOV return is due 31 March 2027, or within 30 days if the forms arrive after the end of February 2027 (https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/vertreter/steuerbuch/zstb-nr-133-1/ZStB-Nr-133-1_Aufforderung-2027.pdf).
- Source tax practice for employees (tariff codes, rate-determining income, NOV) is in ZStB 87.3, valid from 1 January 2024 (https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/vertreter/steuerbuch/zstb-nr-87-3/zstb-nr-87-3.pdf).

## Tariffs, multipliers and deductions (pointers to documents; numbers live in the documents)
- Mechanics: the StG tariff gives the einfache Staatssteuer, and the Kantonsrat sets the cantonal multiplier (Staatssteuerfuss) as a percentage of it for two calendar years at a time (StG section 2) (https://www.notes.zh.ch/appl/zhlex_r.nsf/WebView/A1B72A220CD6E747C1258DC4002A921E/$File/631.1_8.6.97_132.pdf). Municipal and church multipliers are also stated in percent of the einfache Staatssteuer (https://daten.statistik.zh.ch/ogd/daten/ressourcen/KTZH_00000005_00001150.xlsx).
- There are two schedules, a Grundtarif and a Verheiratetentarif; the second also applies to single parents living with children (StG sections 35 and 47) (same PDF).
- Income and wealth tariffs for 2025: StG section 35 and 47 in the text in force on 1 January 2025 (Nachtrag 127) (https://www.notes.zh.ch/appl/zhlex_r.nsf/WebView/B51FEC97C47FE954C1258BBF004304B6/$File/631.1_8.6.97_127.pdf), set by the cold progression ordinance from 1 January 2024 (https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/vertreter/steuerbuch/zstb-nr-48-1/zstb-nr-48-1_gueltig-ab-01-01-2024.pdf).

## Open points for the rules (packages/rules-zh, 2026-10-14)
Found while reading the downloaded documents for the cantonal and communal tax of 2025 and 2026. Each cites the document id and page; the numbers live in the documents.
- Conflict, cantonal multiplier 2026: the Kantonsrat decision for 2026 and 2027 (zh-2026-tariff-staatssteuerfuss-2026-2027 p. 1) sets a different Staatssteuerfuss from the one printed on the 2026 source-tax parameter sheet (zh-2026-source-tax-grundlagen-parameter p. 1, "Steuerfüsse, Staatssteuer"). StG § 2 Abs. 2 (zh-2026-law-steuergesetz-631-1-stand-2026 p. 1) gives the Kantonsrat the power to set it, so rules-zh uses the decision. Open: whether the 2026 source-tax tables were computed with the old multiplier. Ask the cantonal tax office before using the sheet for anything.
- Inconsistency, cold-progression ordinance 2026: its title says it was adopted on 30 June 2025 (zh-2026-tariff-kalte-progression-ab-2026 p. 1), its closing paragraph names the ordinance "vom 28. Juni 2023" (same document, p. 4). The StG footnote 113 (zh-2026-law-steuergesetz-631-1-stand-2026 p. 102) gives 30 June 2025. No amount is affected.
- Not a conflict, childcare 2025: the cold-progression ordinance from 2024 (zh-2025-tariff-kalte-progression-ab-2024 p. 2) prints an older childcare maximum, but a later law (StG footnote 110, zh-2025-law-steuergesetz-631-1-stand-2025 p. 102) replaced § 31 Abs. 1 lit. j from 1 January 2024. The consolidated StG (p. 18) and the 2025 guide (zh-2025-guide-natuerliche-personen p. 18) agree; rules-zh cites them.
- Interpretation, insurance premiums without pension contributions: StG § 31 Abs. 1 lit. g raises the limit by half for taxpayers "ohne Beiträge gemäss lit. d und e" (lit. d also covers AHV), while the schedule and the guide say: when no contributions were made to a pension fund (2. Säule) or pillar 3a (zh-2025-deductions-form-versicherungspraemien p. 1, part B; zh-2025-guide-natuerliche-personen p. 15). rules-zh follows the schedule; for a couple, the higher limit only when neither spouse contributes.
- Not in the documents, rounding: none of the Zurich documents in the library says whether the taxable income and wealth are rounded before the tariff (to 100 or 1,000 francs, as some cantons do), or how the simple tax and the taxes are rounded. The guide's examples print shares to the nearest franc (zh-2026-guide-unterjaehrig p. 37). rules-zh applies the tariff to whole francs, rounds each tax to the centime, and says so in `unverifiedRules`. One official tax calculation (an assessment, or the canton's tax calculator) would settle it.
- Not in the documents, communes with parts: the communal table gives two or three multipliers for some communes (school districts) and two church multipliers for some parishes, numbered 1 to 3 without naming the parts (zh-na-communal-steuerfuesse-seit-2012, sheets STFUSS2025 and STFUSS2026, columns O to Q and I to L). rules-zh asks which part applies and does not compute without it.
- Not in the documents, a worked tax: the 2025 guide's example (zh-2025-guide-natuerliche-personen p. 33 to 38) stops at the taxable income and wealth; no Zurich document in the library prints a sample tax amount. The tests work the taxes out by hand.
- Not covered by rules-zh: property (Liegenschaften), self-employment, part-year liability, capital payments (StG § 37), qualified participations, the support deduction (§ 34 Abs. 1 lit. b), weekly stays away from home, shift work meals.

## Open points from wiring Zurich into the extension (2026-10-14)
Found while giving the Zurich return its federal tax and the extension's estimate, final check and plans. Each cites the document id and page; the numbers live in the documents.
- The cantonal multiplier 2026 (above) is now shown to the user as a rule the documents don't spell out, next to the rounding (rules-zh, `unverifiedRules`), on every 2026 Zurich estimate and in the final check.
- Difference, federal married couple deduction: the federal Form 2a takes it after the net income (chiffre 23), as the Zurich return does on line 365 after 350 (zh-2025-form-steuererklaerung-hauptformular p. 3), while the Geneva form takes it before the medical costs and donations (Geneva's 59.20). The base of the federal medical costs threshold therefore differs between the two forms for a married couple. rules-zh follows the Zurich form; a test pins the difference.
- Difference, federal donations and political party payments: on the Zurich return both the federal donations (324) and the party payments (284) are measured against the Nettoeinkommen (310), and party payments come before it (zh-2025-form-steuererklaerung-hauptformular p. 3); Form 2a agrees (chiffres 15 and 20, ch-2026-ifd-instructions-personnes-physiques). Geneva takes party payments after its line 92.40. rules-zh follows the Zurich form; a test pins the difference.
- Difference, replacement income: Zurich puts daily allowances on 142/143 and unemployment benefits on 140/141, outside the salary (zh-2025-form-steuererklaerung-hauptformular p. 2); they get no professional costs, while Geneva counts them in its flat professional base.
