# Zurich (ZH) sources: notes for natural persons, tax year 2025

Verified 2026-10-13. Each fact is followed by its official source. The documents are the source of truth.

## Filing channels, tax year 2025
- ZHprivateTax is the online channel; the offline Private Tax software ended with tax year 2025 (https://www.zh.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuern-natuerliche-personen/steuererklaerung-natuerliche-personen.html).
- Login is AGOV with strong two-factor authentication by app or security key (https://www.zh.ch/de/news-uebersicht/medienmitteilungen/2026/01/digitale-steuererklaerung-kanton-zuerich-setzt-auf-den-online-kanal-zhprivatetax.html).
- Online filing needs no printing, signature or postal dispatch; paper is still possible, signed, to the Gemeinden Scan-Center, Postfach 8010 Zürich (https://www.zh.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuern-natuerliche-personen/steuererklaerung-natuerliche-personen.html).
- A third party can be given delegated access in AGOV, or a representative can receive an access code, sent within about 24 hours (same page).

## Third-party software
- Still allowed according to the 6 Jan 2026 press release, with no conditions stated (https://www.zh.ch/de/news-uebersicht/medienmitteilungen/2026/01/digitale-steuererklaerung-kanton-zuerich-setzt-auf-den-online-kanal-zhprivatetax.html).
- No approval, certification, interface specification or provider list was found on the zh.ch pages read (https://www.zh.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuern-natuerliche-personen/steuererklaerung-natuerliche-personen.html).
- Ordinance LS 631.121 (Nachtrag 129, marked aktuell) could not be read as text, so its third-party rules are unverified (https://www.zh.ch/de/politik-staat/gesetze-beschluesse/gesetzessammlung/zhlex-ls/erlass-631_121-2011_10_18-2013_01_01-129.html).

## Deadlines
- Ordinary deadline for the 2025 return: 31 March 2026 (https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/natuerlichepersonen/2025/est-wegleitungen/305_Wegleitung_ZH_2025_HA%20bf%20DEF.pdf).
- Extension: request before the deadline at the Gemeindesteueramt; a timely first request is tacitly granted; latest 30 November (https://www.zh.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/treuhaender/steuerbuch/steuerbuch-definition/zstb-132-1.html).
- Automatic extension to 30 September for self-employed, branch-assigned and non-resident (L and B) cases (same ZStB 132.1 page).
- No extension fee is stated; reminders may not be charged fees (same ZStB 132.1 page).
- Corrections after filing: the portal says they are possible while the assessment is not final, citing 30 days after receipt. Confirm before relying on it (https://www.zh.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuern-natuerliche-personen/steuererklaerung-natuerliche-personen.html).

## Source tax (Quellensteuer)
- NOV request: online via ZHServices by 31 March of the following year; not extendable. If you deregister earlier, by the deregistration date (https://www.zh.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/quellensteuer/nachtraegliche-ordentliche-veranlagung-oder-quellensteuerkorrekt.html).
- Irrevocability of the NOV request is not stated on that page (same page).
- Quellensteuerkorrektur: same 31 March deadline, online via ZHServices, not extendable (https://www.zh.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/quellensteuer/quellensteuerkorrektur-beantragen.html).
- 2026 source-tax tariffs are valid from 1 January 2026; codes A, B, C and H come with and without church tax, G is one table, L, M, N, P and Q are for German cross-border commuters (https://www.zh.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/quellensteuer/quellensteuer-tarife.html).

## New for 2025 and 2026
- Private Tax (offline) discontinued from tax year 2025 (https://www.zh.ch/de/news-uebersicht/medienmitteilungen/2026/01/digitale-steuererklaerung-kanton-zuerich-setzt-auf-den-online-kanal-zhprivatetax.html).
- Weisung on the filing procedure (ZStB 132.1) in force 1 January 2026; it governs the 2026 filing round for tax year 2025 (https://www.zh.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/treuhaender/steuerbuch/steuerbuch-definition/zstb-132-1.html).
- Employee professional-expense flat rates (ZStB 26.1) apply from tax period 2026; the mileage flat rate rises from 2026 (https://www.zh.ch/de/news-uebersicht/mitteilungen/2026/steuern-finanzen/ueberblick-ueber-aenderungen-im-zuercher-steuerbuch-.html).
- New Weisung on social deductions and tariffs from tax period 2026 (https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/vertreter/steuerbuch/zstb-nr-34-1/ZStB-Nr-34-1%203.pdf).
- The canton does not raise rental values (Eigenmietwerte) for 2026 (https://www.zh.ch/de/news-uebersicht/mitteilungen/2026/steuern-finanzen/ueberblick-ueber-aenderungen-im-zuercher-steuerbuch-.html).
- Steuergesetz Nachtrag 132 published 26 March 2026 (https://www.zh.ch/de/politik-staat/gesetze-beschluesse/gesetzessammlung/zhlex-ls/erlass-631_1-1997_06_08-1999_01_01-132.html).

## Tariffs and rates (numbers live in the documents)
- Income and wealth tariffs are set in StG sections 35 and 47; both Weisungen contain rules only, not tables (https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/vertreter/steuerbuch/zstb-nr-34-1/ZStB-Nr-34-1%203.pdf).
- Municipal rates exist only as an interactive current-year page; no downloadable file found (https://www.zh.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuerstatistiken/aktuelle-gemeinde-steuerfuesse.html).
- Employee expense rules (commuting, meals, other costs, side income) are in the 2025 Berufsauslagen form (https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/natuerlichepersonen/2025/est-formulare/360%20Berufsauslagen%20ZH%202025%20HA%20DEF.pdf).

## Forms and other rules
- Securities schedule valued at 31 December 2025, with a Verrechnungssteuer refund request (https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/natuerlichepersonen/2025/est-formulare/340%20WV%20ZH%202025%20HA%20DEF.pdf).
- Crypto: wealth tax, wallet printout at year end, private price gains income-tax free (https://www.zh.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/treuhaender/steuerbuch/steuerbuch-definition/zstb-16-5.html).
- DA-1 covers US-withheld securities income and DA-3 licence and service income for natural persons (https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/natuerlichepersonen/2025/vst-formulare/438%20Wegleitung%20DA1_DA-3%202025%20bf%20DEF.pdf).
- Fraud warning: tax authorities never request payment or tax details by email (https://www.zh.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuern-natuerliche-personen/steuererklaerung-natuerliche-personen.html).
