# Geneva (GE) source notes for individuals, verified 2026-10-13

## Filing channels, tax year 2025
- Routes: GeTax 2025 desktop software (Windows, macOS, Linux builds), the online return in e-démarches (no install), or a paper form on request by phone or at the Hôtel des Finances. (https://www.ge.ch/getax/telecharger-installer-getax-particuliers ; https://www.ge.ch/getax/declaration-ligne-particuliers ; https://www.ge.ch/remplir-ma-declaration-impot-tant-que-particulier/comment-remplir-ma-declaration-impot)

- Fully electronic ("zéro papier") only if all supporting documents are uploaded as PDF; otherwise the summary page is printed, signed and posted. (https://www.ge.ch/espace-fiscal-e-demarches/gerer-mes-declarations-impot)
- GeTax upload ("Déposer - Téléverser"): no account needed; the signed summary page must be posted, and its return date is the filing date. (https://www.ge.ch/getax/retour-declarations-internet)
- GeTax deposit via e-démarches: needs an e-démarches account; gives a timestamped receipt and transaction number; no postal mail. (https://www.ge.ch/getax/retour-declarations-internet)
- The online return pre-fills only from last year's declaration, and only if that one was sent electronically. (https://www.ge.ch/getax/declaration-ligne-particuliers)
- Supporting documents can be attached as PDF; GeTax rejects a file already imported, even if renamed. (https://www.ge.ch/getax/retour-declarations-internet)

## Source tax
- DRIS/TOU form due by 31 March of the year after withholding; for 2025 withholding that is 31 March 2026 (inferred). Filed online via e-démarches, or on paper if it came by post. (https://www.ge.ch/demande-rectification-taxation-ordinaire-ulterieure)
- DRIS is optional unless TOU is mandatory; if not filed, the withheld tax stands unless the AFC starts a correction. (https://www.ge.ch/demande-rectification-taxation-ordinaire-ulterieure)
- TOU on request: written request by 31 March of the following year; once filed it can't be withdrawn (RISP art. 8 al. 1). Added 2026-10-13 with the planning tools. (https://silgeneve.ch/legis/data/rsg_d3_20p01.htm)
- The 2026 directives say the same, "même si la taxation est finalement défavorable", and that TOU then applies every following year until source taxation ends; late or informal requests are refused (ch. 8.2.3, p. 52). (https://www.ge.ch/document/42009/annexe/file/580297)
- Compulsory TOU for residents (LISP art. 4 al. 1; RISP art. 7; directives 2026 ch. 8.2.1 p. 51): gross salary taxed at source over a threshold, other income not taxed at source over a threshold, taxable wealth, a TOU the year before. The thresholds and the 2026 wealth deductions are in those documents. (https://silgeneve.ch/legis/data/rsg_d3_20.htm)
- CONFLICT, open: the AFC page "Quand demander ou annoncer une TOU" also lists "votre conjoint (couple marié) est sans activité" as a compulsory case for residents; the 2026 directives (p. 51) and LISP art. 4 don't. Taxpresso shows it as "to check with the AFC", never as compulsory. Ask the AFC which is right. (https://www.ge.ch/demande-rectification-taxation-ordinaire-ulterieure/quand-demander-annoncer-taxation-ordinaire-ulterieure)
- The same page also lists "vous avez une autre activité à l'étranger" as a compulsory case, which neither the directives' list (2026 p. 51, 2025 p. 50) nor LISP art. 4 names (income not taxed at source of CHF 3,000 or more would cover most of it). Taxpresso doesn't prepare foreign income in Geneva (it is not supported), so it never relies on this case. Noted 2026-10-14 by the source-tax agent.
- Geneva computes source tax with the annual model: the rate comes from the year's income, the year's tax is that income times the rate (directives 2026 ch. 2 and 4, p. 9 and p. 14). With tariff C the tax withheld can be corrected afterwards on both spouses' real incomes (ch. 8.1.3, p. 49). (https://www.ge.ch/document/42009/annexe/file/580297)
- The tariffs are published as PDF and as ASCII files in the ESTV record format (tar25GE.zip, tar26GE.zip), only without church tax (code letter N). The ASCII and PDF tables of 2025 and 2026 were compared row by row on 2026-10-13 (A0, B0 to B5, C0 to C5, H1 to H5; 1,441 rows each): no difference. A1 to A5 and D are separate annexes, G and the cross-border tariffs are not used. (https://www.ge.ch/publication?organisation=51&type=183&titre=perception)
- The 2026 directives' web page summary still says "valables dès le 1er janvier 2025"; the PDF itself is the 2026 edition (dated 9 February 2026). (https://www.ge.ch/document/directives-concernant-imposition-source-2026)
- The 2025 source-tax employee form goes to the employer, not the tax office; changes must be reported within 14 days; no deadline is stated. (https://www.ge.ch/document/37885/annexe/file/371410)
- 2026 withholding tables A, B, C and H are published (dated December 2025, page updated February 2026). (https://www.ge.ch/document/41802/telecharger)

## Law and e-filing basis
- LPFisc art. 27: return deadline at least 30 days from delivery of the form, with extensions under al. 2; art. 29A: electronic filing; art. 49: 30 days to appeal. (https://silgeneve.ch/legis/data/rsg_d3_17.htm)
- LIPP: spouses' income and wealth are added (art. 8); joint taxation and income splitting (art. 41, 66); tax period is the calendar year (art. 61); no e-filing provision. (https://silgeneve.ch/legis/data/rsg_d3_08.htm)

Added by the main session.

## Securities list (état des titres): conflicts and open points (added 2026-10-13 by the securities agent)
- Ask the human: where crypto goes. The guide says to multiply the ESTV tax rate by the units held and report the result "dans l'état des titres, sous Compte bancaire ou postal" (ge-2025-guide-declaration-pp p. 40), while the heading of the 2025 form's securities sheet lists "crypto-monnaies" among the titres suisses et étrangers (ge-2025-declaration-specimen p. 23, sheet F3). The totals are the same either way; Taxpresso follows the guide (with the accounts, F2) until the AFC says otherwise.
- Wording conflict, the law wins: the guide says the IA is not levied on interest "inférieurs à 200" francs (ge-2025-guide-declaration-pp p. 40); the law says interest that "n'excède pas 200 francs" for a calendar year (ch-na-law-lia art. 5 al. 1 let. c, p. 4). Taxpresso never decides it: it takes the IA the bank shows, and only flags Swiss interest above the threshold with no IA as something to check.
- The guide's short link to the declaration specimen (www.ge.ch/c/imp-spde25, p. 9) answers 404; the specimen is on https://www.ge.ch/document/declaration-impot-2025-documents-envoyes (ge-2025-declaration-specimen). The rubric codes the état des titres feeds (14.00, 16.40, 16.70, 52.11, 55.10, 56.20, 56.30) come from it; the guide itself never names 14.00.
- Not published yet, so Taxpresso fails closed for tax year 2026: the official exchange rates at 31 December 2026 and the 2026 averages (ge-2025-taux-donnees-fiscales gives 2025 and earlier), and the list of non-recoverable foreign taxes for income of 2026 (the DFF annex in force on 1 February 2026 covers income of 2025, ch-2025-oiis-dff-annexe p. 2). Add both in January and February 2027.

## Added and found while building the tax engine (2026-10-14)
- Added ge-na-law-lcp (LCP, rsGE D 3 05): the communal centimes are levied on the cantonal base tax (art. 293); the privileged share of each base tax goes to the commune of residence and the rest to the communes concerned in proportion to the income and wealth attributable to each (art. 295A); the taxe personnelle and its exemptions (art. 374 to 377). (https://silgeneve.ch/legis/data/rsg_d3_05.htm)
- Added ge-2026-part-privilegiee-communique: the 2026 privileged share of every commune, fixed by the Conseil d'Etat on 15 October 2025, p. 11. The AFC table ge-2025-communal-centimes-pdf stops at 2025; the 2027 shares were fixed on 7 October 2026. (https://www.ge.ch/document/40923/telecharger)
- Rounding not stated in the documents: the tariff (ge-2025-icc-tariff-pdf) gives every amount to 5 centimes, but not how the cantonal centimes, the 12 % reduction, the home care centime and the communal shares are rounded. The engine rounds each line to the nearest 5 centimes, halves up, and flags the rule; check it against a real bordereau.
- Rubric codes: the engine uses the codes printed on the 2025 form (ge-2025-declaration-specimen): 14.00 for accounts and securities, in the income (PG2) and wealth (PG4) columns; 55.10 for private debts and their interest (E1; 55.00 on the recapitulation); 56.20 and 56.30 (F1); 32.40 for the 3a of a self-employed person (B2). The guide's text names the donations 73.10 (ICC) and 73.20 (IFD), p. 15, while the form prints one rubric, 73.00, with an ICC and an IFD column (p. 3): the engine uses 73.00. Check every code on the 2026 form.
- PG3 (specimen p. 3) puts 95.00 revenu net total after the social deductions 61.20 to 61.30, then 97.00 and 99.00 revenu imposable: the engine's total income is 95.00, its net income the subtotal before 61.xx.
- The guide's code ranges for the two-earner deduction ("31.10 à 31.63", p. 21) follow the form's order; the engine reads them as every employment deduction before 31.90, which is what LIFD art. 33 al. 2 says for the IFD.
- Life insurance limit 52.15 (p. 28): "augmentée de moitié" gives a half franc for the per-charge amount; the engine rounds it down and flags it.
- The tax shield (LIPP art. 60, guide p. 51) is not computed: when it may apply the engine computes no ICC at all.

## Travel, meals and shared custody (added 2026-10-14 by the travel, meals and custody agent)
- Travel to work, ICC: LIPP art. 29 al. 1 let. a allows the "frais de déplacement nécessaires" up to 500 francs (ge-na-law-lipp), indexed to 534 for 2025 "quel que soit le mode de transport choisi" (ge-2025-guide-declaration-pp p. 11, 19 and 21). Neither says how a bike or a car is priced before that cap; the guide prices them only for the IFD (p. 20). Taxpresso prices them as the federal tax does, then applies the 534 cap, and flags the rule as not confirmed. Check with the AFC or GeTax.
- Meals, ICC: allowed only when the trip by public transport takes over two hours a day (guide p. 19 and the examples p. 22), at the federal rates. When the person has no public transport they can use at all, the guide doesn't say whether the condition is met: Taxpresso does not count Geneva meals then (fail closed) and says so.
- Days worked from home: no travel or meal deduction for them (guide p. 20). Taxpresso asks the way to work on most days; "from home almost every day" counts neither.
- Shared custody and the ICC: with child support (contribution d'entretien), the parent who receives it is the one who mainly supports the child, for the splitting (p. 45) and for the family charge (p. 46); without it and when both support the child, the charge is shared half and half (p. 46). Taxpresso asks the question once and applies it to the ICC and the IFD (ch-na-circulaire-30-epoux-famille for the IFD). Partial splitting (55.56 %) still assumes the judgment or signed agreement of p. 45 and is flagged.
- The 2025 car rate in the guide (0.70 a km, p. 20) is the 2025 figure; the ESTV circular gives 0.75 for 2026 (ch-2026-ifd-progression-deductions p. 1). Not a conflict: a new tax year.

## Tax at source from plain facts (added 2026-10-14 by the source-tax agent)
Taxpresso no longer asks whether someone is taxed at source, which barème applies or how much tax was withheld: it asks plain facts and works the rest out, from these documents (both years read; the pages differ by one in places).
- Who is taxed at source: foreign workers without a C permit, on their salary and on the benefits that replace it (unemployment, sickness, accident, invalidity, maternity): ge-na-law-lisp art. 1 al. 1, art. 2 al. 2 let. a and b; ge-na-law-risp art. 3; ch-na-law-lifd art. 83 al. 1, art. 84 al. 2 (p. 49); ge-2026-source-directives p. 5 ("salarié" includes those with replacement income), p. 6 (ch. 1.1.1), p. 11 (ch. 3.1.3.2); the same pages in ge-2025-source-directives. The old question was asked only of people with a salary, so a spouse on unemployment benefits was never asked and the couple showed "only one of you is taxed at source": that was the code's gap, not a document conflict.
- Not taxed at source: Swiss citizens and C permit holders, and a spouse living with one of them (LISP art. 1 al. 2; LIFD art. 83 al. 2; directives 2026 p. 6, ch. 1.1.2); a salary paid directly by an employer abroad, unless a Swiss branch, the Swiss company of a secondment or a Swiss hirer pays it in the end (ge-na-law-risp art. 5; directives p. 6). A C permit, Swiss citizenship or a marriage to a Swiss or C permit holder during the year makes the whole year ordinary, and the tax withheld counts towards it (ge-na-law-risp art. 10; directives 2026 p. 56, ch. 9.3.1): Taxpresso asks the situation on 31 December and says so when a certificate shows box 12.
- AVS pensions, full invalidity pensions and pension fund retirement pensions are not replacement income and are taxed ordinarily; partial invalidity pensions are (directives 2026 p. 11; RISP art. 3). Taxpresso doesn't ask which kind of pension it is: for someone without a C permit whose only such income is a pension, it asks whether tax was taken at source (the statement shows it).
- Barème, derived (ge-na-law-risp art. 1 al. 1; directives 2026 ch. 5.2.1.1 to 5.2.1.5, p. 25 to 27, and 2025 p. 24 to 26): A0 alone, in alternating custody (ch. 5.5.2) or living with the children's other parent unmarried (ch. 5.5.1); H plus children for a single parent with the children in main custody; B plus children when the spouse has no income, C plus children when the spouse has a salary, self-employment or replacement income (note 3 of the table); at most 5 children (ch. 5.5.5); N, because Geneva levies no church tax at source (ch. 6.10, p. 42; the tariff files list N only). A barème read from a payslip (plan.source_tax_tariff) wins over the derived one, and both show.
- Open points, where Taxpresso fails closed: a spouse whose only income is a pension (the directives' text for B reads "pas de revenus (salaire, activité indépendante, indemnités ..., etc.)", while note 3 and RISP art. 1 let. b count activity and replacement income only: no barème derived); a married couple whose children live in alternating custody (no barème in the directives); children of 25 or more who study are counted only through a DRIS or TOU (p. 27 and 29), and adult children under 25 count only under three conditions (p. 26): Taxpresso counts every child it is told about and says so; an arrival from abroad during the year (the CHF 120,000 is annualized, RISP art. 7 al. 5): only a known gross at or over the limit makes TOU compulsory, otherwise it stays unknown; a separation from a Swiss or C permit spouse during the year (RISP art. 11) is not asked.
- Barème G for benefits an insurance pays itself (directives 2026 p. 28, ch. 5.2.2.5; p. 23, ch. 4.2.12): no table in the library, so only the fund's attestation gives that tax (new key replacement.source_tax; LIFD art. 88 al. 1 let. b).
- The tax withheld: the salary certificate (box 12) and the benefits attestation first; else the official tables (ge-<year>-source-tariff-ascii) on the gross salary with the barème, flagged as an estimate everywhere (annual model, directives 2026 p. 9 and 14); else Taxpresso says which document gives it.
- Compulsory TOU: the CHF 120,000 counts the gross taxed at source, salary and replacement income (RISP art. 7 al. 1 and 2, LISP art. 2 al. 2 let. a and b); the code counted the salary only before. The 2025 amounts are the 2025 directives' (p. 50: wealth deductions 87'632, 175'264 and 43'816); the 2026 ones p. 51.
- TOU already in place is a fact: it stays every year while taxed at source, the spouse included, and the AFC sends the declaration each year with no new DRIS/TOU form (LISP art. 4 al. 5, art. 5 al. 5; directives 2026 p. 51 and 52; ge-na-tou-quand-page). Compulsory TOU is announced by 31 March. Only when none of the compulsory cases applies is TOU a choice, with the comparison and its irrevocability (RISP art. 8 al. 1; directives p. 52).
- "Only one of you is taxed at source" now shows only when one spouse is taxed at source and the other is not while having income of their own (for example an international civil servant, who answers the open question; directives 2026 p. 25 and 26). A spouse without income is barème B, part of the same TOU (LISP art. 4 al. 3, art. 5 al. 2), not a mixed couple.
- Where it shows: the facts are sentences in "About you" (extension/src/journey.ts, source_tax, each with its source; get_status gives them to Claude), and TOU in place or compulsory is a fact there too; the TOU "Decide" card shows only for a real choice (docs/design/simple.md). Checked against main on 14 October 2026 with extension/test/record-source-tax.test.ts (answers drawn per question, hashed records): of 136 households (the four synthetic ones and 12 per canton), the 103 not taxed at source give the same 2,715 tool results, dashboard states and API responses, once the question counts and the answers listed are set aside (one question replaced another); the 33 drawn as taxed at source change by design, some no longer taxed at source (a spouse who is Swiss, LIFD art. 83 al. 2).

## Pension capital, 98.12 (added 2026-10-14 by the pension capital agent)
- The rule: lump sums from a pension fund, vested benefits or pillar 3a (LIPP art. 25) are taxed apart from the other income, a full year's tax, at the rate of a fifth of the scale of art. 41; every lump sum of the year is added up for the rate, the spouses' too, and the splitting of art. 41 al. 2 to 4 still applies; no social deduction (ge-na-law-lipp art. 45). Declared at 98.12, "Prestations en capital du 2ème et 3ème pilier A imposées au 1/5ème du barème", among the information of PG2 (ge-2025-declaration-specimen p. 2; ge-2025-guide-declaration-pp p. 13: "imposées séparément au moment de leur versement").
- Added ge-na-law-ldirpp (LDIRPP, rsGE D 3 06): art. 1 reduces the direct tax on the income of individuals by 12 %, except the communal centimes; the tariff calls it "la loi du 26 septembre 1999" (ge-2025-icc-tariff-pdf p. 2 and 4). Added ge-na-law-lcacant (LCACant, rsGE D 3 07): art. 2 levies 47.5 cantonal centimes and 1 for home care on "les impôts cantonaux sur le revenu et la fortune". Both laws are why the capital tax carries the same surcharges as the income tax. (https://silgeneve.ch/legis/data/rsg_d3_06.htm ; https://silgeneve.ch/legis/data/rsg_d3_07.htm)

## Arrival from abroad and departure abroad (added 2026-10-14 by the arrival and departure agent)
- The rule: the tax liability starts on the day of arrival and ends on the day of departure (ge-na-law-lipp art. 7 al. 1 and 2; ch-na-law-lifd art. 8, p. 5). Only the income of that period is taxed; periodic income is converted to twelve months for the rate, non-periodic income is not (LIPP art. 62 al. 3; LIFD art. 40 al. 3, p. 33), and "l'alinéa 3 s'applique par analogie aux déductions" (LIPP art. 62 al. 4). The social deductions follow the situation at the end of the liability, are given in proportion and count in full for the rate (LIPP art. 65 al. 1 and 2; LIFD art. 35 al. 2 and 3, p. 29). Wealth is taken at the end of the liability and only the tax for that period is levied (LIPP art. 64 al. 1 and 3). The guide says it plainly: an arrival from abroad is taxed on the income from the day of arrival to 31 December, "au taux d'un revenu annuel", on the wealth on 31 December (ge-2025-guide-declaration-pp p. 53); a departure abroad on the income from 1 January to the day of departure, at the same rate, on the wealth on the day of departure (p. 54). The 2025 guide words both for an arrival or departure in 2026; they are the law's rules, the same for 2025. Code: packages/rules-ge, engine/part-year.ts; the federal part in packages/rules-ch, ifd/part-year.ts.
- Counting a year: the federal guide for new taxpayers gives the formula, periodic income x 360 / days of tax liability (ch-2026-afc-guide-futur-contribuable p. 30, ch. 3.1.1.3; p. 31 for an arrival, p. 40 for a departure); the source-tax directives count 30 days a month, February and 31-day months included (ge-2026-source-directives p. 21, ch. 4.2.9; 2025: p. 20). Neither the Geneva guide nor the LIPP says how the days are counted for the ordinary tax: Taxpresso counts them as those two documents do, and flags it in every result.
- Periodic or not: the documents name salaries and pensions as periodic (the federal guide, p. 30) and list what isn't (bonuses, gratifications, overtime and holiday pay, board fees, severance pay, employee participations: ge-2026-source-directives p. 13, ch. 4.1.1; capital gains and lottery wins: Form. 2a p. 1). Taxpresso converts the salary with its contributions and work costs, pensions, and what is paid by the month (health and accident premiums, the premium subsidy, childcare); it takes the rest as it is (pillar 3a, buy-ins, medical costs, donations, savings income and costs). The classification of each deduction is not spelled out: a reading, flagged. A salary certificate with an amount in boxes 3 to 7 is not prepared (fail closed): that pay isn't converted, and how the contributions on it count is not said.
- The deductions of the period, cantonal: only the social deductions are reduced (LIPP art. 65 al. 2): the family charges (61.25), in proportion to the days. The other cantonal limits (the flat professional costs 31.50, the two-earner deduction 31.95, the health premium cap 52.21) are applied as for a year, since the law reduces only the social deductions; a reading, flagged. Federal: see docs/sources/ch/NOTES.md. Pillar 3a keeps the whole year's maximum (ch-na-law-opp3 art. 7 al. 1, "par année").
- Open point, wealth (fail closed): for part of a year, LIPP art. 64 al. 3 levies only the tax for the period, and art. 65 al. 2 gives the social deductions (the wealth deduction 51.50 is one, LIPP art. 58) in proportion and in full for the rate. Whether both apply to wealth, and how the days are counted for it, no document says. When the wealth after the full deduction is zero, every reading gives no wealth tax, and Taxpresso computes the return; above it, the cantonal tax is not computed and says why (the federal tax has no wealth tax).
- The taxe personnelle: due "dès le premier jour de résidence", 25 francs, "perçue annuellement" (ge-2026-source-directives p. 54, ch. 8.8; 2025: p. 53; ge-na-law-lcp art. 375). Taxpresso counts it in full; that it isn't reduced for part of a year is a reading, flagged. Its exemption (LCP art. 377 let. c) depends on the taxable income: when the income of the period and the year's give different answers, the cantonal tax is not computed (fail closed).
- The rate: the ICC base is the scale's tax on the year's taxable income (times the splitting share for a couple) applied to the period's taxable income, period x tax / rate income, to 5 centimes, the way the splitting example works (ge-2025-icc-tariff-pdf p. 3). The federal tax: the scale's tax on the year's income applied to the period's (both without the fraction under CHF 100), rounded down to 5 centimes, the reduction per child in proportion, also rounded down. Circular 30's example (ch-na-circulaire-30-epoux-famille p. 26) shows its rate to three decimals and rounds its amounts to 5 centimes; how the rate itself is rounded is in no document: flagged.
- The form: the day of arrival goes on page 1 ("Date d'arrivée", ge-2025-declaration-specimen p. 1), now in the copy panel (PG1). On PG3, "95.00 Revenu net total, le cas échéant, revenu net pour le taux", "97.00 Part proportionnelle du revenu non imposable" and "99.00 Revenu imposable" (specimen p. 3): Taxpresso gives 95.00 the year's net income for the rate, 97.00 the difference, 99.00 the period's taxable income. How GeTax and the online declaration work 95.00 and 97.00 out for an arrival is not documented: the official PDF check shows them and never compares them; it compares 99.00. A departure is declared at the counter at least 15 days before leaving, with the departure questionnaire (questionnaire de départ) and this year's declaration from GeTax (menu Fichier > Déclaration Départ/Décès): guide p. 54. The specimen has no line for the day of departure.
- Tax at source and TOU: with the day of arrival known, the CHF 120,000 of a compulsory TOU is decidable: for less than 12 months the threshold is worked out "en annualisant les revenus périodiques (salaire) à l'exclusion des revenus non périodiques" (ge-2026-source-directives p. 51, ch. 8.2.1; 2025: p. 50). Whether the non-periodic pay is then added unconverted or left out is not said: Taxpresso reads it both ways and says TOU is compulsory, or not, only when both readings agree; benefits replacing a salary keep it undecided. The tax withheld, when estimated from the tables, takes the rate of the salary converted to a year (ch. 4.2.5 and 4.2.10, p. 16 and 22; 2025: p. 16 and 21). On a departure, a TOU, compulsory or asked for, goes with the departure form, before leaving (ch. 8.5, p. 54; 2025: p. 53): its deadline is the day of departure, not 31 March. Someone who worked in Geneva as a non-resident before arriving (ch. 8.2.3.3, p. 53) is not prepared: a salary certificate that starts before the day of arrival, or ends after the day of departure, fails closed.
- The plans: a pension fund buy-in by someone arriving from abroad who was never in a Swiss pension fund is limited in the first five years (ch-na-law-lpp art. 79b al. 2, "Le Conseil fédéral règle le rachat"; that ordinance is not in the library): Taxpresso says so and leaves the amount to the pension fund's statement.
- Couples: spouses are taxed together (LIPP art. 66 al. 1), the scales and social deductions on the situation at the end of the liability (LIPP art. 65 al. 1). Spouses who arrived or left on different days are not spelled out (circular 30, p. 7, ch. 2.2, covers a spouse liable only through economic ties, LIFD art. 4 and 5): fail closed.
- Fails closed, said plainly: spouses on different days; a salary certificate before the arrival or after the departure; self-employment; benefits replacing a salary; pay in boxes 3 to 7; an AVS or AI pension (the deduction 61.30 depends on a net income the documents don't define for part of a year); wealth above the deduction (the cantonal tax only); a taxe personnelle settled differently by the two incomes; a lump sum of pension capital in the year (arrival, as before; departure: taxed at source around a departure, directives p. 39, ch. 6.5); a property abroad in the same year; someone taxed at source and not in TOU (the tax at source is final, as before); every canton other than Geneva (the question still says not supported).
- Nothing else changed, checked against main (9bb00f4) on 14 October 2026 with extension/test/record-years.test.ts (TAXPRESSO_RECORD_SPLIT=1, Dupont, Ribeiro and 20 seeded households in each of the 12 cantons): 17,028 records for 2026 and 14,463 for 2025 before. - The values come from the AFC, never from Taxpresso: the tax value (valeur fiscale) of a villa or a PPE flat is raised by 12 % for 2025 under the LEFI, with exceptions, and indexed each year from 2026; the AFC sends it before 31 March (ge-na-valeur-fiscale-page, "Indexation de la valeur fiscale"; ge-na-law-lefi; ge-na-law-rivfi). The rental value (valeur locative) is the AFC's too (ge-na-revenu-immobilier-page). Taxpresso reads both from the AFC's letter or the avis de taxation immobilier (the new kind property_values); until it is in, the user's own figures (plan.property_value, plan.property_rental_value, plan.property_rent, plan.mortgage_balance, plan.mortgage_interest) count as estimates and say so. Added ge-na-law-lefi and ge-na-law-rivfi for that reason only.
- The abattement: 4 % a year of continuous use by the same owner, at most 40 % (LIPP art. 50 let. e), counted from the year the owner moved in to the tax year, both included, as the guide's example does ("Villa occupée de 2010 à 2025, soit 16 ans", p. 33). The guide applies it to the tax value and to the cantonal rental value ("ICC - Valeur locative après abattement", p. 33 and 34); the federal tax takes the gross rental value. Taxpresso matches the guide's own example to the franc (capital 840,000; rental value 35,856, 21,514 after the abattement; flat upkeep 7,171 federal and 5,379 cantonal: packages/rules-ge/test/property.test.ts). Years of a spouse, parent or sibling the home came from count too (art. 50 let. e): Taxpresso takes the year the user gives, and says so.
- Upkeep, one's own home: the flat deduction is 15 % (building of 10 years or less on 1 January) or 25 % of the cantonal rental value after the abattement for the ICC (LIPP art. 34 let. d; guide p. 33), 10 % or 20 % of the gross rental value for the IFD (ch-2025- and ch-2026-ifd-instructions-personnes-physiques p. 6; LIFD art. 32 al. 4). Actual costs are allowed instead, chosen each year and for each tax (ge-na-information-1-2021-frais-entretien p. 2 and 4): Taxpresso takes the higher of the two in each tax and says which. A property let: actual costs only for the ICC ("Aucun forfait n'est applicable pour l'ICC", guide p. 35), the actual costs or 10 % or 20 % of the rents for the IFD. The invoices are kept, not attached; the management account (compte de gestion) of a property let is attached.
- Conflict, noted and settled by the stronger document: the AFC's page on deductible charges (ge-na-charges-deductibles-page, "La déduction forfaitaire") gives one flat rate, "10% du revenu immobilier pour les bâtiments de 10 ans ou moins, 20% au-delà", without saying it is the federal one, while the law (LIPP art. 34 let. d), the guide (p. 33 and its example p. 34) and information 1/2021 (p. 4) give 15 % and 25 % for the ICC of one's own home. Taxpresso follows the law, the guide and the information.
- The taux d'effort (LIPP art. 24 al. 2): the cantonal rental value of the main home can't exceed 20 % of the gross income, at least of the first tax-free bracket (x 2 for spouses and single parents), and the limited value counts only if the interest financing the home is not higher. The guide (p. 51) computes it on "les revenus bruts totaux (code 91.00)", which includes the rental value (15.00, specimen p. 2), and sends to the AFC's calculator for the amount. When the rental value is at most 20 % of 91.00, nothing changes and Taxpresso says so; when it is above, how much it comes down is not spelled out (the reduction changes 91.00 itself), so the cantonal tax fails closed with that reason and the federal tax is computed; when the interest is higher than the limited value however it is read (20 % of 91.00, or 25 % of the income without the rental value), it doesn't apply, and that is said. Conflict: the guide gives the minimum for single people and twice it for couples and single parents; LIPP art. 24 al. 2 refers to art. 41, whose al. 4 (partial splitting) gives 1.8 times. Taxpresso follows the law (law/property.ts, EFFORT).
- Debt interest: up to the gross income from wealth, the property's rental value or rents included, plus 50,000 (guide p. 37; LIFD art. 33 al. 1 let. a). The mortgage statement (the new kind mortgage_statement) puts the debt and the interest on E1 55.20; the engine adds the interest to the other debt interest, as 55.10 and 55.20 together are capped.
- The impôt immobilier complémentaire (ge-na-law-lcp art. 76; ge-na-iic-page): 1 per mille of the tax value, 0.2 per mille for an individual's main home from tax period 2025 (guide p. 36), on the value without the abattement and without debts. It is a tax of its own: shown apart, never in the total. Its rounding is not in the documents: Taxpresso rounds to 5 centimes and says so. A building with a high energy performance standard (HPE, THPE) attested by the OCEN is exempt for 20 years (LCP art. 78): said, never assumed.
- The reform of the rental value accepted by the people and the cantons in September 2025: the Federal Council set its entry into force on 1 January 2029 (ch-na-reforme-valeur-locative-2029), so 2025 and 2026 keep the rental value and the upkeep deduction, as the 2025 and 2026 federal instructions say. A tax year from 2029 fails closed (rentalValueTaxed in rules-ch).
- The plans: pillar 3a and a buy-in are worked out at the household's real rate with the property counted. Paying off the mortgage through pillar 3a (amortissement indirect) is spelled out in no document in the library: left out.
- Fails closed, said plainly (no tax computed unless said): a property in another canton; more than one property in Switzerland; one bought, sold, moved into or out of during the year, and any gain; a property let part of the year, partly let or let for holidays; used for a business; held through a company; land or a building being built; a usufruct or a right of residence; one shared with someone outside the household; works that add value, a demolition or a listed building; a subsidy for works or subsidised housing; costs carried over; a property let in another Geneva commune than the home (the communal tax is then shared between communes: ge-na-law-lcp art. 293 and 295A), the taux d'effort when it lowers the rental value (the cantonal tax only), a home moved into in the tax year, a year of arrival from abroad or departure abroad, tax years from 2029, and every canton other than Geneva (the question still says not supported). Someone taxed at source with a property: the source-tax directives make TOU compulsory for an owner (ge-2026-source-directives p. 6 and 51), which Taxpresso doesn't prepare with a property yet. Open point: it could become one more reason for TOU instead.
- The questions ask only plain facts (docs/design/simple.md): what the property is (a house or a flat the household lives in, or one let), whose it is, the year they moved in, whether the building was more than 10 years old on 1 January, where a property let lies, what of a list applies, and whether a mortgage is on it. Every figure comes from its documents (property_values, property_rent_statement, property_upkeep_invoices, ppe_charges_statement, mortgage_statement, with their official names and ge.ch pages) or, until they are in, from what the user says in the chat, as estimates.
