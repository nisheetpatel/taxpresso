# Geneva (GE) source notes for individuals, verified 2026-10-13

## Filing channels, tax year 2025
- Routes: GeTax 2025 desktop software (Windows, macOS, Linux builds), the online return in e-démarches (no install), or a paper form on request by phone or at the Hôtel des Finances. (https://www.ge.ch/getax/telecharger-installer-getax-particuliers ; https://www.ge.ch/getax/declaration-ligne-particuliers ; https://www.ge.ch/remplir-ma-declaration-impot-tant-que-particulier/comment-remplir-ma-declaration-impot)
- Online login: ge.ch/connexion, then Mon espace fiscal, e-démarches fiscales, Gérer mes déclarations. The login method (ID, code or e-ID) is not stated. (https://www.ge.ch/espace-fiscal-e-demarches/gerer-mes-declarations-impot)
- Fully electronic ("zéro papier") only if all supporting documents are uploaded as PDF; otherwise the summary page is printed, signed and posted. (https://www.ge.ch/espace-fiscal-e-demarches/gerer-mes-declarations-impot)
- GeTax upload ("Déposer - Téléverser"): no account needed; the signed summary page must be posted, and its return date is the filing date. (https://www.ge.ch/getax/retour-declarations-internet)
- GeTax deposit via e-démarches: needs an e-démarches account; gives a timestamped receipt and transaction number; no postal mail. (https://www.ge.ch/getax/retour-declarations-internet)
- The online return pre-fills only from last year's declaration, and only if that one was sent electronically. (https://www.ge.ch/getax/declaration-ligne-particuliers)
- Supporting documents can be attached as PDF; GeTax rejects a file already imported, even if renamed. (https://www.ge.ch/getax/retour-declarations-internet)

## Declaration form (tax year 2025)
- The official form is published as a specimen of the handwritten declaration (manifest id ge-2025-declaration-specimen), 26 pages: PG1 to PG4, annexes A1, A2, B1 to B4, C1 to C4, D1 to D4, E1, E2, F1 to F4, G1, G2. The guide's short link to it (www.ge.ch/c/imp-spde25, guide p. 9) answered 404 on 2026-10-13. (https://www.ge.ch/document/42265/telecharger ; https://www.ge.ch/document/declaration-impot-2025-documents-envoyes)
- Amounts are rounded to the franc, without centimes, except the imputations of annexe F (specimen p. 21; guide p. 9 for handwritten forms). Whether the online declaration accepts centimes is not stated on the pages read. (https://www.ge.ch/document/42265/telecharger)
- Annexe A names the salary certificate box of each rubric: 11.10 takes box 1, boxes 2 to 7 have rubrics of their own, box 8 (their total) is not typed. (https://www.ge.ch/document/42265/telecharger, p. 5)
- The online declaration offers the same features, checks and printouts as GeTax; its screens are not documented publicly. (https://www.ge.ch/getax/declaration-ligne-particuliers)

## Deadlines
- Ordinary deadline: 31 March. (https://www.ge.ch/remplir-ma-declaration-impot-tant-que-particulier/demander-delai-ma-declaration-impot)
- Tax year 2025: due 31 March 2026, or 30 June 2026 if an extension was requested. Both dates are past as of 13 October 2026; late-filing rules are not in the verified pages. (https://www.ge.ch/getax/telecharger-installer-getax-particuliers)
- Late-filing page seen in search, not read: https://www.ge.ch/remplir-ma-declaration-impot-tant-que-particulier/je-n-ai-pas-depose-ma-declaration-dans-delai
- Extension requests: 1 January to 31 October online, by phone (022 546 94 00, option 1) or by post; 1 November to 31 December by post only, with reasons. (https://www.ge.ch/remplir-ma-declaration-impot-tant-que-particulier/demander-delai-ma-declaration-impot)
- Extension fees: CHF 20 (up to 3 months), CHF 40 (up to 5 months), CHF 60 (over 5 months); a reminder is also billed if no extension was requested by the deadline. (https://www.ge.ch/remplir-ma-declaration-impot-tant-que-particulier/demander-delai-ma-declaration-impot)
- Canton-of-domicile rule: if resident elsewhere at year-end, ask by post for the home canton's deadline, writing "Accord canton domicile". (https://www.ge.ch/remplir-ma-declaration-impot-tant-que-particulier/demander-delai-ma-declaration-impot)

## Source tax
- DRIS/TOU form due by 31 March of the year after withholding; for 2025 withholding that is 31 March 2026 (inferred). Filed online via e-démarches, or on paper if it came by post. (https://www.ge.ch/demande-rectification-taxation-ordinaire-ulterieure)
- DRIS is optional unless TOU is mandatory; if not filed, the withheld tax stands unless the AFC starts a correction. (https://www.ge.ch/demande-rectification-taxation-ordinaire-ulterieure)
- TOU on request: written request by 31 March of the following year; once filed it can't be withdrawn (RISP art. 8 al. 1). Added 2026-10-13 with the planning tools. (https://silgeneve.ch/legis/data/rsg_d3_20p01.htm)
- The 2026 directives say the same, "même si la taxation est finalement défavorable", and that TOU then applies every following year until source taxation ends; late or informal requests are refused (ch. 8.2.3, p. 52). (https://www.ge.ch/document/42009/annexe/file/580297)
- Compulsory TOU for residents (LISP art. 4 al. 1; RISP art. 7; directives 2026 ch. 8.2.1 p. 51): gross salary taxed at source over a threshold, other income not taxed at source over a threshold, taxable wealth, a TOU the year before. The thresholds and the 2026 wealth deductions are in those documents. (https://silgeneve.ch/legis/data/rsg_d3_20.htm)
- CONFLICT, open: the AFC page "Quand demander ou annoncer une TOU" also lists "votre conjoint (couple marié) est sans activité" as a compulsory case for residents; the 2026 directives (p. 51) and LISP art. 4 don't. Taxpresso shows it as "to check with the AFC", never as compulsory. Ask the AFC which is right. (https://www.ge.ch/demande-rectification-taxation-ordinaire-ulterieure/quand-demander-annoncer-taxation-ordinaire-ulterieure)
- Geneva computes source tax with the annual model: the rate comes from the year's income, the year's tax is that income times the rate (directives 2026 ch. 2 and 4, p. 9 and p. 14). With tariff C the tax withheld can be corrected afterwards on both spouses' real incomes (ch. 8.1.3, p. 49). (https://www.ge.ch/document/42009/annexe/file/580297)
- The tariffs are published as PDF and as ASCII files in the ESTV record format (tar25GE.zip, tar26GE.zip), only without church tax (code letter N). The ASCII and PDF tables of 2025 and 2026 were compared row by row on 2026-10-13 (A0, B0 to B5, C0 to C5, H1 to H5; 1,441 rows each): no difference. A1 to A5 and D are separate annexes, G and the cross-border tariffs are not used. (https://www.ge.ch/publication?organisation=51&type=183&titre=perception)
- The 2026 directives' web page summary still says "valables dès le 1er janvier 2025"; the PDF itself is the 2026 edition (dated 9 February 2026). (https://www.ge.ch/document/directives-concernant-imposition-source-2026)
- The 2025 source-tax employee form goes to the employer, not the tax office; changes must be reported within 14 days; no deadline is stated. (https://www.ge.ch/document/37885/annexe/file/371410)
- 2026 withholding tables A, B, C and H are published (dated December 2025, page updated February 2026). (https://www.ge.ch/document/41802/telecharger)

## Law and e-filing basis
- LPFisc art. 27: return deadline at least 30 days from delivery of the form, with extensions under al. 2; art. 29A: electronic filing; art. 49: 30 days to appeal. (https://silgeneve.ch/legis/data/rsg_d3_17.htm)
- LIPP: spouses' income and wealth are added (art. 8); joint taxation and income splitting (art. 41, 66); tax period is the calendar year (art. 61); no e-filing provision. (https://silgeneve.ch/legis/data/rsg_d3_08.htm)
- Two implementing texts: RDIOI, rsGE D 3 17.03, on approved software and barcoded forms (https://www.getax.ch/support/sites/default/files/D_3_17.03_-_Rglt_relatif_aux_declarations_impot.pdf.pdf), and RPFisc-29A, rsGE D 3 17.07, in force 6 March 2024: only official or AFC-approved software may produce non-paper returns, and a return filed with strong identification counts as filed on electronic receipt (https://silgeneve.ch/legis/program/books/rsg/htm/rsg_d3_17p07.htm). Added by the main session.

## Securities list (état des titres): conflicts and open points (added 2026-10-13 by the securities agent)
- Ask the human: where crypto goes. The guide says to multiply the ESTV tax rate by the units held and report the result "dans l'état des titres, sous Compte bancaire ou postal" (ge-2025-guide-declaration-pp p. 40), while the heading of the 2025 form's securities sheet lists "crypto-monnaies" among the titres suisses et étrangers (ge-2025-declaration-specimen p. 23, sheet F3). The totals are the same either way; Taxpresso follows the guide (with the accounts, F2) until the AFC says otherwise.
- Wording conflict, the law wins: the guide says the IA is not levied on interest "inférieurs à 200" francs (ge-2025-guide-declaration-pp p. 40); the law says interest that "n'excède pas 200 francs" for a calendar year (ch-na-law-lia art. 5 al. 1 let. c, p. 4). Taxpresso never decides it: it takes the IA the bank shows, and only flags Swiss interest above the threshold with no IA as something to check.
- The guide's short link to the declaration specimen (www.ge.ch/c/imp-spde25, p. 9) answers 404; the specimen is on https://www.ge.ch/document/declaration-impot-2025-documents-envoyes (ge-2025-declaration-specimen). The rubric codes the état des titres feeds (14.00, 16.40, 16.70, 52.11, 55.10, 56.20, 56.30) come from it; the guide itself never names 14.00.
- Not published yet, so Taxpresso fails closed for tax year 2026: the official exchange rates at 31 December 2026 and the 2026 averages (ge-2025-taux-donnees-fiscales gives 2025 and earlier), and the list of non-recoverable foreign taxes for income of 2026 (the DFF annex in force on 1 February 2026 covers income of 2025, ch-2025-oiis-dff-annexe p. 2). Add both in January and February 2027.
- The ICTax price list (liste des cours) and its search need a browser (ch-na-listes-des-cours-ictax). The extension never fetches it: the user looks up official values and Taxpresso records them as stated, with where they came from.

## Added and found while building the tax engine (2026-10-14)
- Added ge-na-law-lcp (LCP, rsGE D 3 05): the communal centimes are levied on the cantonal base tax (art. 293); the privileged share of each base tax goes to the commune of residence and the rest to the communes concerned in proportion to the income and wealth attributable to each (art. 295A); the taxe personnelle and its exemptions (art. 374 to 377). (https://silgeneve.ch/legis/data/rsg_d3_05.htm)
- Added ge-2026-part-privilegiee-communique: the 2026 privileged share of every commune, fixed by the Conseil d'Etat on 15 October 2025, p. 11. The AFC table ge-2025-communal-centimes-pdf stops at 2025; the 2027 shares were fixed on 7 October 2026. (https://www.ge.ch/document/40923/telecharger)
- Rounding not stated in the documents: the tariff (ge-2025-icc-tariff-pdf) gives every amount to 5 centimes, but not how the cantonal centimes, the 12 % reduction, the home care centime and the communal shares are rounded. The engine rounds each line to the nearest 5 centimes, halves up, and flags the rule; check it against a real bordereau.
- Rubric codes: the engine uses the codes printed on the 2025 form (ge-2025-declaration-specimen): 14.00 for accounts and securities, in the income (PG2) and wealth (PG4) columns; 55.10 for private debts and their interest (E1; 55.00 on the recapitulation); 56.20 and 56.30 (F1); 32.40 for the 3a of a self-employed person (B2). The guide's text names the donations 73.10 (ICC) and 73.20 (IFD), p. 15, while the form prints one rubric, 73.00, with an ICC and an IFD column (p. 3): the engine uses 73.00. Check every code on the 2026 form.
- PG3 (specimen p. 3) puts 95.00 revenu net total after the social deductions 61.20 to 61.30, then 97.00 and 99.00 revenu imposable: the engine's total income is 95.00, its net income the subtotal before 61.xx.
- The guide's code ranges for the two-earner deduction ("31.10 à 31.63", p. 21) follow the form's order; the engine reads them as every employment deduction before 31.90, which is what LIFD art. 33 al. 2 says for the IFD.
- Life insurance limit 52.15 (p. 28): "augmentée de moitié" gives a half franc for the per-charge amount; the engine rounds it down and flags it.
- The tax shield (LIPP art. 60, guide p. 51) is not computed: when it may apply the engine computes no ICC at all.

## Travel, meals and shared custody (added 2026-10-14 by the travel, meals and custody agent)
- Travel to work, ICC: LIPP art. 29 al. 1 let. a allows the "frais de déplacement nécessaires" up to 500 francs (ge-na-law-lipp), indexed to 534 for 2025 "quel que soit le mode de transport choisi" (ge-2025-guide-declaration-pp p. 11, 19 and 21). Neither says how a bike or a car is priced before that cap; the guide prices them only for the IFD (p. 20). Taxpresso prices them as the federal tax does, then applies the 534 cap, and flags the rule as not confirmed. Check with the AFC or GeTax.
- Meals, ICC: allowed only when the trip by public transport takes over two hours a day (guide p. 19 and the examples p. 22), at the federal rates. When the person has no public transport they can use at all, the guide doesn't say whether the condition is met: Taxpresso does not count Geneva meals then (fail closed) and says so.
- Days worked from home: no travel or meal deduction for them (guide p. 20). Taxpresso asks the way to work on most days; "from home almost every day" counts neither.
- Shared custody and the ICC: with child support (contribution d'entretien), the parent who receives it is the one who mainly supports the child, for the splitting (p. 45) and for the family charge (p. 46); without it and when both support the child, the charge is shared half and half (p. 46). Taxpresso asks the question once and applies it to the ICC and the IFD (ch-na-circulaire-30-epoux-famille for the IFD). Partial splitting (55.56 %) still assumes the judgment or signed agreement of p. 45 and is flagged.
- The 2025 car rate in the guide (0.70 a km, p. 20) is the 2025 figure; the ESTV circular gives 0.75 for 2026 (ch-2026-ifd-progression-deductions p. 1). Not a conflict: a new tax year.

## Pension capital, 98.12 (added 2026-10-14 by the pension capital agent)
- The rule: lump sums from a pension fund, vested benefits or pillar 3a (LIPP art. 25) are taxed apart from the other income, a full year's tax, at the rate of a fifth of the scale of art. 41; every lump sum of the year is added up for the rate, the spouses' too, and the splitting of art. 41 al. 2 to 4 still applies; no social deduction (ge-na-law-lipp art. 45). Declared at 98.12, "Prestations en capital du 2ème et 3ème pilier A imposées au 1/5ème du barème", among the information of PG2 (ge-2025-declaration-specimen p. 2; ge-2025-guide-declaration-pp p. 13: "imposées séparément au moment de leur versement").
- Added ge-na-law-ldirpp (LDIRPP, rsGE D 3 06): art. 1 reduces the direct tax on the income of individuals by 12 %, except the communal centimes; the tariff calls it "la loi du 26 septembre 1999" (ge-2025-icc-tariff-pdf p. 2 and 4). Added ge-na-law-lcacant (LCACant, rsGE D 3 07): art. 2 levies 47.5 cantonal centimes and 1 for home care on "les impôts cantonaux sur le revenu et la fortune". Both laws are why the capital tax carries the same surcharges as the income tax. (https://silgeneve.ch/legis/data/rsg_d3_06.htm ; https://silgeneve.ch/legis/data/rsg_d3_07.htm)
- Added ge-2025-source-capital-icc-tariff and ge-2026-source-capital-icc-tariff, the AFC's own rates of this tax at source (art. 45 LIPP, LISP art. 10 to 12), for one person without splitting, the cantonal centimes (48.5 %) and a flat communal 43.69 % (2025) or 43.44 % (2026) included. The 2025 table confirms the method to the hundredth of a percent on all 624 rows: the scale at the amount, a fifth of it, + 47.5 %, - 12 % of the base and those centimes, + 1 %, + the communal centimes, each range's rate taken at its middle (packages/rules-ge/test/capital.test.ts, extension/test/capital-tariff.test.ts). (https://www.ge.ch/document/38083/telecharger ; https://www.ge.ch/document/41804/telecharger)
- Open point: the 2026 table (November 2025) matches the 2025 scale at each range's lower bound on 606 of 621 rows; the 15 others differ by 0.01 point and no single reading of it fits every row. The 2026 scale is not published (the 2026 guide comes in January 2027): Taxpresso keeps the 2025 scale for 2026, flagged as an estimate, as for the income tax. Re-check against the 2026 tariff.
- Not spelled out, flagged in every result (NOTE.capitalRounding): the rounding of the fifth and of each line (Taxpresso: to the nearest 5 centimes, as the tariff rounds its amounts), and the communal share. A lump sum is not income from a profession in another commune (ge-na-law-lcp art. 293 let. A ch. 1), so the whole base goes to the commune of residence, its privileged share and the rest alike (art. 295A al. 2). The AFC table uses a flat average for people abroad (LISP art. 11 and 12) and can't confirm it.
- Fails closed (not computed, with the reason): a lump sum paid on leaving Switzerland or to someone abroad (taxed at source: ge-2026-source-directives p. 39, 6.5); someone whose salary is taxed at source (the directives tax some lump sums at source, "rentes partielles et prestations en capital provenant de l'AI et de la prévoyance professionnelle", and others in the declaration, for people who have stopped working: p. 11); the year of an arrival from abroad (a lump sum paid before the arrival is not taxed here); any other reason (disability, a death, a divorce, a transfer to a pension fund: 98.75 "Indemnités en capital imposées au 1/5ème du barème" or no tax); a buy-in and a 2nd pillar lump sum in the same year (the buy-in's deduction can be taken back: ge-2025-guide-declaration-pp p. 18; ch-na-law-lpp art. 79b al. 3): then the income tax is not computed, the capital tax is.
- Capital paid by the employer (salary certificate box 4, 11.70 and 21.70) is unchanged: box 8 adds it in, so it is counted with the salary. The guide taxes it with the other income when it is a mere complement of salary, and at a converted rate when it replaces periodic payments (p. 17 and 18, LIPP art. 43); the federal instructions tax it apart, at a fifth, when it is a pension benefit (ch-2026-ifd-instructions-personnes-physiques p. 9). Taxpresso can't tell which: it says so when box 4 has an amount.
- The reason for a withdrawal doesn't change the tax: retirement, one's own home (LPP art. 30c and 83a: the advance is taxed as a capital benefit) and starting self-employment are all capital benefits of art. 25 and 45. Taxpresso supports these three.
