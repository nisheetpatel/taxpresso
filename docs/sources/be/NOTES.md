# Bern (BE) official sources, natural persons, tax years 2025 and 2026

Verified 2026-10-13. Tax rates and tariff numbers are left out on purpose; read them from the documents. Never copy a number from this file.

## Filing channels, tax year 2025
- Online filing runs through TaxMe-Online, logged in with BE-Login; the 2025 guide covers paper forms and names TaxMe for online filing (https://www.wegleitung.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/wegleitungen/2025/wl_natuerliche-personen_2025_de.pdf)
- The January 2026 info sheet says the return can be filed online in BE-Login or on paper, and that AGOV is the mandatory login for all natural persons in BE-Login (checked in the PDF) (https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/info_10minuten/info-np_januar2026_de.pdf)
- Login: go to www.taxme.ch, pick an existing BE-Login account or register, help at www.taxme.ch/hilfe (https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/belogin_leitfaden_online-vs_de.pdf)
- Fully electronic: click "Freigeben", confirm completeness, then "Elektronisch einreichen"; no signed page is needed (https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/leitfaden_online_dr-tax_de.pdf)
- Paper route: signed page, and the release receipt goes to the municipality of residence (https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/leitfaden_online_dr-tax_de.pdf)

## Third-party software (Dr. Tax)
- The preparer enters the return, the client agrees, then "Online einreichen" and "Zur Datenübertragung" transmit it (https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/leitfaden_online_dr-tax_de.pdf)
- Only the taxpayer may release the return via BE-Login; preparers may not (https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/argumentarium_dr-tax_treuhaender_de.pdf)
- No approval (Zulassung) process and no published list of approved software appear in these documents (https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/argumentarium_dr-tax_treuhaender_de.pdf)

## Deadlines and extensions
- Standard deadline 15 March; 15 May for self-employed, farmers and certain partnerships; the letter's date governs (https://www.wegleitung.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/wegleitungen/2025/wl_natuerliche-personen_2025_de.pdf)
- Extension fees, checked in the January 2026 sheet: online free until 15 July; written, phone or counter 20 francs until 15 July (https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/info_10minuten/info-np_januar2026_de.pdf)
- Same sheet: until 15 September online 20 francs, written 40 francs; until 15 November online 40 francs, written 60 francs (https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/info_10minuten/info-np_januar2026_de.pdf)
- Objection (réclamation): 30 days from notification, not extendable (https://www.wegleitung.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/fr/wegleitungen/2025/wl_natuerliche-personen_2025_fr.pdf)

## Source tax (Quellensteuer), NOV, rectification
- NOV request due 31 March of the following year; leaving Switzerland ends it at deregistration (https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/info_10minuten/info-nov_de.pdf)
- Once filed on time, the NOV request is irrevocable (https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/formulare/quellensteuer/aktuelles_steuerjahr/form_qst_antrag-nov-ansaessig_de.pdf)
- Swiss residents can request NOV to claim deductions the tables miss, e.g. pension buy-ins, pillar 3a, professional costs (https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/info_10minuten/info-nov_de.pdf)
- Submit by email to nov.sv@be.ch or by post to Steuerverwaltung des Kantons Bern, Quellensteuer / NOV, Postfach, 3001 Bern (https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/formulare/quellensteuer/aktuelles_steuerjahr/form_qst_antrag-nov-ansaessig_de.pdf)
- Non-residents use a separate NOV form (version 7.2026): same 31 March deadline, irrevocable once filed, and a quasi-resident condition on the share of worldwide income (spouse included) taxable in Switzerland; read the share in the form (https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/formulare/quellensteuer/aktuelles_steuerjahr/form_qst_antrag-nov-nichtansaessig_de.pdf)
- Mandatory NOV for residents (gross income, wealth and other-income thresholds) and the 31 March filing duty are described in TaxInfo (https://www.taxinfo.sv.fin.be.ch/taxinfo/1ddebd20-de30-4181-9913-0cb49aeea127)
- NOV on request: a defective request gets a short period to fix it; a valid one leads to a tax return being sent (https://www.taxinfo.sv.fin.be.ch/taxinfo/0f9796b7-81dc-4504-95cc-8a31400e94f4)
- The cantonal source tax ordinance (QSV, BSG 661.711.1, Stand 01.01.2021, still current) sets the tariff calculation and the NOV conditions and procedure (https://www.belex.sites.be.ch/api/de/versions/2017/pdf_file)
- The NOV sheet gives no rectification deadline (https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/info_10minuten/info-nov_de.pdf)
- Source tax tables 2025 and 2026 are each one German and French PDF (print version) plus an xlsx, linked from the source tax calculation page; the PDFs state no validity start date (https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/de/start/themen/steuern-berechnen/quellensteuer.html)
- 2025 tables (https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/qst_quellensteuertabelle_2025_de_fr.pdf) and 2026 tables (https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/qst_quellensteuertabelle_2026_de_fr.pdf)
- The deductions built into each tariff code for 2026, with StG article references, are in a separate xlsx; its file name has no year (https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/qst_berechnung-quellensteuertarife_de.xlsx)

## e-tax statements (eCH-0196) and other imports
- Only when filing in BE-Login can you import bank eSteuerauszüge: you upload the PDF and the data are taken over; assets taken from it must not be entered again one by one, and you remain responsible for checking them (https://www.wegleitung.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/wegleitungen/2025/wl_natuerliche-personen_2025_de.pdf)
- Receipts can be uploaded from the computer or photographed with a phone (https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/info_10minuten/info-np_januar2026_de.pdf)
- No eCH-0196 version is named in these documents

- StG Stand 01.01.2026, the current version on belex (decision of 26.03.2025, which also carries the 2026 cold progression adjustment) (https://www.belex.sites.be.ch/api/de/versions/3330/pdf_file)
- Professional costs: Art. 31 for employees, Art. 32 for self-employed (https://www.belex.sites.be.ch/api/de/versions/3018/pdf_file)
- Communal quotas and the cantonal rate history for 2025 (https://www.fin.be.ch/content/dam/fin/dokumente/de/finanzverwaltung/finanzausgleich/publikationen/fv-stat-steueranlage_2025.pdf)
- Communal quotas for 2026, Stand 01.02.2026 (https://www.fin.be.ch/content/dam/fin/dokumente/de/finanzverwaltung/finanzausgleich/publikationen/fv-stat-steueranlage_2026_1.pdf)

## Tariffs, multipliers and deductions (pointers to documents)
- Mechanics: the Art. 42 tariff gives the einfache Steuer; it is multiplied by the cantonal, communal and church Steueranlagen. Since 2021 the canton and communes may set different rates for natural and legal persons, within a limit stated on the page (https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/de/start/themen/steuern-berechnen/steueranlagen.html)
- Two income tariffs: Art. 42 Abs. 1 for married couples and single-parent families, Abs. 2 for everyone else; the situation at year end decides, with rules for cohabiting parents, divorce, separation and death (https://www.taxinfo.sv.fin.be.ch/taxinfo/f3ad83d5-30d6-41c9-8f79-c34e192c57ad)
- Income tax tables (einfache Steuer per income step): 2025 married (https://www.taxinfo.sv.fin.be.ch/taxinfo/a2435e4c-8d14-4692-a542-9820c2d6281e), 2025 others (https://www.taxinfo.sv.fin.be.ch/taxinfo/f56cd527-72c9-4a47-9552-5d0aaf1ba8cf), 2026 married (https://www.taxinfo.sv.fin.be.ch/taxinfo/36f0ac7e-6293-4612-90dd-e4a1cb2f5915), 2026 others (https://www.taxinfo.sv.fin.be.ch/taxinfo/f244ba97-5848-473f-9037-0621b32f8836)
- Wealth tax tariff: Art. 65 StG in the versions above; no separate table was found
- Cold progression: the Grosser Rat adjusts by decree when the consumer price index has moved past the threshold in Art. 3 Abs. 3 StG; adjustments per 2024, 2025 and 2026, none per 2027 (https://www.taxinfo.sv.fin.be.ch/taxinfo/44dcc198-4a11-4ab4-aabc-051e94ae01f2)
- All cantonal and federal deduction amounts and caps by tax year 2022 to 2026 are in one xlsx linked from that page (https://www.taxinfo.sv.fin.be.ch/_assets/file/phonemos-media%3Afile.id%3A3513189d-e092-48b0-8e66-d084e51af8ad/Abz%C3%BCge%202022-2026.xlsx)
- Communal and church Steueranlagen 1983 to 2026 and the cantonal Steueranlage history, as one xlsx dated 11.06.2026 (https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/gemeindeanlagen_de_fr.xlsx)
- Employee professional costs: Berufskostenverordnung BKV (BSG 661.312.56), Stand 01.01.2024, still current: commuting, extra meal costs, other costs, weekly stay away from home, side income (https://www.belex.sites.be.ch/api/de/versions/2873/pdf_file)
- TaxInfo practice pages: commuting (https://www.taxinfo.sv.fin.be.ch/taxinfo/1613b2e2-276f-4293-ad05-3995d29b40cb), insurance deduction (https://www.taxinfo.sv.fin.be.ch/taxinfo/fb35fd5b-0bd3-46c6-a28c-1656bec91f3b), childcare (https://www.taxinfo.sv.fin.be.ch/taxinfo/291bf1b9-10c8-42eb-bdd4-fd54f1a8e708), dual earner (https://www.taxinfo.sv.fin.be.ch/taxinfo/5818ab45-a359-4462-8370-d93149535249)
- More TaxInfo pages: pillar 3a, with the new 3a buy-in first possible in 2026 for gaps from 2025 (https://www.taxinfo.sv.fin.be.ch/taxinfo/a2830da2-1a00-4928-b58b-74da5d48a598), home office (https://www.taxinfo.sv.fin.be.ch/taxinfo/233e0b49-8907-4294-8057-0fa76116a4b2), job-related training (https://www.taxinfo.sv.fin.be.ch/taxinfo/8fc11889-fa95-4160-b59a-8cba8f1ad457), medical costs (https://www.taxinfo.sv.fin.be.ch/taxinfo/e11abaa8-c657-4848-bedd-4f4d5a647c4d)
- TaxInfo amounts on the practice pages are for tax year 2025; each page refers to the deductions xlsx for other years (https://www.taxinfo.sv.fin.be.ch/taxinfo/fb35fd5b-0bd3-46c6-a28c-1656bec91f3b)

## Changes for 2025 (versus 2024)
- Pensions: only the taxable share of a life annuity is declared, not the full amount (https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/wegleitungen/2025/wl_natuerliche-personen_2025-2024-aenderungen_de.pdf)
- Gaming and lottery exemptions, stake caps, child care caps, adult child support, pillar 3a caps and party donation caps were adjusted (https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/wegleitungen/2025/wl_natuerliche-personen_2025-2024-aenderungen_de.pdf)
- Solar and PV systems have their own section; the tax value follows acquisition cost (https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/wegleitungen/2025/wl_natuerliche-personen_2025-2024-aenderungen_de.pdf)
- Crypto assets are declared in the securities list, valued at 31 December 2025 (https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/info_10minuten/info-np_januar2026_de.pdf)
- Income tax tariffs were lowered for 2025 to offset cold progression; federal deductions were raised (https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/info_10minuten/info-np_januar2026_de.pdf)

## Changes for 2026
- Anyone who prepaid in 2025 gets a new QR bill; the new QR reference number is mandatory for 2026 (https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/info_10minuten/info-np_januar2026_de.pdf)
- Prepayment interest falls cantonally and communally, and federally to zero (https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/info_10minuten/info-np_januar2026_de.pdf)
- Income tax tariffs are adjusted for cold progression again from 2026, by the StG change of 26.03.2025 (https://www.taxinfo.sv.fin.be.ch/taxinfo/862cf9fd-5117-4a10-a650-0e5c0731064a)
- Pillar 3a buy-ins (Nachzahlung) become possible from tax year 2026 (https://www.taxinfo.sv.fin.be.ch/taxinfo/a2830da2-1a00-4928-b58b-74da5d48a598)
