# Vaud (VD) natural-person tax sources, checked 2026-10-13

Verified 2026-10-13, second pass (gap check) the same day. Each fact is followed by its official source. The documents are the source of truth; never copy a number from this file.

## Filing channels, tax year 2025
- VaudTax is the online filing service for individuals, open 24/7 on phone, computer or tablet; start at https://prestations.vd.ch/pub/101660/ (https://www.vd.ch/prestation/remplir-ma-declaration-dimpot-2025-avec-la-prestation-vaudtax-pour-les-individus-personnes-physiques)
- The VaudTax desktop software is no longer produced for the 2025 return; taxpayers must use the online VaudTax service, and a .tax file from an earlier year can still be imported (https://www.vd.ch/etat-droit-finances/impots/impots-pour-les-individus/remplir-ma-declaration-dimpot)
- Login: no Vaud login or e-ID is described; access uses the taxpayer number and the control code from the A3 transmission form (https://www.vd.ch/prestation/remplir-ma-declaration-dimpot-2025-avec-la-prestation-vaudtax-pour-les-individus-personnes-physiques)
- Fully electronic filing needs no signature; save and print the summary notice; a corrected return may follow within 6 days of the first electronic submission (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/21001_2025.pdf)
- The same rules are in the filing regulation RDVE: electronic filing through the ACI application on the State of Vaud website (art. 2), immediate receipt and a summary, and the return counts as validly filed if not contested or replaced within 6 days (art. 3) (https://prestations.vd.ch/pub/blv-publication/api/actes/42a955c1-f170-495d-badc-8d2881e476f5/pdf?type=PDF_RENDU)
- Paper route: print on white A4, each taxpayer signs (each spouse or partner living together), put it in the A3 envelope with no staples or clips, and post it (https://www.vd.ch/etat-droit-finances/impots/impots-pour-les-individus/remplir-ma-declaration-dimpot ; https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/21001_2025.pdf)
- Email submission is not allowed (https://www.vd.ch/etat-droit-finances/impots/impots-pour-les-individus/remplir-ma-declaration-dimpot)
- Supporting documents are not sent with the return but must be kept; securities holders send the bank tax statement (RDVE art. 5) (https://prestations.vd.ch/pub/blv-publication/api/actes/42a955c1-f170-495d-badc-8d2881e476f5/pdf?type=PDF_RENDU)

## Third-party software
- Returns made with approved software ("logiciel agréé") can be submitted electronically or sent by post (https://www.vd.ch/etat-droit-finances/impots/impots-pour-les-individus/remplir-ma-declaration-dimpot ; https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/21001_2025.pdf)
- Approved 2025 programmes ("Liste des logiciels agréés"), confirmed on the page with the fetch user agent: Dr. Tax VD PP 2025, Windfisc 2025, KTax Vaud 2025. For approved software the ACI certifies only the barcodes, not the calculations; documents from non-approved software are returned (https://www.vd.ch/etat-droit-finances/impots/impots-pour-les-individus/remplir-ma-declaration-dimpot)
- Software printouts must meet the ACI notice based on the Conseil d'Etat directive of 13 August 2003; the notice is dated 09.2015 and cites the 2011 RDVE, which the 2016 RDVE repealed (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/Notice_relative_etablissement_declaration_impot_PC.pdf ; https://prestations.vd.ch/pub/blv-publication/api/actes/42a955c1-f170-495d-badc-8d2881e476f5/pdf?type=PDF_RENDU)
- Vendor approval procedure: not described on the pages read, and the RDVE is silent on it (gap).

## Deadlines, tax year 2025
- Ordinary deadline: 15 March 2026 (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/Directive_D%C3%A9lai_d%C3%A9p%C3%B4t_DI_PP_Demandes_unitaires.pdf)
- Unlimited-liability taxpayers have a tolerance date of 30 June 2026 with no request needed (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/21001_2025.pdf)
- Extension requests are free. Requests received up to 15 May 2026 may be granted to 30 June; from 16 May, to 30 September; all requests must be submitted by 30 June (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/Directive_D%C3%A9lai_d%C3%A9p%C3%B4t_DI_PP_Demandes_unitaires.pdf)
- Force majeure: a written, justified request sent by post until 30 September 2026 may get a final extension to 31 October 2026 (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/Directive_D%C3%A9lai_d%C3%A9p%C3%B4t_DI_PP_Demandes_unitaires.pdf)
- A reminder (sommation) for a late return carries a CHF 50 fee; the tax office may then assess ex officio (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/21001_2025.pdf)
- The RDVE sets a 30-day period in the sommation before an ex officio assessment (art. 4) (https://prestations.vd.ch/pub/blv-publication/api/actes/42a955c1-f170-495d-badc-8d2881e476f5/pdf?type=PDF_RENDU)
- Voluntary payment before 31 March 2026 can help avoid compensatory interest (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/21001_2025.pdf)
- Taxpayers outside the canton or outside Switzerland: general deadline 30 September 2026, tolerance 30 November 2026 (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/Directive_D%C3%A9lai_d%C3%A9p%C3%B4t_DI_PP_Demandes_unitaires.pdf)
- As of 13 October 2026 the last published window, postal force-majeure requests, closed on 30 September 2026. Check whether the 2025 return was filed (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/Directive_D%C3%A9lai_d%C3%A9p%C3%B4t_DI_PP_Demandes_unitaires.pdf)

## Source tax (impôt à la source)
- TOU on request: due by 31 March of the year after the fiscal year concerned; irrevocable once validly filed; both spouses sign if married or in a partnership (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/21049_TOU_Formulaire_WEB.pdf)
- The source-tax regulation RIS states the same rule (art. 8: written request by 31 March of the following year, cannot be withdrawn) and sets the income threshold for mandatory TOU in art. 7; version in force from 1 January 2026 (https://prestations.vd.ch/pub/blv-publication/api/actes/0fb0ca28-b0f4-40db-b178-de8719767ef5/pdf?type=PDF_RENDU). The version for 2021 to 2025 is https://prestations.vd.ch/pub/blv-publication/api/actes/2021870/pdf?type=PDF_RENDU
- The 2026 employer brochure gives the same 31 March deadline and irrevocability (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/21034-10_Employeurs_2026.pdf)
- Rectification: the official term is "nouveau calcul de l'impôt à la source"; the RIS limits it to a wrong gross salary, a wrong rate-determining income or a wrong tariff, requested by 31 March of the following year (https://prestations.vd.ch/pub/blv-publication/api/actes/0fb0ca28-b0f4-40db-b178-de8719767ef5/pdf?type=PDF_RENDU ; https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/21034-10_Employeurs_2026.pdf)
- Mandatory TOU: foreign nationals without permit C above the income threshold stated in the guide are taxed ordinarily; those who received no return can register by 31 March 2026 (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/21001_2025.pdf)
- Non-resident workers may request TOU by 31 March if at least 90 percent of worldwide gross income is taxable in Switzerland (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/21001_2025.pdf)
- Cross-border workers living in France: the French residence certificate (ARF) must be in hand before the activity starts, and before 1 January in later years; telework above 40 percent of working time ends frontalier status (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/Directives_concernant_l_imposition_des_travailleurs_frontaliers.pdf)
- Tables A, B, C and H (all codes, valid from 1 January) are printed inside the employer brochures, 79 pages for 2026; vd.ch has no separate PDF per tariff and links the ESTV txt files instead (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/21034-10_Employeurs_2026.pdf ; https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/21034-10_Employeurs_2025.pdf)
- Tariff G (2025: https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/2025_G.pdf ; 2026: https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/2026_G.pdf) and tariffs I, J, K for pension capital (2025: https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/2025_IJK.pdf ; 2026: https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/2026_IJK.pdf) are separate PDFs.
- The deductions built into tariffs A, B, C and H are published per year: 2025 (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/Publications_d%C3%A9ductions_bar%C3%A8ms_IS_2025.pdf), 2026 (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/Publications_d%C3%A9ductions_bar%C3%A8ms_IS_2026.pdf)
- The annual tax law also sets flat source-tax rates for special categories (for example artists, sportspeople and lecturers under LI art. 139, mortgage creditors under art. 141) (https://prestations.vd.ch/pub/blv-publication/api/actes/2253990/pdf?type=PDF_RENDU)

## e-tax statements (eCH-0196) and other imports
- The online VaudTax service imports the electronic tax statement ("eRelevé fiscal électronique") from the barcodes of the bank's PDF, filling the securities list and the debts schedule; it also imports earlier .tax and .vaudtax files (https://www.vd.ch/etat-droit-finances/impots/impots-pour-les-individus/remplir-ma-declaration-dimpot)

## Tariffs, coefficients, deductions
- Income and wealth scales are published for 2025 and 2026, each stated at a 100 percent base; the cantonal coefficient is applied separately (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/bar%C3%A8me_revenu_2025.pdf ; https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/Bar%C3%A8mes_Revenu_2026.pdf)
- Cantonal coefficient: set each year in art. 2 of the annual tax law. 2025: Loi sur l'impôt 2025 (https://prestations.vd.ch/pub/blv-publication/api/actes/2253963/pdf?type=PDF_RENDU). 2026: Loi sur l'impôt 2026 (https://prestations.vd.ch/pub/blv-publication/api/actes/2253990/pdf?type=PDF_RENDU). Both laws were adopted on 1 October 2024.
- The ACI coefficient list stops at 2025; its URL now redirects to an anchor on the forms page (https://www.vd.ch/etat-droit-finances/impots/formulaires-directives-et-baremes/coefficient-cantonal)
- Unusual mechanic: under the LRIPP the cantonal base income tax is reduced by a percentage fixed per tax year (art. 4), the reduction does not reach the communal tax (art. 2 al. 2), and it also applies to the cantonal share of source-tax tariffs (art. 5). 2025 version: https://prestations.vd.ch/pub/blv-publication/api/actes/77e5659e-4ab5-4829-8946-4d5e3a6467b1/pdf?type=PDF_RENDU ; 2026 version: https://prestations.vd.ch/pub/blv-publication/api/actes/91623681-55fd-42c7-b37c-16e7fe3ef402/pdf?type=PDF_RENDU
- Principal deductions tables (codes 140 to 810, caps and flat rates, quotient familial parts): 2025 (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/Tableau_des_d%C3%A9ductions_2025.pdf), 2026 (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/Tableau_des_d%C3%A9ductions_2026.pdf)
- Medical, accident and disability costs: ACI directive (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/Directive_ACI_FM-LHand.pdf)
- Consolidated LI as PDF from the BLV: 2025 version (https://prestations.vd.ch/pub/blv-publication/api/actes/002eaf00-bebb-4bf2-a811-4f9abd04b89f/pdf?type=PDF_RENDU); 2026 versions from 1 January to 30 April 2026 (https://prestations.vd.ch/pub/blv-publication/api/actes/a28b9d22-658e-4236-b5b1-95eccc04d2b1/pdf?type=PDF_RENDU) and from 1 May 2026 (https://prestations.vd.ch/pub/blv-publication/api/actes/47ac13a4-78a9-4327-ae95-c6adce9124a7/pdf?type=PDF_RENDU)
- BLV PDF endpoint: https://prestations.vd.ch/pub/blv-publication/api/actes/<version id>/pdf?type=PDF_RENDU. The version ids come from https://prestations.vd.ch/pub/blv-publication/api/actes/CONSOLIDE?id=<act id>&cote=<BLV number>; past versions stay available, so each id is a stable link to one version.

## Communal coefficients
- Each commune sets its own annual coefficient in an arrêté d'imposition, sent to the préfet before 30 October; the department publishes them in the Feuille des avis officiels in August and November (https://publication.vd.ch/publications/dgaic/aide-memoire/finances-communales/arretes-dimposition)
- The official tables of all communes, in percent of the cantonal base tax, are published by the DGAIC (Direction des finances communales): 2026 (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/themes/territoire/communes/finances_communales/fichiers_xls/Arr%C3%AAt%C3%A9s_d_imposition_2026.xls, linked from https://www.vd.ch/etat-droit-finances/communes/finances-communales/arretes-dimposition-et-tableaux-des-impots-communaux) and 2025, updated after the referendum of 18 May 2025 (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/themes/territoire/communes/finances_communales/Arr%C3%AAt%C3%A9s_d_imposition_2025-mis_%C3%A0_jour_apr%C3%A8s_r%C3%A9f%C3%A9rendum.xls, linked from https://www.vd.ch/etat-droit-finances/communes/finances-communales/archives-des-1994)
- Each table has a main rate column, an "impôt spécial affecté" column and a total; read the column that the communal tax computation needs, not only the first one (same files).
- The Statistique Vaud spreadsheet T22.18 is readable after all, but it covers 1990 to 2024 only and gives weighted averages, so it is not a source for 2025 or 2026 (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/statvd/Dom_22/Tableaux/T22.18_Taux-impot_depuis-1990.xlsx)
