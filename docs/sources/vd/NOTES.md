# Vaud (VD) natural-person tax sources, checked 2026-10-13

Verified 2026-10-13, second pass (gap check) the same day. Each fact is followed by its official source. The documents are the source of truth; never copy a number from this file.

## Filing channels, tax year 2025
- VaudTax is the online filing service for individuals, open 24/7 on phone, computer or tablet; start at https://prestations.vd.ch/pub/101660/ (https://www.vd.ch/prestation/remplir-ma-declaration-dimpot-2025-avec-la-prestation-vaudtax-pour-les-individus-personnes-physiques)
- The VaudTax desktop software is no longer produced for the 2025 return; taxpayers must use the online VaudTax service, and a .tax file from an earlier year can still be imported (https://www.vd.ch/etat-droit-finances/impots/impots-pour-les-individus/remplir-ma-declaration-dimpot)
- Login: no Vaud login or e-ID is described; access uses the taxpayer number and the control code from the A3 transmission form (https://www.vd.ch/prestation/remplir-ma-declaration-dimpot-2025-avec-la-prestation-vaudtax-pour-les-individus-personnes-physiques)
- Fully electronic filing needs no signature; save and print the summary notice; a corrected return may follow within 6 days of the first electronic submission (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/21001_2025.pdf)
- The same rules are in the filing regulation RDVE: electronic filing through the ACI application on the State of Vaud website (art. 2), immediate receipt and a summary, and the return counts as validly filed if not contested or replaced within 6 days (art. 3) (https://prestations.vd.ch/pub/blv-publication/api/actes/42a955c1-f170-495d-badc-8d2881e476f5/pdf?type=PDF_RENDU)
- Paper route: print on white A4, each taxpayer signs (each spouse or partner living together), put it in the A3 envelope with no staples or clips, and post it (https://www.vd.ch/etat-droit-finances/impots/impots-pour-les-individus/remplir-ma-declaration-dimpot ; https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/21001_2025.pdf)
- Email submission is not allowed (https://www.vd.ch/etat-droit-finances/impots/impots-pour-les-individus/remplir-ma-declaration-dimpot)
- Supporting documents are not sent with the return but must be kept; securities holders send the bank tax statement (RDVE art. 5) (https://prestations.vd.ch/pub/blv-publication/api/actes/42a955c1-f170-495d-badc-8d2881e476f5/pdf?type=PDF_RENDU)

## Source tax (impôt à la source)
- TOU on request: due by 31 March of the year after the fiscal year concerned; irrevocable once validly filed; both spouses sign if married or in a partnership (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/21049_TOU_Formulaire_WEB.pdf)
- The source-tax regulation RIS states the same rule (art. 8: written request by 31 March of the following year, cannot be withdrawn) and sets the income threshold for mandatory TOU in art. 7; version in force from 1 January 2026 (https://prestations.vd.ch/pub/blv-publication/api/actes/0fb0ca28-b0f4-40db-b178-de8719767ef5/pdf?type=PDF_RENDU). The version for 2021 to 2025 is https://prestations.vd.ch/pub/blv-publication/api/actes/2021870/pdf?type=PDF_RENDU
- The 2026 employer brochure gives the same 31 March deadline and irrevocability (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/21034-10_Employeurs_2026.pdf)
- Rectification: the official term is "nouveau calcul de l'impôt à la source"; the RIS limits it to a wrong gross salary, a wrong rate-determining income or a wrong tariff, requested by 31 March of the following year (https://prestations.vd.ch/pub/blv-publication/api/actes/0fb0ca28-b0f4-40db-b178-de8719767ef5/pdf?type=PDF_RENDU ; https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/21034-10_Employeurs_2026.pdf)
- Mandatory TOU: foreign nationals without permit C above the income threshold stated in the guide are taxed ordinarily; those who received no return can register by 31 March 2026 (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/21001_2025.pdf)
- Non-resident workers may request TOU by 31 March if at least 90 percent of worldwide gross income is taxable in Switzerland (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/21001_2025.pdf)
- Cross-border workers living in France: the French residence certificate (ARF) must be in hand before the activity starts, and before 1 January in later years; telework above 40 percent of working time ends frontalier status (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/Directives_concernant_l_imposition_des_travailleurs_frontaliers.pdf)
- Tables A, B, C and H (all codes, valid from 1 January) are printed inside the employer brochures, 79 pages for 2026; vd.ch has no separate PDF per tariff and links the ESTV txt files instead (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/21034-10_Employeurs_2026.pdf ; https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/21034-10_Employeurs_2025.pdf)
- Tariff G (2025: https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/2025_G.pdf ; 2026: https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/2026_G.pdf) and tariffs I, J, K for pension capital (2025: https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/2025_IJK.pdf ; 2026: https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/2026_IJK.pdf) are separate PDFs.
- The deductions built into tariffs A, B, C and H are published per year: 2025 (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/Publications_d%C3%A9ductions_bar%C3%A8ms_IS_2025.pdf), 2026 (https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/Publications_d%C3%A9ductions_bar%C3%A8ms_IS_2026.pdf)
- The annual tax law also sets flat source-tax rates for special categories (for example artists, sportspeople and lecturers under LI art. 139, mortgage creditors under art. 141) (https://prestations.vd.ch/pub/blv-publication/api/actes/2253990/pdf?type=PDF_RENDU)

## Open points for the rules (packages/rules-vd, 2026-10-14)
Found while reading the downloaded documents for the cantonal and communal income and wealth tax of 2025 and 2026. Each cites the document id and page ("p." is the PDF's page; the 2025 guide prints the same numbers); the numbers live in the documents.
- Conflict, family deduction (déduction pour famille, code 725) for a single person with children who has no part of 1.3: the law gives the deduction only to spouses living together and to the taxpayers of art. 43 al. 2 lit. c, the single parents with the part of 1.3 (vd-2025-law-li art. 42a al. 1, p. 24; vd-2026-law-li p. 25), and so do the guide's text (vd-2025-guide p. 49) and both tables of deductions (vd-2025-deductions and vd-2026-deductions p. 2). The guide's example 3 (vd-2025-guide p. 51) gives CHF 1,000 per child to a single person with two children with whom they don't keep a household alone (parts 1.0 + 0.5 + 0.5), and the family regulation shares or gives "la déduction supplémentaire pour enfant (art. 42a, al. 2 LI)" to unmarried parents living together (vd-na-law-rifam art. 10, 12, 14, p. 4 and 5), who never have the part of 1.3 (art. 9 al. 2, 11 al. 2, 13 al. 2). rules-vd does not compute the tax in that case. Ask the ACI which applies.
- Open point, the LRIPP reduction: the guide's section on computing the tax (vd-2025-guide p. 60) and both tables of deductions (p. 2) describe the cantonal tax as the base tax times the cantonal coefficient, without the LRIPP reduction; the LRIPP reduces the cantonal base income tax by 4 % for 2025 (vd-2025-law-lripp art. 4 al. 1, p. 2) and 5 % for 2026 (vd-2026-law-lripp art. 4 al. 2, p. 2), not the communal tax (art. 2 al. 2, p. 1), and the guide's worked examples apply the 4 % (vd-2025-guide p. 61). rules-vd applies the law and the examples.
- Interpretation, which column of the communal tables: the total (1+2). The communal tax on income and wealth is levied in percent of the cantonal base tax, and a commune may add special taxes earmarked for given expenses, in percent of the same base (vd-na-law-licom art. 6 al. 1, p. 4; al. 3, p. 5); the maxima of art. 8 count them (al. 1, p. 5). Communes with a special tax in 2025 and 2026: Ormont-Dessous, Ormont-Dessus, Vufflens-la-Ville, Aubonne, Château-d'Oex, La Tour-de-Peilz.
- Interpretation, fractions of communes: the tables give the four fractions a rate of 3 to 10 % (rows "(fraction)" after their commune), and LICom art. 1 al. 1 (p. 1) lets fractions of communes levy the taxes. No document says in so many words that the fraction's rate comes on top of the commune's, but a rate that low can only be an addition. rules-vd adds it for a taxpayer living in the fraction and asks which part of L'Abbaye or Le Chenit they live in.
- Open point, childcare with shared custody: each parent may deduct up to CHF 7,500 a child (vd-na-law-rifam art. 4 al. 2, p. 2), half the law's unadjusted CHF 15,000, while the full limit is adjusted to CHF 15,200 (vd-2025-guide p. 44; both tables, code 670). rules-vd uses 7,500 and says so in `unverifiedRules`.
- Not in the documents, housing deduction (code 660) with shared custody and no child support: the rent counted rises per child whose upkeep the taxpayer fully bears (vd-2025-law-li art. 39 al. 2, p. 24; vd-2025-guide p. 44), and the family regulation shares only the insurance and savings, modest taxpayer and family amounts (vd-na-law-rifam art. 6, p. 3). Whether a parent with half the part of 0.5 counts the child, and has the higher amount for a single parent, is not stated. rules-vd computes both and does not compute the tax when they differ.
- Not in the documents, the cap of the quotient familial (code 820, LI art. 43 al. 3) for half parts (0.25, RIFam art. 5 al. 2) and for more children than the guide's tables (vd-2025-guide p. 52 to 54: 1.8 with 1 to 5 children, 1.3 and 1.0 with 1 to 3). At or below the first threshold (CHF 212,900 in 2025) no cap applies in any reading; above it rules-vd does not compute the tax for those households.
- 2026: the 2026 guide (21001) is not published. Amounts only the guide prints are missing for 2026: the public transport flat amounts by distance and the bike and motorbike rates (rules-vd computes no such travel for 2026); the most rent counted for the housing deduction, the thresholds of the quotient cap and the household contents deduction, which LI art. 60 adjusts each year and never down (vd-2026-law-li art. 60 al. 2, p. 31), so rules-vd takes 2025's as lower bounds and computes only where the bound settles the result. The guide's methods (240 working days a year, p. 19 and annex 04; the rate to six decimals and the 5-centime rounding, p. 61; the rounding of the modest taxpayer deduction, p. 47) are applied to 2026 and named in the result. The 2026 table prints a higher rate per km for a car (vd-2026-deductions p. 1).
- Not in the documents, rounding: the guide's worked examples (vd-2025-guide p. 61) take the rate to six decimals of a percent and round the base tax, the LRIPP reduction, the cantonal and the communal income tax to 5 centimes. Nothing on the wealth tax (the detailed wealth tables print the base tax to 5 centimes), nor on how the 5 % medical threshold (code 710), the 20 % cap on donations (720) and the 20 % of the net income in the housing deduction (660) are rounded. rules-vd rounds the wealth taxes like the income taxes and the shares to the nearest franc, and says so in `unverifiedRules`. Whether the rate is rounded or cut at six decimals is not shown either (the example's digits are the same both ways); rules-vd rounds.
- Checked, modest taxpayer deduction (695): the guide's method (half the excess over the maximum, rounded to the hundred from 50 up, vd-2025-guide p. 47) gives the guide's tables 1a to 1c exactly and is LI art. 42 al. 3 (CHF 100 less per started CHF 200 above the limit, which al. 1 sets at the deduction plus 99). No conflict.
- Not in the documents, the maxima of LICom art. 8 (p. 5): at most 30 % of the income and 10 per mille of the wealth, and both taxes at most 60 % of the net income with a floor of 1 % on the income from wealth (annual tax laws art. 7, p. 2), the wealth tax then at least 3 per mille. The documents don't say on which amounts exactly (taxable or net income; which wealth for the 1 % floor) nor how the cut is shared and rounded. rules-vd checks with bounds (the tax before the LRIPP reduction, the lowest base) and does not compute the tax when a maximum could apply.
- Not in the documents: how a fraction of a km counts in the public transport table (rules-vd rounds to the nearest km). The guide prices bikes and mopeds (CHF 700 a year, p. 20) under "autres moyens de transport", which it otherwise admits only when public transport can't be used; rules-vd gives a cyclist the bike amount.
- Personal tax: 31 communes in 2025 and 30 in 2026 levy a fixed personal tax (impôt personnel fixe, the tables' column 8), at most CHF 10 per adult domiciled on 1 January, half for spouses, with the commune's own exemptions (vd-na-law-licom art. 21, p. 9). rules-vd does not compute it and says so.
- Not covered by rules-vd: property (annex 07, rental value), owners' housing deduction, self-employment, capital benefits and liquidation profits taxed separately (LI art. 48a, 49), the deduction for a dependent person (680), disability costs and their flat amounts (710), lottery winnings and stakes (495), weekly stays away from home, part-year liability, a married couple with a child in shared custody from another relationship, the federal tax for Vaud's form (`ifd: null`, as for Zurich and Bern).
