# Taxpresso NE: official sources (verified 2026-10-13)

## Filing channels, tax year 2025
- Prepare the return in the free Clic&Tax software, then transmit it through the Guichet unique (www.GuichetUnique.ch). Clic&Tax creates the XML transmission file. (https://www.ne.ch/sites/default/files/2025-09/SCCO_Guide_transmissionDI_GU_CT.pdf)
- Through the Guichet unique no handwritten signed page is needed; outside it, a signed paper document must reach the tax office. (https://www.ne.ch/sites/default/files/2025-09/SCCO_Guide_transmissionDI_GU_CT.pdf)
- Login to the Guichet unique is required; the login method is not described on the pages checked. (https://www.ne.ch/sites/default/files/2025-09/SCCO_Guide_transmissionDI_GU_CT.pdf)
- At transmission you choose whether supporting documents go electronically or by post. (https://www.ne.ch/sites/default/files/2025-09/SCCO_Guide_transmissionDI_GU_CT.pdf)
- Email transmission of the return is refused; only the official form supplied by the tax authority may be used on paper. (https://www.ne.ch/sites/default/files/2025-10/SCCO_I754_InstructionsGenerales_PP.pdf)
- A tax agent can be given access through the "Mandat de perception" box on page 1 of the form. (https://www.ne.ch/sites/default/files/2025-10/SCCO_I754_InstructionsGenerales_PP.pdf)
- Departure abroad or death: a separate intermediate guide applies, signed paper only, not via Clic&Tax or the Guichet unique. (https://www.ne.ch/sites/default/files/2025-10/SCCO_I759_InstructionsGeneralesIntermediaires_PP.pdf)
- Current Clic&Tax release is 25.3.31, dated 06.10.2026. (https://www.ne.ch/themes/impots/clic-tax)

## Third-party software
- The Clic&Tax page recommends an external product, Dr. Tax, for professional use. (https://www.ne.ch/themes/impots/clic-tax)
- Not stated whether third-party or fiduciary software may transmit returns; no approval procedure or approved list was found. (https://www.ne.ch/sites/default/files/2025-09/SCCO_Guide_transmissionDI_GU_CT.pdf)

## Deadlines and extensions
- The 2025 guide prints no calendar date; the return slip reads "jusqu'au 20xx". (https://www.ne.ch/sites/default/files/2025-10/SCCO_I754_InstructionsGenerales_PP.pdf)
- The personal-tax page refers to deadlines on linked pages but shows no date. (https://www.ne.ch/themes/impots/impot-des-personnes-physiques)
- Extension: request it through the SCCO website, the Guichet unique, or the form in the guide. (https://www.ne.ch/sites/default/files/2025-10/SCCO_I754_InstructionsGenerales_PP.pdf)
- Deadline requests are subject to fees; the amount is not stated. (https://www.ne.ch/themes/impots/documents-et-liens-utiles)
- Not a return deadline: the 8th instalment for 2026 is due 31 October 2026. (https://www.ne.ch/autorites/dffi/scco)

## Source tax (impôt à la source)
- TOU request deadline: 31 March of the following year. (https://www.ne.ch/themes/impots/impot-la-source-et-frontaliers/taxation-ordinaire-ulterieure-tou ; https://www.ne.ch/sites/default/files/2025-09/SCCO_DemandeTOU_IS.pdf)
- A TOU request cannot be withdrawn once filed. (https://www.ne.ch/themes/impots/impot-la-source-et-frontaliers/taxation-ordinaire-ulterieure-tou)
- Taxpayers resident in Switzerland may request TOU only once; for married or registered partners both sign. (https://www.ne.ch/sites/default/files/2025-09/SCCO_DemandeTOU_IS.pdf)
- TOU is compulsory above income or wealth thresholds, for other untaxed income, or for owning real estate, and then applies automatically the following year. (https://www.ne.ch/themes/impots/impot-la-source-et-frontaliers/taxation-ordinaire-ulterieure-tou)
- Send the TOU form to Office de l'impôt à la source, Rue du Docteur Coullery 5, 2300 La Chaux-de-Fonds, or to Office.ImpotSource@ne.ch; no fee for the request is listed. (https://www.ne.ch/sites/default/files/2025-09/SCCO_DemandeTOU_IS.pdf)
- Rectification deadline and procedure are not on the TOU or source-tax pages. (https://www.ne.ch/themes/impots/impot-la-source-et-frontaliers)

## Tariffs and coefficients (the figures live in the documents)
- Income and wealth base tables are PDFs; the income table is headed from 1 January 2024 and listed as valid for 2024 to 2026. (https://www.ne.ch/themes/impots/coefficients-et-baremes)
- The 2025 decree (Feuille officielle no 50, 12 December 2025) sets the cantonal coefficient for 2025 and 2026. (https://www.ne.ch/legislation-jurisprudence/pubfo/ld/Documents/2025/5__D__D_Coefficients_Impots.pdf)
- Communal coefficients follow each commune's own coefficient under decree RSN 631.00; no per-commune table is linked on ne.ch. (https://rsn.ne.ch/DATA/program/books/rsne/pdf/631.00.pdf ; https://www.ne.ch/themes/impots/coefficients-et-baremes)
- LCdir (RSN 631.0, act of 21 March 2000) and decree RSN 631.00 are consolidated as of 1 January 2026. (https://rsn.ne.ch/DATA/program/books/rsne/pdf/6310.pdf ; https://rsn.ne.ch/DATA/program/books/rsne/pdf/631.00.pdf)
